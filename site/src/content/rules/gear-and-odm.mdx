<SectionHead id="gear-and-gear-dice" eyebrow="Section 1">Gear and Gear Dice</SectionHead>

A <GlossaryTerm term="Gear item">gear item</GlossaryTerm> is one of these and nothing else: **ODM Gear, gas canisters, Blade Sets, horses, medical kits, tool kits, flintlock pistols, muskets, prosthetics.**

Any other object — a rope, a lantern, a wheelbarrow — adds no <GlossaryTerm term="Gear Dice" /> and counts as no item. It matters only where a rule names it, or through <GlossaryTerm term="Circumstances" />: a rope and a grapnel might make a climb Easy.

<FinePrint title="ratings, wear, and what brings gear back">

<Subsection title="Ratings and wear" id="ratings">

Everything but a canister and a prosthetic is **rated 1 to 3**, set on receipt and never changed. Its **<GlossaryTerm term="Current rating">current rating</GlossaryTerm>** runs from 0 up to that, and **the item's Gear Dice are its current rating**. One item per roll.

<MarginNote>Pushing, Covering, and the order a Pushed roll resolves in: <a href="/guide/rules-of-play/#pushing-your-luck">The Rules of Play</a>.</MarginNote>

<StepFlow id="fig-wear" fig="Fig. 1" title="From a Push to a Jam" steps={wearFlow()} />

An item at **0 counts as not had** for every Catalog entry and gives no dice. Some items count as not had while whole, too — an empty firearm, a Blade Set out of the handles.

<CoreTable table="gear-items" />

**No ruling ever wears an item.** A called roll's stakes can cost you a carried item, never a point of wear.

<span id="restoring" />**Four things restore a rating,** never above the rating: <GlossaryTerm term="Field Repair" /> (ODM Gear, tool kits, firearms), **restocking** a medical kit in a care window, **<GlossaryTerm term="Standard Issue" />** (which replaces rather than mends, and never a firearm), and the **Maintain Gear** Downtime Action, which restores everything you hold.

</Subsection>

</FinePrint>

<Subsection title="Field Repair" id="field-repair">

A **Wits** action with a tool kit — Wits alone without one. Name the item first: {repair.items.slice(0, -1).join(', ')}, or {repair.items[repair.items.length - 1]}. Blade Sets, horses, and medical kits cannot be repaired, and a tool kit never mends itself.

It needs **{repair.needs} success**, and each success raises the current rating by 1. ODM Gear raised above 0 stops being <GlossaryTerm term="Jam">Jammed</GlossaryTerm>.

**In a Titan Engagement** it spends your action, on your own item or one held by a comrade in your zone, and can be Helped, Pushed, and retried on a later turn. **Outside one** it is rolled only in a care window — one repair roll each, separate from the Treat Injury roll, with Help spending the helper's own repair.

</Subsection>

</FinePrint>

<SectionHead id="odm-gear-and-gas" eyebrow="Section 2">ODM Gear and Gas</SectionHead>

<GlossaryTerm term="ODM Gear" /> supplies Gear Dice to four entries: **the dodge, Break Attention, Fly, and <GlossaryTerm term="Leap Clear" />**. Strikes and Break Free take theirs from the <GlossaryTerm term="Blade Set" /> in your handles, never from the harness.

<FinePrint title="ODM moves, ODM use, and being airborne">

<BoxPair>
<RuleBox title="Strikes need a working harness" icon="ph:anchor-simple">

In a Titan Engagement a <GlossaryTerm term="Nape strike" />, and a <GlossaryTerm term="Body Part strike" /> from On Body or Blind Spot, need your harness to count as had: **not Jammed, and <GlossaryTerm term="Gas Rating" /> above 0**.

The strike still takes its dice from the Blade Set and is not ODM use, so it makes no <GlossaryTerm term="Gas Roll" />. Only a <GlossaryTerm term="Grounded">grounded</GlossaryTerm> Titan lifts the requirement.

</RuleBox>
<RuleBox title="Airborne" icon="ph:wind">

You are **<GlossaryTerm term="Airborne">airborne</GlossaryTerm>** while in the air on your own ODM Gear. An ODM move always puts you there, however the dice fall.

- **In an Urban zone you are not airborne at Blind Spot** — you are anchored to a roof, so a <GlossaryTerm term="Jam" /> leaves you standing.
- **You stop** on a move that is not an ODM move, on a fall, on being <GlossaryTerm term="Grabbed" />, and when the fight ends, where everyone still up lands with no fall.
- **Never airborne** while carried, Grabbed, or <GlossaryTerm term="Mounted">mounted</GlossaryTerm>. Running out of gas does not bring you down.

</RuleBox>
</BoxPair>

<Subsection title="ODM moves and ODM use" id="odm-moves">

An **<GlossaryTerm term="ODM move">ODM move</GlossaryTerm>** is a move on your own harness. In a Titan Engagement every one is a <GlossaryTerm term="Flight" />. It needs a harness that counts as had, dismounts you first, and — while <GlossaryTerm term="Overloaded" /> — spends your action as well.

**You use ODM Gear** in a round if you make an ODM move, roll with your own harness, or take an act a rule names as ODM use. **That is what the Gas Roll asks.** Holding a Position, a mounted charge, a Nape strike, and being carried are none of them. However many uses, **one Gas Roll a round**.

</Subsection>

</FinePrint>

<FinePrint title="running dry, the Jam, and changing a canister">

<Subsection title="Gas and the Gas Roll" id="the-gas-roll">

Your **Gas Rating** is the gas in the fitted canister, 0 to {gas.full}. Spare canisters each record their own, and an empty one is discarded as it comes off — so a carried spare always has gas.

<StepFlow id="fig-gas" fig="Fig. 2" title="The Gas Roll, and running dry" steps={gasRollFlow()} />

A <GlossaryTerm term="Squadmate" /> never Pushes, so its Gas Roll is always {gas.dice} dice and its harness never Jams from wear.

**A Gas Roll a <GlossaryTerm term="Fear Roll" /> calls for** is {gas.dice} dice, read like any other, and it is neither the round's roll nor ODM use.

**No canister changes hands before the gas it owes is rolled.** Declare a pass or a take and you make that round's Gas Roll first, with the dice your rolls so far have earned — and it counts for the whole round. If it empties the canister, the canister is discarded, nothing moves, and the pass spends nothing. A soldier who dies makes that roll too, before their canister becomes a left item.

**Change Canister** is not rolled. You need a spare in hand; in a fight it spends your action, or your move with Quick Refit. You can do it airborne.

</Subsection>

<MarginNote stamp="Read twice">Dry is not Jammed. Dry, the harness is whole and one canister fixes it. Jammed, it is broken, and only a repair or a new one will do.</MarginNote>

<SectionHead id="blades" eyebrow="Section 3">Blades</SectionHead>

A **Blade Set** is one pair of ODM blades — the only unit in which blades are carried, counted, passed, and lost. Your handles hold **at most one**, and only that set gives Gear Dice or counts as had for a strike, Break Free, Block, or Fight. **Empty handles, no Nape strike.** The set in your handles counts as no item; every other counts as 1.

<FinePrint title="ruin, the swap, and what a horse rates">

<Subsection title="Ruin and the swap" id="ruin">

**Any wear ruins a Blade Set.** When a Pushed roll using the set in your handles is final and 1 point of wear survives Blade Discipline, it is discarded at once and your handles are empty. A set's rating never falls — ruin takes wear's place, so a higher rating buys more dice and no more safety.

**The swap** fits a carried set into empty handles. In a Titan Engagement: once per turn, **spending your move** — so you refit where you stand and still strike this turn, and what ruin really costs you is that turn's <GlossaryTerm term="Flight" /> and its Momentum. Outside a fight: whenever you like. A Down soldier cannot swap.

A Fear Roll can drop the set in your handles. It lies in your zone, nobody picks it up during the fight, and it is shared out at the end.

</Subsection>

<SectionHead id="horses" eyebrow="Section 4">Horses</SectionHead>

Your horse is the one your sheet records, and **you can mount only your own**.

</FinePrint>

<FinePrint title="where a horse stands, mounting, and the charge">

<Subsection title="Where the horse is" id="where-the-horse-is">

In a Titan Engagement a horse is in a zone of its own: yours while you ride it, and wherever you left it once you dismount. **Horse Whistle is the only Talent that moves one.** A horse taken as a riderless-horse decoy leaves the fight entirely. When the fight ends no horse is in a zone, and every living soldier's horse is theirs again.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Mounting, riding, and the charge" id="mounting">

Neither mounting nor dismounting is rolled. **In a Titan Engagement** each happens as part of a move: the horse must be in your zone and not be lame, and you must be free (on the Ground or Anchored) and not airborne, Down, Grabbed, or carried. **A mounted soldier's ODM move dismounts them first,** leaving the horse behind.

**The charge.** A mounted move that enters or leaves a zone holding a Focus Titan may mark you loudest on one such Titan, your choice, once per move. It is never rolled, spends nothing beyond the move, and is not ODM use. A mounted move makes up to two zone steps and never enters an Urban zone, and it stops on entering a zone that holds a standing Focus Titan unless that zone is Open — so a rider can still ride through an Open zone holding a Titan and out again in one move.

**Forced dismounts:** your horse going lame or you going Down dismounts you **and you fall**. Being Grabbed dismounts you with no fall. The horse stays put each time.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="What a horse rates" id="what-a-horse-rates">

<MarginNote>A Funding 3 harness is rated 3 and a Funding 3 horse rated 2, so a mounted dodge rolls one die fewer. What it buys: a cheaper item to wear out, and no gas and no Jam.</MarginNote>

It gives Gear Dice to **the dodge, Ride, and Leap Clear** while you are mounted on it, and to **Break Attention** while mounted or while it is in your zone. Otherwise it counts as not had.

Mounted, you may take a dodge's or a Break Attention's dice from your harness instead. **A Pushed roll made with the horse wears the horse,** not the harness, and does not by itself make your Gas Roll three dice.

**A horse worn to 0 is <GlossaryTerm term="Lame">lame</GlossaryTerm>:** unmountable, no dice, and it dismounts and drops its rider. Only Standard Issue's exchange and Maintain Gear bring one back — Field Repair cannot mend a horse.

</Subsection>

</FinePrint>

<SectionHead id="falling" eyebrow="Section 5">Falling</SectionHead>

A fall inflicts **damage**, and is never a Titan attack.

<FinePrint title="when you fall, and being Overloaded">

<Subsection title="When you fall" id="when-you-fall">

Only for these reasons, and no other:

- **Your harness Jams while you are airborne,** once that roll is finished.
- **You go Down while airborne or mounted,** once the harm is resolved.
- **Your horse goes lame while you ride it.**
- **The comrade carrying you falls,** is Grabbed while airborne, or dies airborne or mounted. You fall from their band, at the same moment.
- **A rule or a ruling names one.** A called roll's stakes may name low or high, **never extreme**, and low if the GM names none.

Running dry, landing through a move, and being set down are never falls, and **a fall never causes a second fall**.

</Subsection>

</FinePrint>

<StepFlow id="fig-fall" fig="Fig. 3" title="A fall, band to landing" steps={fallFlow()} />

<CoreTable table="fall-bands" />

<CoreTable table="fall-damage" />

A fall changes no rating, no Gas Rating, and no item you carry. No <GlossaryTerm term="Reaction" /> answers it, and it is no Fear Roll trigger. Where it leaves you is in <a href="/guide/fighting-titans/#where-a-fall-lands">Fighting Titans</a>.

<SectionHead id="carrying" eyebrow="Section 6">Carrying</SectionHead>

You carry **{carryingLimit()} items** before you are <GlossaryTerm term="Overloaded" />. Attributes never rise, so the limit never changes.

<CoreTable table="carrying" />

<Subsection title="Overloaded, and shedding load" id="overloaded">

Over your limit, **every ODM move also spends your action**, and none can be made once that action is gone. Nothing else changes. A soldier who has lost one leg carries the same cost on every move on foot.

**Shedding load** costs nothing, at any point in your own turn, as often as you like: drop any carried item (it leaves play), set down a comrade, or stop being carried. A Down soldier cannot; an airborne carrier cannot set anyone down, and nobody leaves an airborne carrier.

</Subsection>

<FinePrint title="carrying a comrade, passing, taking, and the dead">

<Subsection title="Carrying a comrade" id="lift-comrade">

**Lift Comrade** is not rolled. In a fight it spends your action — or your move with Rescue Ride while mounted.

**The comrade** must be in your zone and be Down, or consent while the both-legs grade forbids them to move. Never one who is Grabbed, <GlossaryTerm term="Pinned" />, or already carried. **You** must not be Down, already carrying, or carried yourself.

**While you carry them** their zone and attachment are yours and change when yours do, never airborne on their own harness. Their turns still happen: their move changes nothing, and if not Down they keep their action for entries needing no move. They count as **5 items plus everything they carry** — Strong Back removes the 5 but not their items.

**Carrying ends** when you set them down or they leave, when either of you is Grabbed, dies, retires, or is Pinned, or when you go Down or fall. If you were airborne or mounted, they fall with you from the same band; otherwise they are simply set down where you stand.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Passing and taking" id="passing-and-taking">

<CoreTable table="passing-items" />

Each spends your action in a Titan Engagement and nothing outside one, and you must not be Down. **Passing** needs a living comrade in your zone, who may be Down or Grabbed. **Taking** works on a comrade who is **Down and not Grabbed**, or — in a fight only — on a dead comrade's left items where they lie. A fitted canister you take leaves them at Gas Rating 0 and reaches you as a spare.

**No rule passes an item to a comrade in another zone.** Disagreements go to the usual roll-off, and a Down comrade's player has no veto.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="When a soldier dies or retires" id="death-and-retirement">

A dead soldier's **ODM Gear, horse, and fitted prosthetics leave play.** Everything else is left for the Squad: canisters, every Blade Set, every kit, every firearm.

- **During the fight** a left item lies in the zone where they fell and can only be taken with Take Item, by a soldier in that zone.
- **Sharing out** happens when the procedure ends, before any promotion: the players give each remaining item to a living soldier who took part. Anything nobody takes leaves play.
- **Left behind.** The items of a soldier who dies left behind when a fight ends are never shared out.
- **Retirement** takes a soldier's gear out of play with them. Squad Supply belongs to the Squad, so no death takes any of it.

</Subsection>

</FinePrint>

<SectionHead id="standard-issue" eyebrow="Section 7">Standard Issue and Squad Supply</SectionHead>

<FinePrint title="Standard Issue, Squad Supply, and what a ruling may do">

<Subsection title="Standard Issue" id="what-you-are-issued">

What every soldier receives on joining the Squad, and whenever a rule issues it, scaled by <GlossaryTerm term="Funding" />.

<CoreTable table="standard-issue" />

<CoreTable table="standard-issue-specialty" />

**You receive it** when you make a new soldier or join the Squad; as a **full issue** with the whole Squad before each Expedition and at a depot Waypoint; and as an **interim issue** at the start of each session while no Expedition or Downtime is under way.

**The interim issue** differs three ways: it replaces ODM Gear only if you have none or yours is rated below the row, gives a Specialty kit only to a soldier holding none of that kind, and keeps the full issue's horse step.

**Standard Issue never restores the rating of an item you keep** — Field Repair, restocking, and Maintain Gear do that. Whenever the whole Squad is issued together, Squad Supply is restocked with it.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Squad Supply" id="squad-supply">

<GlossaryTerm term="Squad Supply" /> is the shared pool. The Squad holds all of it, no soldier carries it, and it counts as no item. Each kind is a whole number that never drops below 0.

  { icon: 'gear-medical-kit', label: 'Medical supplies', note: 'A Bonus Die in a care window, or a refilled kit.' },

<CoreTable table="squad-supply-kinds" />

<CoreTable table="squad-supply-stock" />

Each kind is set to its row when the Squad forms, and any kind below its row rises to it whenever the whole Squad is issued together. **None is ever lowered,** so units a Requisition added above the stock stay there.

**Medical supplies** have two uses, both outside a fight: **1 unit for 1 <GlossaryTerm term="Bonus Dice">Bonus Die</GlossaryTerm>** on a care-window Treat Injury roll, declared before rolling and never on an aftermath roll; and **1 unit to refill one medical kit** to its rating, once per kit per window, spending nobody's roll.

</Subsection>

<RuleBox title="What a ruling can do to gear and supply" icon="ph:gavel">

Before the dice, and only these ways:

- **A called roll's stakes** may name a fall (low or high, never extreme), one carried item lost, or 1 unit of Squad Supply spent.
- **A successful called search** may find at most **1 unit per success** of one kind the place could plausibly hold. Each place yields to one search only, everyone else Helping; a Waypoint scene yields to two at most.
- **An object that is not a gear item** may count toward the Circumstances.

Everything else is applied as written — wear, Gear Dice, the Gas Roll, the fall tables, Standard Issue, and every spend of Squad Supply. **No ruling ever hands out a gear item.**

</RuleBox>
