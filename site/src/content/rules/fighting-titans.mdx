<SectionHead id="how-a-titan-fight-works" eyebrow="Section 1">How a Titan Fight Works</SectionHead>

A <GlossaryTerm term="Titan Engagement" /> runs in rounds until the Titans are dead or you are out. A Titan tracked in full is a <GlossaryTerm term="Focus Titan" />, labelled A then B — **at most two at once**. Anything else closing in is a <GlossaryTerm term="Background Titan" />: a clock everyone can see.

<Subsection title="How one starts" id="how-one-starts">

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="The round" note="Five steps, in order" id="the-round">
<RoundDiagram fig="Fig. 1" />
</Subsection>

<MarginNote>Turns, actions, and Reactions: <a href="/guide/rules-of-play/#turns-actions-and-reactions">The Rules of Play</a>.</MarginNote>

On your card you take one move and one action, in either order, never splitting the move around the action. A <GlossaryTerm term="Reaction" /> spends a whole turn — which is how the soldier holding Attention runs out of turns, and why comrades Help and swap with them.

<RuleBox title="Something the rules do not cover" icon="ph:question">

An <GlossaryTerm term="Improvised act">improvised act</GlossaryTerm> does only what the entry the GM names for it does: a wagon rolled across its path is a <GlossaryTerm term="Break Attention" /> <GlossaryTerm term="Decoy">decoy</GlossaryTerm>, a lantern swung in its face is <GlossaryTerm term="Draw Attention" />, a chimney dropped on a leg is a <GlossaryTerm term="Body Part strike" />, a dive behind a stack is a move or nothing. One modelled on the <GlossaryTerm term="Nape strike" /> falls short whatever it rolls.

Fights are at Standard <GlossaryTerm term="Circumstances" /> unless something no rule prices — rain, smoke, ice — moves them, and the step never reaches the Titan.

</RuleBox>

<SectionHead id="positions-and-moves" eyebrow="Section 2">Positions and Moves</SectionHead>

<PositionsMap fig="Fig. 2" rating="wooded" />

<CoreTable table="positions" />

You hold a Position relative to **each** Focus Titan and each corpse. Holding one is the test for being in the fight.

<CoreTable table="position-steps" />

<MarginNote>A Flight uses your own ODM Gear, so it counts toward your <GlossaryTerm term="Gas Roll" /> and leaves you airborne: <a href="/guide/gear-and-odm/">Gear & ODM</a>.</MarginNote>

<Subsection title="What a move can do" id="what-a-move-can-do">

A move changes your Position relative to **one named Titan** by one step, or changes nothing. It may include one mount or dismount. **A move is the only way you change your own Position** — everything else comes from a rule that names it: a fall, a Grab, Fall Back, a Fear Roll result.

<FactTable compact columns={['State', 'Your move']} rows={[
  ['On your lines', 'Every ODM move is a <b>Flight</b>. More than one step only by spending Momentum on Carry.'],
  ['Mounted', 'A mounted step, or an ODM move that dismounts you first. Distant to In Reach may be a <b>charge</b>.'],
  ['<GlossaryTerm term="Down" />', 'In Reach to Distant, on foot, and nothing else.'],
  ['Grabbed, carried, or <GlossaryTerm term="Pinned" />', 'Nothing. A Pinned soldier cannot be moved by any rule either.'],
  ['On Body or Blind Spot', 'You may <b>let go</b> and fall from that height — how a <GlossaryTerm term="Jam">Jammed</GlossaryTerm> soldier gets off.'],
]} />

**A forced step** from a Fear Roll is a result, not your move: it spends nothing, happens even on a spent turn, and is **never a Flight** — unrolled, no Momentum, marking nobody. It never makes you leave or let go.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="The Flight" id="the-flight">

**Every ODM move in a fight is a Flight.** Roll Fly with your own ODM Gear. It needs no successes.

- **The step happens whatever the dice say.** What the roll decides is what the flight was *worth*.
- **Each success is 1 <GlossaryTerm term="Momentum" />,** up to the Anchors left.
- **No successes and you come in loud:** marked loudest from any Position, Distant included. Not a shout — the sound of a bad landing.
- It is your own roll, so Circumstances, Talents, Help, and the Push all reach it.
- Letting go, a step on foot, a mounted step, and being carried are **not** Flights.

<TryRollButton pool={{ base: 4, gear: 2, stress: 1 }} label="Try a Flight" />

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Momentum and Anchors" id="momentum">

**Momentum** is speed you are carrying, from 0 up to the Anchors left. **You lose all of it at the end of any round in which you made no ODM move.** You spend it at any point in your own turn, and nobody else — the GM included — ever grants, spends, or takes a point.

<CoreTable table="momentum" />

The Anchor Rating gives the fight a public pool of **Anchors**: the trees, roofs, and beams worth firing a line into. **However many are left is every soldier's Momentum cap.**

<CoreTable table="anchors" />

- **Never restored.** A wreck effect destroys 1 whether the behavior landed or whiffed, and a falling Focus Titan destroys 1 where it lands.
- **When they fall, every cap falls with them,** and anyone over the new cap loses the excess at once.
- **At 0, nobody holds Momentum** — but Flights still fly, and **no step row changes.** However flat the place has been beaten, the route to the Nape stays open.

Each rating carries one **Terrain Trait**. Wooded has none, and is the plain ground the others are read against.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Comparing two soldiers" id="comparing-positions">

Help, Covering, Treat Injury, Rally, and the rest ask whether two soldiers share a Position or stand one step apart. Compare relative to **the Titan the act is made against** — or, for a <GlossaryTerm term="Heave" /> or a pinning part, **that body**; failing both, the earliest-labelled living Titan, or the corpse that died last.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Where a fall lands" id="where-a-fall-lands">

A fall is read relative to the Focus Titan you hold the closest Position to; on a tie, the one that caused it. From **On Body or Blind Spot** you land at **In Reach**; from further out you keep your Position. Nothing else moves, and a fall is never your move.

</Subsection>

<InkVignette caption="A soldier swings out of a Titan's shadow and fires an anchor into a pine forty paces behind it." label="A soldier firing an anchor past a Titan's shoulder" art="guide-fighting-titans-positions" />

<Subsection title="Two Titans, and corpses" id="two-focus-titans">

A second Focus Titan arrives from outside, so everyone starts Distant from it. A move changes one Position only.

**The close rule.** Come to hold On Body or Blind Spot on one Titan and your On Body or Blind Spot on any other becomes In Reach. **Nobody is ever hooked into two Titans at once.**

When a Titan dies its Positions end, and the one you held becomes your Position relative to its **corpse** — On Body and Blind Spot reading In Reach. A corpse counts as grounded and its Positions count for every rule that reads one, but it holds no Attention, plays no cards, and is no fall's reference. Only a Heave and a strike on a pinning part are made against it.

A horse moves only with its rider, and no ladder or behavior ever picks one.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="The Behavior Table" id="the-behavior-table">

Titans take no turns. Each acts from a **D6 <GlossaryTerm term="Behavior Table" />** whose results escalate, plus one <GlossaryTerm term="Thrash" /> entry that is never rolled.

<IconKey title="The three tiers" items={[
  { icon: 'tier-terrorize', label: 'Terrorize', note: 'Stress, and what it shows you.' },
  { icon: 'tier-control', label: 'Control', note: 'Falls and wounds that cannot kill.' },
  { icon: 'tier-kill', label: 'Kill', note: 'The bite and the hand.' },
]} />

<CoreTable table="behavior-tiers" />

Each row gives its D6 result, name and tier, **targets**, the **holder's Position** it requires, the **Body Parts** it needs unbroken, its **Attack Dice**, its **effects** in order, and its **fallback** for when the Position does not fit.

<CoreTable table="titan-attack-dice" />

<MarginNote stamp="Read twice">A Titan never Pushes and never reacts. Everything it does is one open roll, and it stands.</MarginNote>

**Titan Dice** succeed on **5 or 6**, rolled in the open in a colour no pool uses. They never take Bonus Dice, Help, Gear Dice, Stress Dice, or Circumstances. Their successes are that card's <GlossaryTerm term="Severity" />, the same for every target.

<CoreTable table="behavior-effects" />

No entry names death. A Titan kills you only through a lethal <GlossaryTerm term="Critical Injury" />, the Grab's devour, or a fall.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Dodging a Titan" id="dodging-a-titan">

Titans cannot be blocked, so the only Reaction is a **dodge**: Agility, with Gear Dice from <GlossaryTerm term="ODM Gear" /> or your horse while mounted.

- **You decide after the roll,** knowing the Severity — whether to dodge, and whether to <GlossaryTerm term="Push" />.
- **One per Titan per round.** Its successes cancel against every later card that Titan plays at you, each separately, without being used up.
- **It spends your earliest wholly unspent turn.** A dodge against a Grab can have that turn given back when the Grab lands.
- A <GlossaryTerm term="Call It">Called</GlossaryTerm> behavior gives every target but the reader **2 Bonus Dice**.
- **Down or Grabbed, you cancel nothing.**

</Subsection>

<SectionHead id="attention" eyebrow="Section 5">Attention</SectionHead>

<FieldQuote cite="A Squad Leader, to a new recruit">While it is watching you, you are not cutting. That is the trade. Make it worth something.</FieldQuote>

A Titan's **Attention** is held by one soldier, by a decoy, or by nothing, and who holds it is public. **The soldier holding it cannot make a Nape strike against that Titan.**

<Subsection title="When it changes" id="when-attention-changes">

Only at these moments — between them the holder does not change, whatever anyone does:

- the Titan becomes a Focus Titan;
- one of its cards comes up (but not a card inside a decoy's hold, nor one while it holds a Grabbed soldier);
- a Break Attention places a decoy;
- its Grab lands;
- the holder dies, leaves, is freed, is Grabbed by another Titan, or stops holding a Position.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="The ladder" id="the-ladder">

**Candidates** are the living soldiers holding a Position relative to it — never one who has left, one held by another Titan, or a horse.

<CoreTable table="attention-ladder" />

1. **Find the highest rung anyone meets.** Those who meet it are tied.
2. **Add everyone marked loudest** — from any Position — unless that rung is *hooked into its body*.
3. **If it is hooked into its body,** and anyone tied is marked *hooked in by a strike*, only they stay. A Nape striker who fell short outdraws a soldier who is merely On Body.
4. **Narrow by each lower rung,** then by closest Position.
5. **Still tied:** the current holder keeps it. Otherwise the **lowest card this round** takes it. Otherwise **nothing** holds it until the next card.

Every ladder begins with *hooked into its body* and ends with *nearest*, so nothing — noise included — turns a Titan off a blade at its Nape. Everything below that line a shout can match.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Marks" id="marks">

<CoreTable table="attention-marks" />

A mark lasts until the end of that Titan's **next card that resolves a behavior**. A card that resolves nothing leaves every mark standing, so a mark is always read by the card that acts.

</Subsection>

<MarginNote>A shout from beyond its reach is not what turns it. From Distant, use a flare — or come in badly.</MarginNote>

<Subsection title="Draw Attention" id="draw-attention">

An unrolled action against one Titan, for a soldier not at Distant and not Grabbed. It spends your action — or your move, with Shout Them Off — and marks you **loudest**.

**What the shout buys.** While marked loudest you meet every rung any other candidate meets, except *hooked into its body*. So it takes the Titan off any comrade who is not hooked in, and never off a blade at the Nape. That is the trade: you can buy a comrade out of anything but the cut they are already making.

A Flight with no successes sets the same mark, and so may a mounted charge. Momentum spent on Quiet keeps it off you.

</Subsection>

<InkVignette caption="A flare arcs over a ruined street and the Titan's head swings after the light." label="A flare arcing over a Titan's head in a ruined street" art="guide-fighting-titans-attention" />

<Subsection title="Break Attention and decoys" id="break-attention">

A Perception action, with Gear Dice from ODM Gear or the horse — none for a Feint on foot. You need a Position relative to the Titan, no Grab on you, and no decoy already holding it.

<CoreTable table="break-attention-needs" />

**On a success** the decoy holds its Attention for as many of its next cards as its Tempo — one round of cards, however the deal falls. Each resolves nothing and spends the Next Behavior; only the last ends the hold and reads the ladder. Each success beyond the need makes 1 Opening, and a soldier it holds is freed.

**Decoys in a row** counts the decoys placed since its last card that resolved a behavior. It returns to 0 when one does. **A Titan fooled twice before it acts comes back fixated.**

<CoreTable table="decoys" />

</Subsection>

<SectionHead id="cutting-titans-down" eyebrow="Section 6">Cutting Titans Down</SectionHead>

Titans have no Health. You take one apart a Body Part at a time, and you kill it at the Nape.

<Subsection title="Body Parts" id="body-parts">

<IconKey title="Where you cut" items={[
  { icon: 'body-eyes', label: 'Eyes', note: 'Blind it and the flare and the cloak stop working.' },
  { icon: 'body-arm', label: 'Arms', note: 'What Grabs you.' },
  { icon: 'body-leg', label: 'Legs', note: 'Break one and it comes down.' },
  { icon: 'body-nape', label: 'Nape', note: 'Not a Body Part. The only cut that kills.' },
]} />

<CoreTable table="body-parts" />

A **Body Part strike** needs the Position its kind names, a part not yet Broken, no Grab on you, and working ODM Gear from On Body or Blind Spot unless the Titan is grounded.

Each success adds 1 to the part's **count**; at its <GlossaryTerm term="Toughness" /> the part moves one state — Intact, Wounded, Broken — and the count resets. Past Broken, each success makes 1 Opening. Any success marks you as having just hurt it.

**A Broken leg grounds it.** While grounded, Nape strikes gain **2 Bonus Dice**, no strike needs working ODM Gear, every part can be struck from In Reach inward (bar an arm holding someone), and close steps are on foot. Grounding brings its body down on whoever is under it; standing again frees everyone it pins.

</Subsection>

<MarginNote>Openings and every other Bonus Dice source, with the cap of 4: <a href="/guide/rules-of-play/#bonus-dice-and-help">The Rules of Play</a>.</MarginNote>

<Subsection title="The Nape strike and Openings" id="the-nape-strike">

A **Nape strike** is Strength with Gear Dice from a <GlossaryTerm term="Blade Set" />. It needs **Blind Spot**, you **not holding its Attention** and not Grabbed, **working ODM Gear** unless it is grounded, and **no retreat under way**.

**Reach the Nape Depth and it dies. Fall short and each success becomes 1 Opening.** Every Nape strike marks you *hooked in by a strike* whatever it rolls, so a striker who falls short draws the next behavior.

An **Opening** is a public token on the Titan recording who made it. A Nape striker spends them as Bonus Dice, 1 die each — but **never one you made yourself**. It waits on the Titan for a comrade, even if you die or leave. Regeneration and death erase them.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Regeneration" id="regeneration">

Each Focus Titan's clock fills 1 segment at every round's end, retreat or not. Full, it resolves in order: Openings erased, counts to 0, the most damaged Body Part one state toward Intact, **steam** on everyone On Body if a part moved, clock empty. Heal its last Broken leg and it stands, freeing everyone it pins.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Killing it" id="killing-it">

Only a Nape strike reaching the Nape Depth kills a Titan. Then, in order:

1. Everyone holding a Position takes the Nape-kill <GlossaryTerm term="Stress" /> relief, and a soldier it held is freed at On Body.
2. **Steam** scalds everyone at On Body or Blind Spot.
3. **The body comes down** — unless it was already grounded, in which case those it pins stay Pinned and corpse heat begins.
4. Openings, clock, marks, and cards are removed, and Positions end and are recorded.
5. The body stays as a corpse. **No Focus Titan alive and nobody Pinned ends the fight.**

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Steam and the falling body" id="steam">

<GlossaryTerm term="Steam" /> is Burn damage, so it reaches the Critical Injury tables only at 0 Health. Neither it nor the falling body rolls dice, and no Reaction answers either.

<CoreTable table="steam" />

A body comes down on death and on grounding, costing the fight **1 Anchor** where it lands. Everyone at On Body or Blind Spot is in its path; an airborne soldier swings clear untouched. The rest roll **<GlossaryTerm term="Leap Clear" />** — Agility, 1 success, no turn spent, Help allowed. Success clears you to In Reach; failure, or being Down or carried, pins you.

</Subsection>

<InkVignette caption="Steam boils off a Titan's shoulders as the body starts to go down." label="Steam rising from a falling Titan's shoulders" art="guide-fighting-titans-steam" />

<Subsection title="Pinned" id="pinned">

<CoreTable table="pinned" />

You take a crushing Critical Injury at a rolled location, never lethal. An arm or leg result is a **limb pin**, held by the Body Part of that kind and side; a torso or head result is a **body pin**, held by nothing. You hold In Reach relative to the body, cannot move or be moved, and a Grab on you lands only its crush.

**Corpse heat.** Under a corpse you take a burning Critical Injury at the start of each of your turns — lethal, and worsening. Under a *living* grounded Titan there is none.

Three ways out, and no others: a **Heave** (Strength, no gear, from In Reach or On Body — each success adds 1 to the body's public heave count, and reaching its Heave rating frees everyone; against a corpse it costs the heaver **1 Burn**), **cutting the pinning part free** (never possible for a body pin), or **the Titan standing**. The end of a fight frees nobody.

</Subsection>

<SectionHead id="read-and-call-it" eyebrow="Section 7">Read and Call It</SectionHead>

A **Read** is an Instinct action against one Focus Titan, no gear, needing **1 success**. Reading from **Distant** gains **2 Bonus Dice**.

<CoreTable table="read-facts" />

A standard Titan's numbers are already public, so its Next Behavior is the only thing a Read can learn about it. So is everything else you can simply see: Body Part states and counts, filled clock segments, Openings, who holds Attention, the previous behavior, Background clocks, the state of a Grab.

<Subsection title="Call It" id="call-it">

An option of a Read that revealed the Next Behavior. It costs **2 of that Read's successes** — 1 with Sharp Call — and gives every target **other than the reader 2 Bonus Dice** on a dodge against that card, fallback and Thrash included.

A behavior can be Called once. It gives the reader nothing, and nothing to a target who cancels with an earlier dodge. It ends when the card is done, when a decoy spends the behavior, or when the Titan dies.

</Subsection>

<SectionHead id="the-grab" eyebrow="Section 8">The Grab</SectionHead>

<FieldQuote cite="From a Cadet's diary">Two turns. I counted them both and I have never stopped counting them.</FieldQuote>

<GrabCountdown fig="Fig. 4" />

<Subsection title="Grabbed" id="grabbed">

Held in the hand of the Titan whose Grab landed. It **forbids Help, Covering, and Reactions** — you can still Push. You hold On Body, stop being airborne, are dismounted with no fall, and stop carrying anyone. Your move does nothing and your only action is Break Free. You hold that Titan's Attention and no other can choose you. Going Down does not end it.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="When a Grab lands" id="when-a-grab-lands">

It lands on **1 or more Net Successes** and takes no rider. The first unbroken arm takes you, and while it holds you that arm's **Toughness is 1**. You take a torso Critical Injury that cannot be lethal but still crosses off a Health box — enough to leave you Down in the hand. Then every <GlossaryTerm term="Witness" /> makes a Fear Roll.

**A Pinned target** cannot be held: only the crush lands.

</Subsection>

<MarginNote stamp="Read twice">Dodging a Grab is always the better play. A failed dodge never costs you more than not dodging at all.</MarginNote>

<Subsection title="The countdown" id="the-countdown">

It counts **your own turns** after the Grab lands — spent turns and turns taken while Down included. The Grab spends the action of the first counted turn.

- **End of the first counted turn:** you are **lifted**. It harms nothing, but Break Free gets harder and fewer comrades can reach the hand.
- **End of the second:** the Titan **devours** you. You die, and every witness makes a Fear Roll.

Neither is a behavior: no Attack Dice, and no Reaction answers either. While it holds a living soldier, each of its cards resolves nothing. If the Grabbed soldier dies by any rule, the Grab ends at once and its next card can act again.

A **witness**, for a Grab or a death, is every soldier who is alive, holds a Position, and is not the victim. Down witnesses and soldiers who have left make no roll, and one event gives each soldier at most one Fear Roll.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Getting out" id="getting-out">

<CoreTable table="grab-escapes" />

Break Free is Strength with Blade Set Gear Dice. A strike on the holding arm from On Body or Blind Spot needs working ODM Gear unless the Titan is grounded. **Grounding never widens the reach in that table** — only Clear the Hand does.

Once freed: **before the lift** you hold In Reach with no fall; **after the lift** you fall from On Body's height and land In Reach. Either way the arm returns to normal, and nothing holds the Titan's Attention until its next card.

</Subsection>

<SectionHead id="retreat-and-ending-the-fight" eyebrow="Section 9">Retreat and Ending the Fight</SectionHead>

<Subsection title="Clocks closing in" id="clocks-closing-in">

A **Background Titan** is a public clock with nobody holding a Position relative to it. It fills 1 segment at the end of each round, and 1 more for each flare spent on a Break Attention.

Full, with fewer than two Focus Titans alive, it **enters**: next label, clean, everyone Distant. Soldiers make the Fear Roll for a second Focus Titan or an Abnormal, and it takes cards from the next round.

Every fight also carries a **retreat clock**, filling 1 a round after the Background clocks. A flare never fills it. When it is full, the fight becomes a retreat.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="The retreat" id="the-retreat">

The fight becomes a **retreat** when the retreat clock fills, or when a Background clock fills while two Focus Titans are alive — in which case that Titan does not enter.

- **Nothing more arrives.** Only Regeneration clocks keep filling.
- **Moves are forced.** Everyone holding a Position, not Down, Grabbed, or carried, must spend each move on one of these:

<FactTable compact caption="Forced moves, your choice" columns={['#', 'The move']} roll rows={[
  ['1', 'A step toward Distant from the earliest-labelled Focus Titan you are not yet Distant from — then from the earliest corpse.'],
  ['2', '<b>Leaving,</b> once Distant from every Titan and every corpse.'],
  ['3', 'A step that brings you closer to a Down, Grabbed, or Pinned comrade.'],
  ['4', '<b>Staying put</b> at such a comrade\'s Position — but only if you already took an action for them earlier this turn.'],
]} />

- **The stay limit.** Options 3 and 4 last as many rounds as the retreat clock has segments, then become 1 or 2. While a Focus Titan is alive, a Down or Grabbed comrade has no such limit.
- **Order.** Move before action — except a stay-move, which follows the action taken for that comrade, and a move on a Lift Comrade turn, so you can lift and carry out in one.
- **No Nape strike during a retreat,** and **no return** while one is under way. Everything else is still taken.

</Subsection>

<InkVignette caption="Two riders carry a third between them as the trees thin out behind them." label="Two riders carrying a wounded comrade away from the treeline" art="guide-fighting-titans-retreat" />

<Subsection title="Leaving" id="leaving">

Distant from every Titan and corpse, and not Down, Grabbed, or carried, you may use your move to **leave**, taking anyone you carry and the horse you ride. You still take a turn each round: your move can only return, and your action can be any entry needing no Position. Leavers share one Position with each other, are never near anyone still fighting, and no Titan reaches them — but ODM use still makes a Gas Roll.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="How it ends" id="how-it-ends">

Two tests end a fight, and nothing else — the GM never does.

1. **No Focus Titan alive and nobody Pinned.** While anyone lies Pinned under a corpse, the rounds go on, clocks and all.
2. **No soldier standing:** nobody alive, holding a Position, and not Down. Everyone having left meets this. A body-pinned soldier is not standing; a limb-pinned one who is not Down is.

**With nobody standing,** a *returner* — a soldier who has left, alive, not Down or carried, no retreat under way — buys one more round. With none, it ends at once; if someone stands again first, the count is erased.

**Ending that way while a Titan lives kills everyone still holding a Position,** and everyone still Pinned dies under the body. Those deaths raise no Fear Roll, but they count for <GlossaryTerm term="Grief" />.

Afterwards every soldier's Momentum becomes 0, and the Anchors and Position records are cleared.

</Subsection>
