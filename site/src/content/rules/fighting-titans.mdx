<SectionHead id="how-a-titan-fight-works" eyebrow="Section 1">How a Titan Fight Works</SectionHead>

Rounds, until the Titans are dead or you are out. A Titan tracked in full is a <GlossaryTerm term="Focus Titan" />, labelled A then B — **two at most**. Anything else closing in is a <GlossaryTerm term="Background Titan" />: a clock everyone can see.

<RoundDiagram fig="Fig. 1" />
</Subsection>

<MarginNote>Turns, actions, and Reactions: <a href="/guide/rules-of-play/#turns-actions-and-reactions">The Rules of Play</a>.</MarginNote>

One move and one action on your card, in either order, never split. **A <GlossaryTerm term="Reaction" /> spends a whole turn** — which is how the soldier holding Attention runs out of time.

<RuleBox title="Something the rules do not cover" icon="ph:question">

An <GlossaryTerm term="Improvised act">improvised act</GlossaryTerm> does only what the entry the GM names for it does. A wagon across its path is a <GlossaryTerm term="Break Attention" /> <GlossaryTerm term="Decoy">decoy</GlossaryTerm>; a lantern in its face, <GlossaryTerm term="Draw Attention" />; a chimney on a leg, a <GlossaryTerm term="Body Part strike" />; a dive behind a stack, a move or nothing. One modelled on the <GlossaryTerm term="Nape strike" /> falls short whatever it rolls.

Fights run at Standard <GlossaryTerm term="Circumstances" />, and the step never reaches the Titan.

</RuleBox>

<SectionHead id="positions-and-moves" eyebrow="Section 2">Positions and Moves</SectionHead>

<PositionsMap fig="Fig. 2" rating="wooded" />

<CoreTable table="positions" />

**One Position per Focus Titan and per corpse.** Holding one is the test for being in the fight.

<RuleBox variant="stop" title="Blind Spot is not on the Titan" icon="ph:tree-evergreen">

**On Body means on it.** Hooked into the body or standing on it, holding on to the thing that is trying to kill you.

**Blind Spot does not.** You are anchored to terrain *behind* the Titan, a tree or a roof or a beam, hanging from it on your lines or standing on it, out of its sight with its Nape within reach. You are not touching the Titan. The name sounds like a place on the monster and it is a place in the world. Whether you are airborne follows the move that brought you there, not the Position: an ODM move leaves you in the air, as anywhere, and only the Urban roof holds you up.

Everything follows from that one fact. Steam at a Regeneration fill burns the soldiers On Body and leaves the Nape strikers alone. A Jam does not drop a soldier standing on an Urban roof. And on ground with nothing to anchor to, there is no Blind Spot at all.

</RuleBox>

<Subsection title="What a move can do" id="what-a-move-can-do">

One named Titan's Position, by one step or not at all, with one mount or dismount. **It is the only way you change your own Position.**

<FactTable compact columns={['State', 'Your move']} rows={[
  ['On your lines', 'Every ODM move is a <b>Flight</b>. More than one step only by spending Momentum on Carry.'],
  ['Mounted', 'A mounted step, or an ODM move that dismounts you first. Distant to In Reach may be a <b>charge</b>.'],
  ['<GlossaryTerm term="Down" />', 'In Reach to Distant, on foot, and nothing else.'],
  ['Grabbed, carried, or <GlossaryTerm term="Pinned" />', 'Nothing. A Pinned soldier cannot be moved by any rule either.'],
  ['On Body or Blind Spot', 'You may <b>let go</b> and fall from that height — how a <GlossaryTerm term="Jam">Jammed</GlossaryTerm> soldier gets off.'],
]} />

**A forced step** from a Fear Roll is a result, not a move: it spends nothing, happens on a spent turn, and is **never a Flight** — unrolled, no Momentum, marking nobody.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="The Flight" id="the-flight">

**Every ODM move in a fight is a Flight:** roll Fly on your own harness, needing no successes.

- **The step happens whatever the dice say.** The roll decides what the flight was *worth*.
- **Each success is 1 <GlossaryTerm term="Momentum" />,** up to the Anchors left.
- **No successes and you come in loud,** marked from any Position, Distant included — not a shout, the sound of a bad landing.
- Your own roll, so Circumstances, Talents, Help, and the Push all reach it. Letting go, a step on foot, a mounted step, and being carried are **not** Flights.

<TryRollButton pool={{ base: 4, gear: 2, stress: 1 }} label="Try a Flight" />

</Subsection>

</FinePrint>

<FinePrint title="Momentum, Anchors, and the Terrain Trait">

<Subsection title="Momentum and Anchors" id="momentum">

Speed you are carrying, 0 up to the Anchors left. **Lose all of it at the end of any round you made no ODM move in.** You spend it in your own turn, and nobody else — the GM included — ever grants, spends, or takes a point.

<CoreTable table="momentum" />

**Anchors** are the trees, roofs, and beams worth a line, in a public pool the rating sets. **However many are left is every soldier's Momentum cap.**

<CoreTable table="anchors" />

- **Never restored.** A wreck destroys 1, landed or whiffed; a falling body destroys 1 where it lands.
- **Caps fall with them,** and anyone over the new cap loses the excess at once.
- **At 0, nobody holds Momentum** — but Flights still fly and **no step row changes.** However flat the place has been beaten, the route to the Nape stays open.

Each rating carries one **Terrain Trait**; Wooded has none, and is the plain ground the rest are read against.

</Subsection>

</FinePrint>

<Subsection title="What changes a Position" id="what-changes-a-position">

**An action never changes a Position.** Only your own move does, plus the rules on this list. Nothing else in the game moves anyone between Distant, In Reach, On Body, and Blind Spot.

<CoreTable table="position-changes" />

Not on the list, and this is the half nobody can see: the **Nape strike**, the **Body Part strike**, Break Attention, Draw Attention, Read, Heave, Treat Injury, Rally, and every other entry in the Action Catalog that is an **action**. **None of them moves you,** whatever it rolls. Neither does a Titan turning its Attention onto you: Attention is not a Position, and the two never move each other.

Three Catalog entries are not actions, and they are on the list above rather than here: **Fly**, which is your own move; **Leap Clear**, which belongs to the body coming down; and **Mount or Dismount**, which is part of a move.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Comparing two soldiers" id="comparing-positions">

Help, Covering, Treat Injury, and Rally all ask whether two soldiers share a Position or stand one step apart. Compare relative to **the Titan the act is made against** — for a <GlossaryTerm term="Heave" /> or a pinning part, **that body**; failing both, the earliest-labelled living Titan.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Where a fall lands" id="where-a-fall-lands">

Read it relative to the Titan you hold the closest Position to; on a tie, the one that caused it. From **the body** you land **In Reach**; from further out your Position holds. Nothing else moves, and a fall is never your move.

</Subsection>

<InkVignette caption="A soldier swings out of a Titan's shadow and fires an anchor into a pine forty paces behind it." label="A soldier firing an anchor past a Titan's shoulder" art="guide-fighting-titans-positions" />

<Subsection title="Two Titans, and corpses" id="two-focus-titans">

A second Titan arrives from outside, so everyone starts Distant from it.

**The close rule.** Come to On Body or Blind Spot on one Titan and every **other** Titan you were On Body or at Blind Spot of becomes In Reach. A Titan you were Distant or In Reach of does not change at all. **Nobody is hooked into two Titans at once.**

When a Titan dies, the Position you held becomes your Position on its **corpse**, On Body and Blind Spot reading In Reach. A corpse counts as grounded and its Positions count for every rule that reads one — but it holds no Attention, plays no cards, and is no fall's reference. Only a Heave and a strike on a pinning part reach it.

A horse moves only with its rider, and no ladder or behavior picks one.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="The Behavior Table" id="the-behavior-table">

Titans take no turns. Each acts from a **<GlossaryTerm term="Behavior Table" />** whose results escalate, rolled on **a D6 plus its <GlossaryTerm term="Frenzy" />**, plus a <GlossaryTerm term="Thrash" /> entry that is never rolled.

<IconKey title="The three tiers" items={[
  { icon: 'tier-terrorize', label: 'Terrorize', note: 'Stress, and what it shows you.' },
  { icon: 'tier-control', label: 'Control', note: 'Falls and wounds that cannot kill.' },
  { icon: 'tier-kill', label: 'Kill', note: 'The bite and the hand.' },
]} />

<CoreTable table="behavior-tiers" />

Each row gives its D6 result, name and tier, **targets**, the **holder's Position** it requires, the **Body Parts** it needs unbroken, its **Attack Dice**, its **effects** in order, and its **fallback**.

<CoreTable table="titan-attack-dice" />

<MarginNote stamp="Read twice">A Titan never Pushes and never reacts. Everything it does is one open roll, and it stands.</MarginNote>

**Titan Dice** succeed on **5 or 6**, rolled in the open in a colour no pool uses, and take nothing — no Bonus Dice, Help, Gear Dice, Stress Dice, or Circumstances. Their successes are that card's <GlossaryTerm term="Severity" />, the same for every target.

<CoreTable table="behavior-effects" />

**No entry names death.** A Titan kills you only through a lethal <GlossaryTerm term="Critical Injury" />, the Grab's devour, or a fall.

</Subsection>

</FinePrint>

<SectionHead id="when-a-titan-acts" eyebrow="Section 4">When a Titan Acts</SectionHead>

Every Focus Titan holds a hidden **Next Behavior**, rolled out of sight and never changed. You learn it when a card resolves it, a telegraph shows it, or a Read buys it.

<Subsection title="Frenzy, and why time is not on your side" id="frenzy">

Every Focus Titan starts a fight at **Frenzy 0**, and gains **1 at the end of each round, to a cap of 3**. Its Frenzy is added to its behavior roll, and a result past the bottom of its table reads as the bottom.

Every Behavior Table runs the same way, from terrorising you to killing you. So the longer a Titan is alive, the further down its own table it rolls. **Round one it postures. By round three it is trying to kill you.** Frenzy is public, and it never falls.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="It turns on whoever it can reach" id="retargeting">

A Titan does not waste a card because the soldier it wants is standing somewhere it cannot be touched. **If the soldier holding its Attention is not in a Position the rolled behavior reaches, it turns on somebody who is** — the next candidate down the same Attention Ladder, and its Attention goes with it.

Only when **nobody** in the fight is standing where that behavior can reach does the card fall back, and a fallback that repeats the last behavior is a Thrash.

**So a safe Position is only ever safe for you.** Hanging at Blind Spot with its Attention on you used to be a place the monster could not answer. It answers now, by taking whoever is nearest to hand.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Dodging a Titan" id="dodging-a-titan">

Titans cannot be blocked, so the only Reaction is a **dodge**: Agility, on <GlossaryTerm term="ODM Gear" /> or your horse while mounted.

- **You decide after the roll,** knowing the Severity — and whether to <GlossaryTerm term="Push" />.
- **One per Titan per round,** cancelling against every later card it plays at you, never used up.
- **It spends your earliest wholly unspent turn,** and one against a Grab can have that turn given back.
- A <GlossaryTerm term="Call It">Called</GlossaryTerm> behavior gives every target but the reader **2 Bonus Dice**. **Down or Grabbed, you cancel nothing.**

</Subsection>

<SectionHead id="attention" eyebrow="Section 5">Attention</SectionHead>

Held by one soldier, a decoy, or nothing — and who holds it is public. **Whoever holds it cannot make a Nape strike against that Titan.**

<Subsection title="When it changes" id="when-attention-changes">

Only here. Between these, the holder does not change, whatever anyone does:

- the Titan becomes a Focus Titan, or one of its cards comes up — but not a card inside a decoy's hold, nor one while it holds a Grabbed soldier;
- a Break Attention places a decoy, or its Grab lands;
- the holder dies, leaves, is freed, is Grabbed by another Titan, or stops holding a Position.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="The ladder" id="the-ladder">

**Candidates** are living soldiers holding a Position relative to it — never a leaver, a soldier another Titan holds, or a horse.

<CoreTable table="attention-ladder" />

1. **Find the highest rung anyone meets.** Those who meet it are tied.
2. **Add everyone marked loudest,** from any Position — unless that rung is *hooked into its body*.
3. **At that rung,** anyone marked *hooked in by a strike* keeps it alone: a Nape striker who fell short outdraws a soldier merely On Body.
4. **Narrow by each lower rung,** then by closest Position.
5. **Still tied:** the current holder keeps it, else the **lowest card this round**, else **nothing** until the next card.

Every ladder begins with *hooked into its body* and ends with *nearest*, so **nothing turns a Titan off a blade at its Nape**. Everything below that line a shout can match.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Marks" id="marks">

<CoreTable table="attention-marks" />

A mark lasts until the end of that Titan's **next card that resolves a behavior** — so a card that resolves nothing leaves every mark standing, and a mark is always read by the card that acts.

</Subsection>

<MarginNote>A shout from beyond its reach is not what turns it. From Distant, use a flare — or come in badly.</MarginNote>

<FinePrint title="Draw Attention, Break Attention, and decoys">

<Subsection title="Draw Attention" id="draw-attention">

An unrolled action against one Titan, for a soldier not at Distant and not Grabbed. It spends your action — your move with Shout Them Off — and marks you **loudest**.

**What the shout buys.** Marked loudest, you meet every rung any other candidate meets except *hooked into its body*. So it pulls the Titan off any comrade who is not hooked in, and **never off a blade at the Nape**: you can buy a comrade out of anything but the cut they are already making.

A Flight with no successes sets the same mark, and so may a mounted charge. Momentum spent on Quiet keeps it off you.

</Subsection>

<InkVignette caption="A flare arcs over a ruined street and the Titan's head swings after the light." label="A flare arcing over a Titan's head in a ruined street" art="guide-fighting-titans-attention" />

<Subsection title="Break Attention and decoys" id="break-attention">

A Perception action on ODM Gear or the horse, and no dice for a Feint on foot. You need a Position relative to the Titan, no Grab on you, and no decoy already holding it.

<CoreTable table="break-attention-needs" />

**On a success** the decoy holds its Attention for as many of its next cards as its Tempo — one round of cards, however the deal falls. Each resolves nothing and spends the Next Behavior; only the last ends the hold and reads the ladder. Every success beyond the need makes 1 Opening, and a soldier it holds is freed.

**Decoys in a row** counts those placed since its last card that acted, returning to 0 when one does. **A Titan fooled twice before it acts comes back fixated.**

<CoreTable table="decoys" />

</Subsection>

</FinePrint>

<SectionHead id="cutting-titans-down" eyebrow="Section 6">Cutting Titans Down</SectionHead>

Titans have no Health. You take one apart a Body Part at a time, and kill it at the Nape.

<Subsection title="Body Parts" id="body-parts">

<IconKey title="Where you cut" items={[
  { icon: 'body-eyes', label: 'Eyes', note: 'Blind it and the flare and the cloak stop working.' },
  { icon: 'body-arm', label: 'Arms', note: 'What Grabs you.' },
  { icon: 'body-leg', label: 'Legs', note: 'Break one and it comes down.' },
  { icon: 'body-nape', label: 'Nape', note: 'Not a Body Part. The only cut that kills.' },
]} />

<CoreTable table="body-parts" />

Needs the Position its kind names, a part not yet Broken, no Grab on you, and working ODM Gear from the body unless the Titan is grounded.

Each success adds 1 to the part's **count**; at its <GlossaryTerm term="Toughness" /> it moves one state — Intact, Wounded, Broken — and the count resets. Past Broken, each success makes 1 Opening, and any success marks you as having just hurt it.

**A Broken leg grounds it.** Grounded, Nape strikes gain **2 Bonus Dice**, no strike needs working ODM Gear, every part can be struck from In Reach inward (bar an arm holding someone), and close steps are on foot. Grounding brings its body down on whoever is under it; standing again frees everyone it pins.

</Subsection>

<MarginNote>Openings and every other Bonus Dice source, with the cap of 4: <a href="/guide/rules-of-play/#bonus-dice-and-help">The Rules of Play</a>.</MarginNote>

<Subsection title="The Nape strike and Openings" id="the-nape-strike">

Strength on a <GlossaryTerm term="Blade Set" />, needing **Blind Spot**, **not holding its Attention**, not Grabbed, **working ODM Gear** unless it is grounded, and **no retreat**.

**Reach the Nape Depth and it dies. Fall short and each success becomes 1 Opening.** Every Nape strike marks you *hooked in by a strike* whatever it rolls, so a striker who falls short draws the Titan's **Attention**, on every ladder. They take its next behavior only if that behavior reaches Blind Spot; if it does not, the Titan turns on a comrade it can reach, and its Attention goes with them.

**The strike does not move you.** Hooking in is an Attention fact and nothing else: you are still at Blind Spot, anchored to the same tree, not on the Titan. And since the strike needs a soldier who does **not** hold its Attention, a striker who has pulled that Attention onto themselves is standing in exactly the right place and **cannot strike again until it moves** — which takes a comrade's shout, a Break Attention with a decoy, or the Titan itself retargeting to someone it can reach.

An **Opening** is a public token recording who made it, worth 1 Bonus Die to a Nape striker — **never to the soldier who made it**. It waits for a comrade even if you die or leave, and Regeneration and death erase them.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Regeneration" id="regeneration">

1 segment at every round's end, retreat or not. Full, in order: Openings erased, counts to 0, the most damaged part one state toward Intact, **steam** on everyone On Body if a part moved, clock empty. Heal its last Broken leg and it stands, freeing everyone it pins.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Killing it" id="killing-it">

Only a Nape strike reaching the Nape Depth kills a Titan. Then, in order:

1. Everyone holding a Position takes the Nape-kill <GlossaryTerm term="Stress" /> relief, and a soldier it held is freed at On Body.
2. **Steam** scalds everyone at On Body or Blind Spot.
3. **The body comes down** — unless it was already grounded, in which case those it pins stay Pinned and corpse heat begins.
4. Openings, clock, marks, and cards are removed, and Positions end and are recorded.
5. The body stays as a corpse. **No Focus Titan alive and nobody Pinned ends the fight.**

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Steam and the falling body" id="steam">

<GlossaryTerm term="Steam" /> is Burn damage, so it reaches the Critical Injury tables only at 0 Health. Neither it nor the falling body rolls dice, and no Reaction answers either.

<CoreTable table="steam" />

A body comes down on death and on grounding, costing **1 Anchor** where it lands. Everyone at On Body or Blind Spot is in its path, though an airborne soldier swings clear untouched. The rest roll **<GlossaryTerm term="Leap Clear" />** — Agility, 1 success, no turn spent, Help allowed. Success clears you to In Reach; failure, or being Down or carried, pins you.

</Subsection>

<InkVignette caption="Steam boils off a Titan's shoulders as the body starts to go down." label="Steam rising from a falling Titan's shoulders" art="guide-fighting-titans-steam" />

<Subsection title="Pinned" id="pinned">

<CoreTable table="pinned" />

A crushing Critical Injury at a rolled location, never lethal. An arm or leg result is a **limb pin**, held by the part of that kind and side; a torso or head result is a **body pin**, held by nothing. You hold In Reach, cannot move or be moved, and a Grab on you lands only its crush.

**Corpse heat.** Under a corpse, a burning Critical Injury at the start of each of your turns — lethal, and worsening. Under a *living* grounded Titan there is none.

Three ways out and no others: a **Heave** (Strength, no gear, from In Reach or On Body — each success adds 1 to the body's public heave count, and its Heave rating frees everyone; against a corpse it costs the heaver **1 Burn**), **cutting the pinning part free** (never a body pin), or **the Titan standing**. The end of a fight frees nobody.

</Subsection>

<SectionHead id="read-and-call-it" eyebrow="Section 7">Read and Call It</SectionHead>

An Instinct action against one Focus Titan, no gear, needing **1 success** — and **2 Bonus Dice** from Distant.

<CoreTable table="read-facts" />

A standard Titan's numbers are already public, so its Next Behavior is the only thing a Read can buy — as is everything you can simply see: part states and counts, filled segments, Openings, who holds Attention.

<Subsection title="Call It" id="call-it">

An option of a Read that revealed the Next Behavior, costing **2 of that Read's successes** — 1 with Sharp Call. Every target **but the reader** gains **2 Bonus Dice** on a dodge against that card, fallback and Thrash included.

Called once per behavior. It gives the reader nothing, and nothing to a target cancelling with an earlier dodge, and it ends when the card is done, a decoy spends it, or the Titan dies.

</Subsection>

<SectionHead id="the-grab" eyebrow="Section 8">The Grab</SectionHead>

<GrabCountdown fig="Fig. 5" />

<Subsection title="Grabbed" id="grabbed">

Held in the Titan's hand. It **forbids Help, Covering, and Reactions** — you can still Push. You hold On Body, stop being airborne, are dismounted with no fall, and stop carrying anyone. Your move does nothing, your only action is Break Free, and you hold that Titan's Attention while no other can choose you. **Going Down does not end it.**

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="When a Grab lands" id="when-a-grab-lands">

It lands on **1 or more Net Successes** and takes no rider. The first unbroken arm takes you, and while it holds you that arm's **Toughness is 1**. The crush is a torso Critical Injury that cannot be lethal but still crosses off a Health box — enough to leave you Down in the hand. Then every <GlossaryTerm term="Witness" /> makes a Fear Roll.

**A Pinned target** cannot be held: only the crush lands.

</Subsection>

<MarginNote stamp="Read twice">Dodging a Grab is always the better play. A failed dodge never costs you more than not dodging at all.</MarginNote>

<Subsection title="The countdown" id="the-countdown">

It counts **your own turns** after the Grab lands — spent ones and ones taken while Down included — and spends the action of the first.

- **End of the first counted turn:** you are **lifted**. It harms nothing, but Break Free gets harder and fewer comrades can reach the hand.
- **End of the second:** the Titan **devours** you. You die, and every witness makes a Fear Roll.

Neither is a behavior: no Attack Dice, no Reaction. While it holds a living soldier its cards resolve nothing — and if that soldier dies, the Grab ends at once and its next card can act again.

A **witness** is every living soldier holding a Position who is not the victim. Down witnesses and leavers make none, and one event gives each soldier at most one Fear Roll.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Getting out" id="getting-out">

<CoreTable table="grab-escapes" />

Break Free is Strength on a Blade Set, and a strike on the holding arm from the body needs working ODM Gear unless the Titan is grounded. **Grounding never widens the reach in that table** — only Clear the Hand does.

Freed, the arm returns to normal and nothing holds the Titan's Attention until its next card. **Before the lift** you hold In Reach with no fall; **after it** you fall from On Body's height.

</Subsection>

<SectionHead id="retreat-and-ending-the-fight" eyebrow="Section 9">Retreat and Ending the Fight</SectionHead>

<Subsection title="Clocks closing in" id="clocks-closing-in">

A public clock, with nobody holding a Position relative to it. It fills 1 a round, and 1 more per flare spent on a Break Attention.

Full, with fewer than two Focus Titans alive, it **enters**: next label, clean, everyone Distant, the Fear Roll for a second Titan or an Abnormal, cards from the next round.

Every fight also carries a **retreat clock**, filling 1 a round after the Background clocks and never by a flare. Full, the fight becomes a retreat.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="The retreat" id="the-retreat">

A **retreat** begins when the retreat clock fills, or when a Background clock fills with two Focus Titans already alive — and that Titan never enters.

**Nothing more arrives:** only Regeneration keeps filling. **Moves are forced** — everyone holding a Position, not Down, Grabbed, or carried, spends each move on one of these:

<FactTable compact caption="Forced moves, your choice" columns={['#', 'The move']} roll rows={[
  ['1', 'A step toward Distant from the earliest-labelled Focus Titan you are not yet Distant from — then from the earliest corpse.'],
  ['2', '<b>Leaving,</b> once Distant from every Titan and every corpse.'],
  ['3', 'A step that brings you closer to a Down, Grabbed, or Pinned comrade.'],
  ['4', '<b>Staying put</b> at such a comrade\'s Position — but only if you already took an action for them earlier this turn.'],
]} />

**The stay limit.** Options 3 and 4 last as many rounds as the retreat clock has segments, then become 1 or 2 — though a Down or Grabbed comrade has no limit while a Focus Titan is alive.

**Order.** Move before action, except a stay-move (after the action taken for that comrade) and a Lift Comrade turn's move (after the lift), so you can lift and carry out in one. **No Nape strike in a retreat,** and **no return** while one runs.

</Subsection>

<InkVignette caption="Two riders carry a third between them as the trees thin out behind them." label="Two riders carrying a wounded comrade away from the treeline" art="guide-fighting-titans-retreat" />

<Subsection title="Leaving" id="leaving">

Distant from every Titan and corpse, and not Down, Grabbed, or carried, you may use your move to **leave**, taking anyone you carry and the horse you ride. You still take a turn each round: your move can only return, and your action can be any entry needing no Position. Leavers share one Position with each other, are never near anyone still fighting, and no Titan reaches them — but ODM use still makes a Gas Roll.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="How it ends" id="how-it-ends">

Two tests, and nothing else — the GM never ends a fight.

1. **No Focus Titan alive and nobody Pinned.** While anyone lies Pinned under a corpse, the rounds go on, clocks and all.
2. **No soldier standing:** nobody alive, holding a Position, and not Down. Everyone having left meets this. A body-pinned soldier is not standing; a limb-pinned one who is not Down is.

**With nobody standing,** a *returner* — left, alive, not Down or carried, no retreat under way — buys one more round. With none, it ends at once; if someone stands again first, the count is erased.

**Ending that way while a Titan lives kills everyone still holding a Position,** and everyone still Pinned dies under the body. Those deaths raise no Fear Roll, but they count for <GlossaryTerm term="Grief" />.

Afterwards Momentum becomes 0, and the Anchors and Position records are cleared.

</Subsection>

<MarginNote>Stress relief, care windows, Grief, and everything else the end of a fight sets off: <a href="/guide/wounds-and-fear/">Wounds & Fear</a>.</MarginNote>

<SectionHead id="squad-tactics" eyebrow="Section 10">Squad Tactics</SectionHead>

It belongs to the Squad, not a soldier. You hold **two**, chosen together before your first fight, each usable **once per fight**. Using one is an option, not an action, and it is spent the moment it is declared.
