<SectionHead id="starting-a-skirmish" eyebrow="Section 1">Starting a Skirmish</SectionHead>

A <GlossaryTerm term="Skirmish" /> is soldiers fighting people: Military Police, bandits, a Garrison picket. People **roll** their attacks, **can be blocked**, **can be talked down**, and hurt you with damage — never with a <GlossaryTerm term="Critical Injury" /> outright. No Titan takes part.

<MarginNote>Every <GlossaryTerm term="Foe" /> kind, with the numbers behind its rolls, stays in the GM's Guide. What you learn about a group, you learn by fighting it or by a Size Up.</MarginNote>

A rule that names a Skirmish starts one — the Night table's prowlers row, a Mission Brief — and so does the GM framing a scene that sets the Squad against people. **One Foe group takes part.** The start names the kind, and may name the number, the Ambush, the dark, and which soldiers are in it; where it names no soldiers, everyone living in the <GlossaryTerm term="Squad" /> is.

<SectionHead id="engaged-and-apart" eyebrow="Section 2">Engaged and Apart</SectionHead>

You are **Engaged** with a Foe or **Apart** from it, held separately for each: Engaged with the one in front of you, Apart from the two by the wagon.

<FinePrint title="Engaged, Apart, and your move">

<RuleBox title="Engaged and Apart are not Positions" icon="ph:warning">

A Skirmish has **no <GlossaryTerm term="Position">Positions</GlossaryTerm>, no <GlossaryTerm term="Anchor Rating" />, no Anchors, no Momentum, no <GlossaryTerm term="ODM move" /> and so no <GlossaryTerm term="Flight" />, and no <GlossaryTerm term="Airborne">airborne</GlossaryTerm> soldier.** The one Fly roll here is a called roll, and it leaves you on the ground.

</RuleBox>

<Subsection title="A move" id="a-move">

Your move does one of these, and may include one mount or dismount.

<ul>
  {moves().map((m) => <li><b>{m.name}.</b> {m.text}</li>)}
</ul>

**Whose move changes nothing:** a soldier <GlossaryTerm term="Held">Held</GlossaryTerm>, a soldier holding a Foe, and a <GlossaryTerm term="Down" /> soldier. A Foe changes its own range only on its own turn.

</Subsection>

</FinePrint>

<SectionHead id="the-ambush" eyebrow="Section 3">The Ambush</SectionHead>

A start may hand the <GlossaryTerm term="Ambush" /> to the Foes. Otherwise the GM rules whether the Squad can approach unseen at all — and if it can, one soldier rolls **Sneak** against the group's **Watch**. No Help, no Cover, Push allowed, never retried. Fail and neither side has it.

<SectionHead id="what-you-can-do" eyebrow="Section 5">What You Can Do</SectionHead>

<CoreTable table="skirmish-actions" />

**Help** comes from up to 3 comrades who are not Down, each spending their action, Engaged or Apart alike.

<BoxPair>
<RuleBox title="Something the rules do not cover" icon="ph:question">

An <GlossaryTerm term="Improvised act">improvised act</GlossaryTerm> takes a **model entry** the GM names — one action this chapter allows, never Block, Dodge, or an option. It spends your action, rolls as that entry rolls, carries its restrictions, and gives only its effect at its size. A Foe's Guard still answers it.

**An object thrown** at a Foe is a Shoot at damage 1 and Crush, with no Gear Dice.

</RuleBox>
<RuleBox title="A called roll here" icon="ph:dice-three">

An act no allowed entry could model, changing nothing the Skirmish tracks — no Health, Guard, Grit, Parley value, Attack Dice, Watch, range, or card.

- It spends your action and **never stands in for a Reaction**.
- A fall it stakes is low or high, never extreme.
- It can be tried again on a later turn.
- **Fly,** if the GM names it, takes your <GlossaryTerm term="ODM Gear">ODM Gear's</GlossaryTerm> dice but leaves you on the ground, makes no <GlossaryTerm term="Gas Roll" />, and drops nobody if the gear <GlossaryTerm term="Jam">Jams</GlossaryTerm>.

</RuleBox>
</BoxPair>

</FinePrint>

<SectionHead id="attacks-and-reactions" eyebrow="Section 6">Attacks and Reactions</SectionHead>

An attack here has the same shape as a Titan's. **People differ in three ways only:** they can be blocked, they answer your attack with **Guard**, and their harm is damage that grows with each Net Success beyond the first — so half a Reaction is worth having.

<StepFlow id="fig-skirmish-attack" fig="Fig. 3" title="An attack and its answer" steps={attackSteps()} />

<FinePrint title="Reactions against a Foe, and damage">

<Subsection title="Reactions against a Foe" id="reactions-against-a-foe">

**Block** is Strength with a <GlossaryTerm term="Blade Set" /> if you have one, and answers a Fight attack and nothing else. **Dodge** is Agility with ODM Gear or the horse, and answers a Fight attack or a shot.

A <GlossaryTerm term="Reaction" /> spends a whole turn, and you make **one per Foe per round** — its successes cancelling against every later attack that Foe makes on you, never used up. The reacting side Pushes last. A Held soldier cannot Dodge, and no Reaction answers an attack the Ambush leaves unanswered.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Damage" id="damage">

A landed attack deals its weapon's **damage, +1 for each Net Success beyond the first**, with the weapon's <GlossaryTerm term="Injury Type" />. A Grapple deals none.

<BoxPair>
<RuleBox title="On a soldier" icon="ph:heart-break">

Ordinary <GlossaryTerm term="Damage" /> at a rolled <GlossaryTerm term="Injury Location" />. At 0 <GlossaryTerm term="Current Health">current Health</GlossaryTerm> you are Down and gain a Critical Injury of that type. **A person never inflicts one directly.**

**Cut and Pierce** reach you only here and through a called roll's stakes, and both carry riders: a **lethal Cut bleeds**, so its <GlossaryTerm term="Death Roll" /> comes at the end of each of your turns until treated; a **Pierce from the 7 row up holds the ball**, so <GlossaryTerm term="Treat Injury" /> on it takes a 1-die penalty and it never heals untreated.

</RuleBox>
<RuleBox title="On a Foe" icon="ph:user-minus">

Damage lowers its Health, never below 0. A Foe has **no Critical Injuries, no <GlossaryTerm term="Stress" />, and no Down state**.

At 0 it is **out of the Skirmish**: dead if the damage was {zero.killed}, out cold if it was {zero.outCold}. It takes no further part and is never a target again.

</RuleBox>
</BoxPair>

</Subsection>

</FinePrint>

<SectionHead id="parley-and-size-up" eyebrow="Section 9">Parley and Size Up</SectionHead>

<FinePrint title="Parley, Size Up, and breaking a group">

<Subsection title="Parley" id="parley">

Once per Skirmish each, on your action, you may **Parley** with the whole Foe group: a Persuade roll on Empathy, or on Strength as a threat. It is still Persuade, so a <GlossaryTerm term="Talent" /> naming Persuade adds its dice.

**What it needs:** the group's **Parley value**, **+1**, **−1 per Foe out of the Skirmish**, never below 1 — then the ask's own number. Up to 3 comrades may Help, each spending their action.

**Success** yields the group and ends the Skirmish. **Failure** does nothing — except that a **failed threat** raises the group's Grit and Parley value by 1 each for the rest of the fight. A threat that does not land makes every later ask harder.

<CoreTable table="parley-asks" />

With **no Skirmish under way**, each soldier may make one Parley with a group the scene sets them before. It needs the Parley value plus the ask, with no Help and no Cover, and is never retried by that soldier with that group.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Size Up" id="size-up">

Once per Foe group per Skirmish, for the whole Squad: an Instinct action needing 1 success, never Helped, Covered, or retried.

**On a success the GM says aloud** the group's **Grit** and how many more Foes must go before it breaks; what each Foe will do on the group's next card; and, for any Foe the GM already means to direct, what it will do instead and why.

Each Foe then does what was stated. **Only something that happens after the Size Up changes it** — the GM never holds a reason back and produces it on that card.

</Subsection>

</FinePrint>

<SectionHead id="ending-a-skirmish" eyebrow="Section 10">Ending a Skirmish</SectionHead>

<Subsection title="When a group breaks" id="when-a-group-breaks">

A group breaks when its Foes out of the Skirmish reach its **Grit**, at the latest. The GM may break it sooner with a reason said aloud, and never later — a Size Up is how you learn the number.

At once, every Foe still in it that is not Held becomes Apart, takes no further turn, and leaves at the end of the round. A group that surrenders yields as the Surrender ask gives, and the Skirmish ends.

</Subsection>

<SectionHead id="fear-drives-and-talents" eyebrow="Section 11">Fear, Drives, and Talents</SectionHead>

<Subsection title="Fear Rolls" id="fear-rolls">

- **A comrade dies.** Every other living soldier taking part rolls. A Down soldier makes none.
- **A first kill.** Bring a Foe to 0 with {zero.killed} and, if you have never killed a person, you roll as the kill resolves. Your sheet records it, and a <GlossaryTerm term="Drive" /> may shrug the result off.
- **Nobody is Grabbed in a Skirmish,** so that trigger never comes up.
- **The GM's own trigger** is open here, as a Skirmish is outside a Titan fight — rarely, and never for an outcome a called roll already staked.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Drives, Talents, and Squadmates" id="drives-talents-squadmates">

- **Drives.** Turn triggers count Skirmish turns. Position triggers need a Titan fight and are never met here.
- **Talents.** A Skirmish has a start and an end, so a once-per-Titan-Engagement Talent can also be used once per Skirmish. One whose effect reads a Position does nothing here unless its own row says what it does.
- **Three carry over cleanly.** Hand-to-Hand adds its dice to Fight and Block; Blade Discipline names Fight, so it applies; Pry Loose frees only a Grabbed comrade, never a Held one.
- **<GlossaryTerm term="Squadmate">Squadmates</GlossaryTerm>** take part on their own cards, and never Push or Cover.

</Subsection>

</FinePrint>
