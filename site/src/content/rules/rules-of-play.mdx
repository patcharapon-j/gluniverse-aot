<SectionHead id="pushing-your-luck" eyebrow="Rolling dice">Pushing Your Luck</SectionHead>

<FieldQuote cite="Survey Corps field manual">Every recruit learns to count their gas. Every veteran learns when to spend it anyway.</FieldQuote>

<MarginNote stamp="Read twice">You can Push a success too. One 6 will not cut through a <GlossaryTerm term="Nape Depth" /> of 4.</MarginNote>

<p class="lede">A roll that falls short does not have to be the end.</p>

You can **Push**: take on <GlossaryTerm term="Stress" /> for a second chance at successes. You can Push after a failed roll or after a successful one.

<MarginNote>Cover costs your comrade, not you. Say it out loud before anyone picks up a die.</MarginNote>

<BoxPair>
<RuleBox variant="stop" title="You cannot Push when" icon="ph:prohibit">

- a Stress Die showed a 1 on this roll;
- the roll says it cannot be Pushed, like a <GlossaryTerm term="Death Roll" />;
- you have already Pushed this roll. You get one Push unless a Talent gives you more.

</RuleBox>
<RuleBox variant="gas" title="Pushing burns gas" icon="ph:wind">

If you Push a roll made with <GlossaryTerm term="ODM Gear" />, your <GlossaryTerm term="Gas Roll" /> that round uses **three** dice instead of two.

</RuleBox>
</BoxPair>

<MarginNote>Gear Dice keep their face. After a Push, look for 1s on them.</MarginNote>

<FieldReport id="example-mira" title="Field report: Mira at the Nape">

Mira swings in at the Nape. Her pool is 5 base dice, 2 Gear Dice, and 1 Stress Die. She rolls one 6: not enough to kill. She Pushes: she gains 1 Stress, adds a second Stress Die, and rolls every base and Stress die that isn't a 6.

<PoolRows rows={[

Her Gear Dice stay put, and one of them shows a 1, so her gear will wear. The reroll turns up two more 6s. Three successes, and no Stress Die showing a 1.
