<SectionHead id="dice-and-successes" eyebrow="Section 1">Dice and Successes</SectionHead>

<FinePrint title="the four fixed rules, and the kinds of die">

<Subsection title="Four fixed rules" id="when-you-roll">

1. **Roll only when it matters.** No rule, no doubt, no cost — no dice.
2. **The GM rules before the dice, never after.**
3. **Results stand.** Only a <GlossaryTerm term="Push" /> changes a roll.
4. **Rolled in the open,** but for a Titan's Next Behavior and a <GlossaryTerm term="Passive roll">passive roll</GlossaryTerm>.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Dice" id="dice">

**Every 6 is a success.** <GlossaryTerm term="Titan Dice" /> alone succeed on 5 or 6, sit in no pool, and never take Bonus Dice, Help, or a Push.

<DiceKey />

Give <GlossaryTerm term="base dice" />, <GlossaryTerm term="Gear Dice" />, and <GlossaryTerm term="Stress Dice" /> their own colours, so a Push keeps them apart, and count Bonus Dice as base dice. **D6** is one die, **xD6** is x dice added up, **D66** two dice read as tens and ones.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Attribute rolls" id="attribute-rolls">

A roll is an **attribute roll** if its rule names an attribute: actions, <GlossaryTerm term="Reactions" />, <GlossaryTerm term="Death Roll">Death Rolls</GlossaryTerm>, <GlossaryTerm term="Called roll">called rolls</GlossaryTerm>. Only these use a pool, count successes, and Push — everything else takes exactly the dice its rule states.

</Subsection>

</FinePrint>

<CoreTable table="roll-exceptions" />

<Subsection title="Counting successes" id="counting-successes">

- **A roll needs 1 success** unless its rule names a number — a <GlossaryTerm term="Nape" /> strike needs the Titan's <GlossaryTerm term="Nape Depth" />. Neither the GM nor the Circumstances ever move it.
- **Each success is used once,** but for a Reaction's, which cancel against several attacks by the same enemy in a round.
- A failure does only what the rule or the stake says. Silence means nothing happens.

</Subsection>

<SectionHead id="circumstances" eyebrow="Section 2">Circumstances</SectionHead>

One step of a seven-step ladder, named by the GM. Name none and the roll is Standard.

<CoreTable table="circumstances" />

<FinePrint title="how Circumstances behave">

- **One step per roll,** named with the stakes and before Bonus Dice, so <GlossaryTerm term="Help" /> can answer a minus step. Rain, dark, and a snapped strap are one judgment, not three.
- **Plus steps are Bonus Dice** under the cap of 4. **Minus steps are penalties:** base dice only, after the cap, and never below **1 base die**.
- **Fixed once named.** No Talent, Push, or <GlossaryTerm term="Cover" /> moves it, and a Reaction takes the step in force when the attacker's card comes up.
- **Dice change, needs never do.** Nape Depth 4 at Hard still wants 4 successes, from one die fewer.
- **Reaches** every attribute roll a soldier or <GlossaryTerm term="Squadmate" /> makes. **Never** Titan Dice, a Foe's dice, or the Death Roll.

</FinePrint>

<MarginNote stamp="Read twice">Routine and Effortless mostly serve a soldier carrying penalties: injured, doing something simple, and keeping their dice.</MarginNote>

<InkVignette caption="Rain sheets off a rooftop as a soldier tests her footing before firing an anchor." label="A soldier bracing on a rain-slick rooftop" art="guide-rules-of-play-circumstances" />

<RuleBox title="In a Titan Engagement" icon="ph:cloud-rain">

Standard is the assumption: <GlossaryTerm term="Anchor Rating" />, <GlossaryTerm term="Position">Positions</GlossaryTerm>, Openings, and your own penalties already price the fight. Name a step only for what no rule prices — rain, smoke, full dark, ice, a gale.

It reaches every roll a soldier makes and **never the Titan**. A Titan mired to the knee makes the dodge against it Easy, and loses no Attack Dice of its own.

</RuleBox>

One cost, staked aloud before the dice — and one cost however many times you Pushed.

<MarginNote>The GM states your current Health with any stake naming damage or a fall.</MarginNote>

<BoxPair>
<RuleBox title="A called roll in a fight" icon="ph:crosshair">

Last resort. An act is first a Catalog entry, then an <a href="#improvised-acts">improvised act</a>, and a called roll only if it changes nothing the fight tracks — no Position, Attention, Opening, card, clock, or Foe value.

It spends your action and never stands in for a Reaction. Facing an attack, you dodge or block.

</RuleBox>
<RuleBox variant="stop" title="What a ruling never touches" icon="ph:prohibit">

Circumstances is the **one** thing a ruling names. Applied exactly as written:

- the Push, Covering, Help, Bonus Dice, the cap, penalties, and what a roll needs;
- the fixed rolls and their tables — Fear, Stress Response, Gas, Death, performance, fall, infirmary;
- Critical Injuries, Scars, Grief, <GlossaryTerm term="Down" />, healing, care windows;
- gear wear, <GlossaryTerm term="Standard Issue" />, Funding, the Ledger, one Requisition per <GlossaryTerm term="Downtime" />;
- everything Titan: <GlossaryTerm term="Behavior Table">Behavior Tables</GlossaryTerm>, Attention, Attack Dice, Openings, the Grab, <GlossaryTerm term="Regeneration" />, Frenzy and its end step, the retreat clock;
- the Leg tables, rations, and the Straggler;
- and every die already rolled.

</RuleBox>
</BoxPair>

<FinePrint title="passive rolls, and trying again">

<Subsection title="Passive rolls" id="passive-rolls">

{exceptionText('passive-roll')}

The GM builds your pool as it stands and keeps the step to themselves. You learn the result only through what you notice, and failing means only that you did not notice. Say you are looking, and it becomes an ordinary called roll.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Trying again" id="trying-again">

In a fight, try again on a later turn for that turn's action. Outside one, only when something has changed — a new approach, a better tool, another soldier, time spent — and the GM prices that change first. One attempt each per changed situation.

</Subsection>

</FinePrint>

<SectionHead id="pushing-your-luck" eyebrow="Section 4">Pushing Your Luck</SectionHead>

<MarginNote stamp="Read twice">Push a success too. One 6 will not reach a Nape Depth of 4.</MarginNote>

A Push trades <GlossaryTerm term="Stress" />, and sometimes gear, for a second chance — after a failure or a success alike.

<Subsection title="How a Push works" note="Five steps, in order" id="the-push">
<PushSteps fig="Fig. 3" />
</Subsection>

Bonus Dice re-roll like any base die. A Gear Die showing 1 stays locked, one showing 6 stays, and the rest roll again. However many Stress Dice come up 1, you suffer **one** <GlossaryTerm term="Stress Response" />.

<BoxPair>
<RuleBox variant="stop" title="You cannot Push when" icon="ph:prohibit">

- a Stress Die showed a 1, now or on an earlier Push;
- the rule forbids it — the Death Roll, the first two Trials of the <GlossaryTerm term="Graduation Exam" />;
- it is a passive roll, or no attribute roll at all;
- you have Pushed once already, and no Talent gives you more;
- a state forbids it, as Down does.

No ruling ever forbids or forces a Push.

</RuleBox>
<RuleBox variant="gas" title="Pushing burns gas" icon="ph:wind">

Push a roll using <GlossaryTerm term="ODM Gear" /> and your Gas Roll that round is **three** dice, not two — three however many such rolls you Pushed.

A Pushed dodge on ODM Gear counts. On your horse it does not. A Push after the round's Gas Roll changes nothing.

</RuleBox>
</BoxPair>

<InkVignette caption="A soldier hangs on taut ODM lines below a Titan's Nape, gas venting from her canister." label="A soldier on ODM lines beneath a Titan's Nape" art="guide-rules-of-play-push" />

<FinePrint title="finishing a roll, and Covering">

<Subsection title="Finishing a roll" note="Four steps, in order" id="finishing-a-roll">

1. **Count successes.**
2. **Resolve any Stress Response,** at your Stress after the Push. A changed count is the count for every later use.
3. **Apply the effect,** or the staked cost.
4. **Apply wear.** A Pushed roll whose Gear Dice show any 1 wears its item by **1 point**, one point however many show it. An unpushed roll never wears anything. ODM Gear at 0 <GlossaryTerm term="Jam">Jams</GlossaryTerm>.

</Subsection>

<Subsection title="Covering" id="covering">

To Cover is to take the Stress of a comrade's Push — declared after the Push, before any die is re-rolled. One Coverer, and the Pusher may refuse.

<FactTable compact columns={['', '']} rows={[
  ['Who', 'A player character, not the roller, not Down. In a fight: same Position or one step away. Outside one: anyone who could <a href="#help">Help</a> that roll.'],
  ['Cost', '1 Stress to the Coverer. Not an action.'],
  ['Effect', 'The Pusher gains no Stress and no new Stress Die. Nothing else changes — a 1 still gives <em>them</em> the Stress Response, their gear still wears.'],
]} />

</Subsection>

</FinePrint>

<SectionHead id="stress" eyebrow="Section 5">Stress</SectionHead>

Mounting fear and adrenaline: it makes you more capable and more likely to break. Every attribute roll takes Stress Dice equal to your Stress — a **6** succeeds like any die, a **1** sets off a Stress Response. It carries between fights, <GlossaryTerm term="Expeditions" />, and sessions.

<InkVignette caption="A soldier on a rooftop grips her handles in shaking hands as a Titan's shadow crosses the street below." label="A soldier steadying her hands on a rooftop" art="guide-rules-of-play-stress" />

<FinePrint title="minimum Stress, and what the dice can trigger">

<Subsection title="Minimum Stress" id="minimum-stress">

Your minimum Stress is your number of <GlossaryTerm term="Scars" />, and no loss goes below it — gain one under that line and your Stress rises to meet it at once. Each Scar also raises <GlossaryTerm term="Resolve" /> by 1. **Five Scars and you retire.**

</Subsection>

<MarginNote>Scars, Resolve, Rally, and Fear: <a href="/guide/wounds-and-fear/">Wounds & Fear</a>. Havens and Downtime: <a href="/guide/expeditions-and-downtime/">Expeditions & Downtime</a>.</MarginNote>

</FinePrint>

<Subsection title="Gaining and losing Stress" id="gaining-and-losing-stress">

Only a trigger on this table moves Stress — never the GM's discretion, but for the stakes of a called roll.

<CoreTable table="stress-changes" />

- **Both at once.** A Nape kill that also ends the fight sets off both losses in full.
- **Holding a Position** is the test for being in the fight. The end-of-fight loss goes to everyone who held one at any point, retreat included; the kill loss only to those holding one at the kill.
- A comrade's <GlossaryTerm term="Rally" /> clears your Stress Response without lowering your Stress.
- **One Stress Response per roll at most,** and **the dice never trigger a Fear Roll** — only the closed list of horrors in Wounds & Fear does.

</Subsection>

<SectionHead id="bonus-dice-and-help" eyebrow="Section 6">Bonus Dice and Help</SectionHead>

<CoreTable table="bonus-dice" />

- **This table only.** Circumstances is the single source a ruling names.
- **Declared before the roll,** never added later, not even before a Push.
- **Cap of 4.** Over it you choose, and what you leave out is not spent: Openings stay on the Titan, a helper keeps their action.
- **They are base dice** — 6 succeeds, a Push re-rolls them, a 1 does nothing. **Penalties come after the cap.**

<InkVignette caption="One soldier braces on a chimney and steadies a comrade's line as she swings in toward the Nape." label="A soldier steadying a comrade's ODM line" art="guide-rules-of-play-help" />

<Subsection title="Help" id="help">

A comrade adds **1 die**, up to **3 comrades** per roll — any other soldier in the <GlossaryTerm term="Squad" />, player or Squadmate. Helpers never roll, so they take no Stress and no Stress Response.

<FinePrint title="who can Help, where">

<FactTable caption="Who can Help, and what it costs" columns={['Where', 'Who qualifies', 'What it spends']} rows={[

In every case: not Down, declared before the roll, no state forbidding it, and a roll that allows Help at all.

</FinePrint>

</Subsection>

<SectionHead id="turns-actions-and-reactions" eyebrow="Section 7">Turns, Actions, and Reactions</SectionHead>

<Subsection title="Turns and actions" id="turns-and-actions">

One **turn** a round: one **move**, one **action**. A move changes your Position by a step; an action is Help, a Catalog entry, an <a href="#improvised-acts">improvised act</a>, or a called roll. <a href="/guide/fighting-titans/">Fighting Titans</a> sets the order of play.

<FinePrint title="turns spent in advance, and what a state forbids">

- **Once per round, never banked.** Decline them and they stay only for Help and Reactions — and a Reaction needs both. Whatever is held is lost at the round's end.
- **A turn spent in advance** — by a Reaction or a result — is your earliest turn with both parts free. It arrives already spent: no Help, no Reaction, nothing held.
- **Not actions:** Reactions, Pushing, Covering, declaring Bonus Dice, spending Openings.
- **A state forbids only what it names** among Push, Help, Cover, React. Down forbids all four; <GlossaryTerm term="Grabbed" /> forbids the last three.

</FinePrint>

</Subsection>

<FinePrint title="improvised acts, and Reactions in detail">

<Subsection title="Improvised acts" id="improvised-acts">

In a fight, an act no entry covers is **improvised**. Before the pool is built the GM names the one Action entry whose effect it imitates — its **model entry** — and that ruling stands.

It spends your action and rolls with the model's attribute, gear, Talents, and needs. On a success it gives **only** that entry's effect, at that size, with its restrictions. It never kills a Titan, never touches a Nape Depth, Toughness, Attack Dice, or a card, and never makes more Openings than the model could. Only a Nape strike taken as written kills a Titan.

An act no entry could model, changing nothing the fight tracks, is a <a href="#called-rolls">called roll</a> instead.

</Subsection>

<InkVignette caption="A Titan's hand sweeps through a courtyard as a soldier fires her anchors and pulls clear." label="A soldier dodging a Titan's sweeping hand" art="guide-rules-of-play-reaction" />

<Subsection title="Reactions" id="reactions">

A **Reaction** is a dodge or block made outside your turn, when a Titan or Foe acts against you. An ordinary attribute roll that **spends one whole turn** — this round's if both parts are free, otherwise your next free one.

- **One Reaction per enemy per round,** covering every attack that enemy makes against you.
- **None against an attack that scored 0,** so you keep it for a later card.
- A turn spent in advance still arrives, and still counts for any rule counting turns, such as a Grab.
- When the fight ends, every advance-spent turn not yet come up is cancelled.

</Subsection>

</FinePrint>

<Subsection title="Attack and Reaction" note="One procedure for every attack" id="attack-and-reaction">

An attack's successes are its <GlossaryTerm term="Severity" />; what the Reaction leaves is its <GlossaryTerm term="Net Successes" />.

<BoxPair>
<RuleBox title="Against a Titan" icon="ph:person-simple-run">

Titans cannot be blocked, so the only Reaction is a **dodge**: Agility, with Gear Dice from ODM Gear or from your horse while mounted.

One dodge covers every card that Titan plays that round. A landed card inflicting a Critical Injury adds **+1** to that roll per Net Success beyond the first; a Grab, a knock loose, and a Stress gain take nothing extra.

</RuleBox>
<RuleBox title="Against a person" icon="ph:sword">

Dodge, or **block** where the attack allows it — a Foe's Fight attack in a Skirmish, never a Titan.

A landed attack deals its weapon's damage, **+1** per Net Success beyond the first.

</RuleBox>
</BoxPair>
