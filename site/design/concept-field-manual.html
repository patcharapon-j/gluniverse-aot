Pushing Your Luck

Every recruit learns to count their gas. Every veteran learns when to spend it anyway.

Survey Corps field manual

A roll that falls short does not have to be the end.

You can Push: take on Stress for a second chance at successes. You can Push after a failed roll or after a successful one.

How a Push works

Five steps, in order
  1. 1

    Roll.

    Roll your pool and count every 6 as a success.

    StopA Stress Die shows a 1: you suffer a Stress Response and cannot Push.
  2. 2

    Decide.

    Keep what you rolled, or Push. A comrade nearby may Cover you before any die is rolled again.

    KeepThe roll stands. You are done.
  3. 3

    Take the Stress.

    Gain 1 Stress and add 1 new Stress Die to your pool.

    CoveredYour comrade gains the Stress instead, and no new Stress Die is added.
  4. 4

    Roll again.

    Pick up every base die and Stress Die not showing a 6, plus the new Stress Die, and roll them. Dice showing 6 stay.

    Gear DiceNever rolled again. They keep whatever they showed.
  5. 5

    Check your Stress Dice.

    If any Stress Die now shows a 1, you suffer a Stress Response.

    LimitA roll never causes more than one Stress Response.
Fig. 1The Push

You cannot Push when

  • a Stress Die showed a 1 on this roll;
  • the roll says it cannot be Pushed, like a Death Roll;
  • you have already Pushed this roll. You get one Push unless a Talent gives you more.

Pushing burns gas

If you Push a roll made with ODM Gear, your Gas Roll that round uses three dice instead of two.

The four kinds of dice

Each kind is its own colour at the table

Base dice

FromYour attribute, Talent, and bonus dice.

A 1 meansNothing.

Gear Dice

FromYour equipment.
Succeeds on6
On a PushNever rolled again.
A 1 meansIf you Pushed, that gear wears down by one. Worn-out ODM Gear jams.

Stress Dice

FromOne for each point of Stress you carry.
Succeeds on6
On a PushRolled again unless it shows a 6.
A 1 meansA Stress Response.

Titan Attack Dice

FromThe GM, for a Titan's attack.
Succeeds on5 or 6
On a PushNever Pushed.
CountsOnly 5s and 6s.

Example

Field report: Mira at the Nape

Mira swings in at the Nape. Her pool is 5 base dice, 2 Gear Dice, and 1 Stress Die. She rolls one 6: not enough to kill. She Pushes: she gains 1 Stress, adds a second Stress Die, and rolls every base and Stress die that isn't a 6.

First roll
1success
After the Push
3successes

Her Gear Dice stay put, and one of them shows a 1, so her gear will wear. The reroll turns up two more 6s. Three successes, and no Stress Die showing a 1.
