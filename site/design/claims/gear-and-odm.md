## Gear and Gear Dice

- The gear list is closed: ODM Gear, gas canisters, Blade Sets, horses, medical kits, tool kits, flintlock pistols, muskets, prosthetic arms and legs. No other object is a gear item. C 43; IT `items`, `rating_rules.other_objects`
- Any other object adds no Gear Dice and counts as no item; it has a mechanical effect only where a rule names it (the thrown cloak as a Break Attention decoy); otherwise it counts toward the Circumstances the GM names. C 43; IT `rating_rules.other_objects`
- On a called roll made with an attribute alone, a gear item that helps counts toward Circumstances and gives no Gear Dice. C 43; IT `rating_rules.other_objects`
- Every item except a gas canister and a prosthetic is rated; the rating runs 1 to 3 and is set when the item is received; it never changes. C 47-49; IT `rating_rules.rating`, item rows `rated`
- Current rating runs from 0 to the rating, and it is the item's Gear Dice. C 47-48; IT `rating_rules.rating`
- A roll takes Gear Dice from at most one item, chosen before the roll when more than one qualifies. C 48; IT `rating_rules.one_item_per_roll` (checked in code)
- Wear: when a Pushed roll is final, each Gear Die showing 1 wears the item that supplied it by 1 point; Gear Dice are never re-rolled, so these are the first roll's 1s; a roll that was not Pushed wears nothing. C 54; IT `rating_rules.wear`, `unpushed_rolls` (Fig. 1)
- A rule Talent that ignores wear (Well-Kept Rig, Blade Discipline, Sure Seat) removes points before they apply. C 55; IT `rating_rules.wear` (Fig. 1)
- Each point left lowers the current rating by 1, never below 0; a Blade Set is ruined by any wear instead. C 56; IT `rating_rules.wear`, `blade-set.wear` (Fig. 1)
- No ruling wears an item; a called roll's stakes may lose a carried item, never a point of wear. C 54; SS `gm`
- At 0 an item adds no Gear Dice and counts as not had for every Action Catalog entry; ODM Gear Jams, a horse is lame, a medical kit is spent, a tool kit or a firearm is worn out. C 60; IT `rating_rules.at_zero`, item rows `at_zero` (Fig. 1 and the gear items table)
- The other states that count as not had, one row per item: ODM Gear Jammed or at Gas Rating 0 (never the Graduation Exam's training harness); a Blade Set outside the handles; a horse for the dodge, Ride, and Leap Clear while unmounted, and for Break Attention while neither mounted nor at the Position it holds relative to its recorded Focus Titan; an empty firearm. C 62-67; IT item rows `counts_as_not_had` (rendered)
- A current rating rises only through a rule in the item's own list, never above the rating: Field Repair, restocking a medical kit, Standard Issue replacing the item, and the Maintain Gear Downtime Action. Standard Issue never restores a firearm; Field Repair and Maintain Gear do. C 71; IT item rows `restored_by` (rendered per item in the Compendium)
- Firearms: received only by Requisition and empty; Standard Issue never gives one; rated like any item and worn out at 0; loaded or empty on the sheet; only Reload loads one, spending 1 unit of shot; a Shoot roll empties it; an empty one counts as not had; outside a Skirmish and a Titan Engagement it may be loaded at any time and stays loaded until fired; a musket's Reload in a Skirmish also spends the move; no use in a Titan Engagement, where a firearm gives Gear Dice to no roll; a pistol counts as 1 item and a musket as 2; both can be passed. C 77-85; IT `flintlock-pistol`, `musket`; CR `items_counted`
- Damage, Injury Type, and which Foes a firearm can be fired at are Skirmish rules; the page links out. C 85
- Prosthetics: received only by Requisition, at Limited Scarcity, one per Requisition; Standard Issue never gives or takes one; not rated, no Gear Dice, never wears, never a weapon; counts as no item and is never passed or taken; fitted during a Downtime once the loss at that side has healed; the lost-limb grades belong to Wounds & Fear; a fitted prosthetic leaves play with its soldier. C 89-95; IT `prosthetic-arm`, `prosthetic-leg`; CR `items_counted`, `leaving_play.death`
- Kept items: an item received by Requisition; the sheet records which; Standard Issue never takes one away unless it is handed in; at a step that would replace it or make it leave play the soldier may hand it in and take the issue, and keeping it is the default; it stays kept when passed, taken, or shared out; in every other way it is an item of its kind, and a Blade Set above rating 1 is still ruined by any wear. C 99-102; IT `kept_item`
- Field Repair is a Wits action taking Gear Dice from a tool kit, Wits alone without one unless Make Do applies; Gearwright adds dice. C 479, 505; FR `attribute`, `gear`, `talents`
- Target named before rolling, one of ODM Gear, a tool kit, a flintlock pistol, or a musket; a Blade Set, a horse, and a medical kit cannot be repaired; the tool kit supplying the Gear Dice is never the target; a kit at 0 gives no Gear Dice and can be repaired with another kit or with Wits alone. C 483; FR `target` (the list is rendered from `target.items`)
- Needs 1 success; Circumstances as any attribute roll, Standard when the GM names no step, and no step changes what it needs. C 484-485; FR `needs`, `circumstances`
- On a success the current rating rises by 1 for each success, to at most the rating, and ODM Gear raised above 0 is no longer Jammed; on a failure nothing happens. C 486-487; FR `on_success`, `on_failure`
- In a Titan Engagement: the item is the soldier's own or a comrade's at their Position, two soldiers who have both left counting as one Position; it spends the action; Help, Push, and Cover as usual; a failure can be tried again on a later turn. C 491-493; FR `in_titan_engagement`, `target.whose`
- Outside one: only in a care window held when a Titan Engagement ends, when a day passes during an Expedition, or on the interim day, and only within that window's scope; never in a Downtime day's window or after harm outside a fight; Downtime restoration is the Maintain Gear Downtime Action. C 497; FR `outside_titan_engagement.when`
- One Field Repair roll per living soldier in scope who is not Down, separate from their Treat Injury roll; a soldier not rolling may Help only if they have not made or Helped one in the window, Help is declared before the roll and spends that soldier's repair, at most 3 Help one roll; anyone who qualifies to Help may Cover, spending nothing; the players choose the order with the usual roll-off; Squadmates take part; a failure waits for a later window. C 499-503; FR `outside_titan_engagement`

## ODM Gear and Gas

- ODM Gear supplies Gear Dice to the dodge, Break Attention, Fly, and Leap Clear; strikes and Break Free take theirs from the Blade Set. C 110; ODM `rolls_it_rates`; IT `odm-gear.gear_dice_for` (rendered in the gear items table; the two lists were checked against `CAT` `gear` and agree)
- In a Titan Engagement a Nape strike, and a Body Part strike from On Body or Blind Spot, need ODM Gear that counts as had: not Jammed and Gas Rating above 0. The strike still takes Blade Set dice and is not ODM use. Only a rule that names the requirement lifts it (the grounded Titan). It does not apply outside a Titan Engagement. C 114; ODM `strikes`
- An ODM move is a move made on the soldier's own ODM Gear; which Position changes need one is the Anchor Rating's, in Fighting Titans. It needs gear that counts as had; a mounted soldier's dismounts them first; while Overloaded it also spends the action and cannot be made once that action is spent; a carried soldier makes none. C 120-124; ODM `odm_moves`
- ODM use: an ODM move, a roll whose gear item is the soldier's own ODM Gear, or an act a rule names as ODM use. C 128-132; ODM `odm_use.definition`
- Not ODM use: holding a Position without moving, airborne included; a roll made with a Blade Set, a horse, or a kit, every Nape strike included; being carried. C 134; ODM `odm_use.not_odm_use` (checked in code for three rows)
- Outside a Titan Engagement there are no rounds; ODM use there makes a Gas Roll only where the calling rule says; horseback travel never spends gas; the Graduation Exam makes no Gas Rolls. C 136; ODM `odm_use.outside_titan_engagement`
- Airborne: in the air on the soldier's own ODM Gear. Set by an ODM move, or by a Fly roll whose calling rule says so, on a success, a failure, or both. Cleared by a move that is not an ODM move, a fall, becoming Grabbed, and the end of the Titan Engagement, where everyone lands with no fall; outside a fight, when the rule's procedure ends unless it names another point. Never airborne while carried, Grabbed, or mounted. Running dry does not clear it. C 140-145; ODM `airborne`
- Gas Rating is the gas in the fitted canister, 0 to 3; no fitted canister means 0; spares each record their own; a Standard Issue canister is full; a canister at 0 is discarded as it comes off, so a carried spare always has gas. C 167-169; ODM `gas`; IT `gas-canister`
- The Gas Roll: every soldier, player character or Squadmate, who used ODM Gear during the round; once at the end of the round however many times they used it; the Gas Rolls of a round cut short by the end of a Titan Engagement are made at once, before the end-of-fight steps. C 175-176; ODM `gas_roll.who`, `when` (Fig. 2)
- Two dice, three if any roll the soldier Pushed that round had their ODM Gear as its gear item, however many; a Pushed dodge made with the horse does not by itself make it three; Light Trigger turns three back into two. C 177; ODM `gas_roll.dice` (Fig. 2, all numbers read from the table)
- Count the dice showing 1; they are not added and a 6 does nothing; lower the Gas Rating by 1 for each 1, to a minimum of 0. C 178-179; ODM `gas_roll.reading`, `effect` (Fig. 2)
- The extra gas a Push costs is the third die of the one roll, never a second roll. C 181; ODM `gas_roll.extra_gas_is_a_die`
- Not an attribute roll: no attribute, Talent dice, Bonus Dice, Gear Dice, Stress Dice, or Circumstances; never Pushed, Helped, or Covered; never causes a Stress Response. A Squadmate never Pushes, so its Gas Roll is always two dice and its harness never Jams from wear. C 181; ODM `gas_roll.not_an_attribute_roll`, `circumstances`, `dice.squadmate`; C 686
- A Gas Roll a Fear Roll result calls for: two dice on the fitted canister, read and applied as above; nothing happens with no canister fitted or at Gas Rating 0; it is not the round's Gas Roll and not ODM use, so the round's own roll is still made with the dice it would have had. C 183
- A canister about to move: the owed Gas Roll is made first, when the passer declares the pass or when a taker declares the take from a Down soldier; it has the dice that soldier's rolls so far this round give; it is that soldier's Gas Roll for the round, whatever canister is fitted later. C 187; ODM `gas_roll.canister_removed` item 1
- If that roll empties the canister it is discarded, nothing moves, and the pass or take is not made and spends nothing, so an action may still be taken that turn; if gas remains the canister moves with its Gas Rating and the pass or take spends what it spends. C 188; ODM `canister_removed` item 2
- A soldier who dies after using ODM Gear this round makes that Gas Roll at once, before the fitted canister becomes a left item; a canister it empties is discarded and is never a left item. C 189; ODM `canister_removed` item 3
- Change Canister: needs a carried spare; spends the action in a Titan Engagement, with Quick Refit spending the move instead, and nothing outside one; the spare is fitted and its Gas Rating becomes the soldier's; the canister taken off becomes a spare unless it is at 0, when it is discarded; it can be done airborne; not rolled, so never Helped or Covered. C 195-198; ODM `change_canister`
- Running dry (Gas Rating 0): ODM Gear counts as not had and adds no Gear Dice; no ODM move and so no later Gas Roll; no Nape strike and no Body Part strike from On Body or Blind Spot; it never causes a fall, and an airborne soldier stays airborne at their Position; it ends when the Gas Rating rises above 0 through Change Canister, Standard Issue, or receiving a canister and changing to it; the Graduation Exam's training harness has no canister. C 204-209; ODM `running_dry`
- Jam (current rating 0): counts as not had and adds no Gear Dice; no ODM move while Jammed, so no Gas Roll for a later round, though the Gas Roll for the round it Jammed in is still made; the Gas Rating does not change; no Nape strike and no close Body Part strike; an airborne soldier falls once the roll that Jammed it is finished; it ends when the current rating rises above 0 through Field Repair or Standard Issue replacing the harness. C 151-159; ODM `jam` (Fig. 1)

## Blades

- A Blade Set is one pair of ODM blades, the only unit in which blades are carried, counted, passed, and lost, and each set keeps its own rating. C 227; BL `unit`
- The handles hold at most one Blade Set; only that set supplies Gear Dice or counts as had for the Nape strike, Body Part strike, Break Free, Block, and Fight; every other carried set counts as not had for them, so empty handles cannot make a Nape strike. C 231-232; BL `handles` (checked in code); IT `blade-set.gear_dice_for`
- When a Titan Engagement begins, a soldier with empty handles who carries a set fits one. C 233; BL `handles.start_of_titan_engagement`
- The set in the handles counts as no item; every other Blade Set counts as 1. C 234; BL `handles.items_counted`; CR `items_counted`
- Ruin: when a Pushed roll whose gear item is the set in the handles is final and at least 1 point of wear is left after Blade Discipline, the set is ruined, discarded at once, and the handles are empty; a Blade Set's current rating never falls; otherwise a set is lost only when dropped, passed, or named by a rule. C 238; BL `wear`
- A Fear Roll result can drop the set in the handles: the handles are empty, the set lies at the soldier's Position, nobody takes it during the Titan Engagement, and it is shared out with the left items when the fight ends. C 240
- The swap: with empty handles, fit one carried set; in a Titan Engagement once during each of the soldier's own turns, at any point in it, spending neither move nor action, and a turn spent in advance still happens; outside one at any time, as often as liked; a Down soldier cannot swap; a Squadmate swaps as a player character does. C 244-247; BL `swap`

## Horses

- A soldier's horse is the one their sheet records, from Standard Issue, and a soldier can mount only their own. C 269; HR `own_horse`
- In a Titan Engagement a horse holds a Position; mounted it holds the soldier's and moves with them; dismounted it stays where the soldier left it, recorded with the Focus Titan that Position is held relative to; a successful riderless-horse decoy takes the horse out of the fight, holding no Position and giving no Gear Dice until it ends. C 270; HR `horse_position`
- Horse Whistle is the one Talent that moves a horse, bringing it to the soldier's Position before a move that mounts it. `data/character/talents.yaml` (`horse-whistle`)
- When the Titan Engagement ends no horse holds a Position; each living soldier's horse is theirs, lame or not, the decoy included; whoever was mounted stays mounted and whoever was not stays dismounted; outside a fight no horse holds a Position. C 271-272; HR `titan_engagement_ends`, `horse_position`
- Mounted is riding one's own horse, and a mounted soldier is never airborne. Neither mounting nor dismounting is rolled. C 276-278; HR `mounted`
- In a Titan Engagement both are part of a move, one or the other, at the Position held before or after the move's change of Position. To mount: the horse holds the soldier's Position compared relative to the recorded Focus Titan, the horse is not lame, and the soldier is not airborne, Down, Grabbed, or carried. Dismounting leaves the horse at the Position the soldier holds. C 279-283; HR `mounted.mount`, `dismount`, `within_a_move`
- Outside a Titan Engagement there are no moves: the soldier mounts or dismounts when the rule for the procedure says so, and otherwise at any time, needing their own horse not lame and themselves not airborne, Down, or carried. C 280, 289; HR `mounted.outside_titan_engagement`, `mount.requirements.outside_titan_engagement`
- A mounted soldier's ODM move dismounts them before its change of Position. C 283; HR `within_a_move`
- Made airborne from the saddle, by an ODM move or a Fly roll whose rule says so, the soldier is dismounted first with no fall, and the horse stays at their Position. C 284; HR `becoming_airborne`
- A soldier who has lost both arms mounts and dismounts only with a comrade's help. C 281; HR `mount.requirements`
- Forced dismounts: the horse going lame and the soldier going Down each dismount with a fall from a horse; being Grabbed dismounts with no fall; the horse stays at the Position each time. C 285-288; HR `forced_dismount`
- The horse rates the dodge, Ride, and Leap Clear only while the soldier is mounted on it, and Break Attention while mounted or while the horse holds the soldier's Position compared relative to its recorded Focus Titan; otherwise it counts as not had. Horsemanship adds dice to that dodge, and Ride is the Leg roll on a Hard Ride. C 295-297; HR `gear_dice`; IT `horse.counts_as_not_had` (checked in code)
- A mounted soldier's dodge or Break Attention may take Gear Dice from ODM Gear instead, one item per roll; that roll is ODM use and does not make them airborne. C 298; HR `gear_dice.choice`
- A Pushed roll made with the horse wears the horse, not ODM Gear, and does not by itself make the Gas Roll three dice; Sure Seat can ignore 1 point of that wear. C 299; HR `pushed_rolls`
- Lame (current rating 0): cannot be mounted, adds no Gear Dice, counts as not had for every entry, and a mounted soldier is dismounted and falls from a horse; Standard Issue exchanges a lame horse unless the soldier declines, and Maintain Gear ends lameness; no other rule restores a horse, so Field Repair cannot mend one. C 305; HR `lame`; FR `target.cannot_repair`
- On an Expedition every soldier who is not Down and whose horse is not lame is mounted on each Leg and dismounted at each Night Camp. C 305; HR `lame.ends`

## Falling

- A fall inflicts damage and is never a Titan attack. C 311; FA `harm_kind`
- The closed trigger list: a Jam while airborne, once the roll that Jammed it is finished; becoming Down while airborne, once the harm and any Critical Injury it gave are resolved; becoming Down while mounted, a fall from a horse, once that harm is resolved; the horse going lame while mounted, a fall from a horse, once the roll that wore it is finished; the carrier falling for any reason, being Grabbed while airborne, or dying while airborne or mounted, at the same moment and from their band; a rule or a ruling naming one. C 317-322; FA `triggers` (Fig. 3)
- A ruling names a fall only as a called roll's stakes, low or high as the GM names from the fall's height and never extreme, in a Titan Engagement as well as outside one, low if none is named, and the Height steps never raise it; the GM states the soldier's current Health with those stakes. C 322, 330; FA `triggers.rule-named`, `height.steps` item 1, `after_the_fall.gm`
- A Grab's hold ends the airborne and mounted states before its crush, so a soldier that crush puts Down meets neither Down trigger. C 324; FA `triggers` notes
- Running dry, landing through a move, and being set down are never falls; a fall never causes a second fall for the same soldier. C 324; FA `not_a_fall`, `never_a_second_fall`
- Height, in order: a band a rule or a ruling named stands, and no later step changes it; a rule that names a fall and no band (a knock loose, letting go, a release from a lift) reads the steps below; find the fall's reference Titan, the Focus Titan whose Position was closest in the order On Body, Blind Spot, In Reach, Distant, on a tie the Titan whose card, Grab, or effect caused the fall and otherwise the living Focus Titan with the earliest label; a fall from a horse is low and no step changes it; otherwise Distant or In Reach is low and On Body or Blind Spot is high; a fall that is not from a horse is raised one band, to at most extreme, at Giant Forest or against a Large Titan, both together still raising it one. C 330-334; FA `height.steps` (Fig. 3)
- Outside a Titan Engagement there is no reference Titan and no Position, so the fall takes the band step 1 gives and is low if none was named. C 336; FA `height.outside_titan_engagement`
- The procedure: find the band; the soldier stops being airborne, mounted, and carried, and a comrade they carry stops being carried and falls at the same moment from the same band; roll D6, add the band's value, read the row; 0 damage does no harm; otherwise take the damage, and a Critical Injury it gives has a rolled Injury Location and Injury Type Crush. C 342-346; FA `procedure`, `injury_type` (Fig. 3, five steps checked in code)
- The bands add +0, +2, and +4. C 351-355; FA `height.bands` (rendered)
- The damage table: 2 or less no harm 0, 3 to 4 jarred 1, 5 to 6 hard landing 2, 7 to 8 heavy landing 3, 9 or more shattering landing 4. C 359-365; FA `damage_table.rows` (rendered)
- The fall roll is a table roll: no Circumstances, no Push, no Help. C 348; FA `damage_table.circumstances`
- After the fall: the Position it leaves the soldier at is Fighting Titans'; a fall changes no gear rating, Gas Rating, or item; it is not a behavior, so no Reaction answers it, and it is not a Fear Roll trigger. C 368; FA `after_the_fall`

## Carrying

- The carrying limit is Strength + 4 items, and it never changes. C 390; CR `limit` (checked in code)
- The closed count: worn ODM Gear with its fitted canister, handles, and scabbards 0; the Blade Set in the handles 0; each spare canister 1; each other Blade Set 1; each medical kit 1; each tool kit 1; each flintlock pistol 1; each musket 2; each prosthetic 0; a carried comrade 5 plus every item that comrade carries; a horse 0; Squad Supply 0. Nothing else counts. C 391; CR `items_counted` (rendered)
- Strong Back removes the carried comrade's own 5, not the items that comrade carries. C 391; CR `items_counted` (the comrade row, `talents`)
- Overloaded: every ODM move also spends the action, and none can be made once the action is spent; nothing else changes, not a roll, a Gas Roll, a Reaction, or a move that is not an ODM move; outside a Titan Engagement it does nothing unless the rule for the procedure says so. C 395-400; CR `overloaded`
- A soldier who has lost one leg makes every move on foot spend the action too, cannot make one once the action is spent, and has the same exception outside a fight. C 402
- Shedding load: drop any carried item counting as 1 or more, other than a comrade, and it leaves play; set down a carried comrade, who holds the soldier's Position; a carried soldier who is not Down stops being carried, holding the carrier's Position. In a Titan Engagement at any point in the soldier's own turn, as often as they like, spending neither move nor action; outside one at any time; a Down soldier cannot; an airborne carrier cannot set anyone down and nobody leaves an airborne carrier. C 408-412; CR `shedding`
- Lift Comrade in a Titan Engagement: a comrade at the soldier's Position who is Down, or who is not Down, consents, and is forbidden to move by the both-legs grade; not Grabbed, not Pinned, not already carried, and a Pinned soldier is never lifted; the soldier is not Down, carries no other comrade, and is not carried; it spends the action, or the move with Rescue Ride while mounted. C 418; CR `lifting_a_comrade`
- Outside one: the comrade takes part in the same procedure as the soldier, meaning the soldiers that procedure's rule names, or every soldier of the Squad on the same Expedition, or every soldier of the Squad when none is under way; it spends nothing. C 419; CR `lifting_a_comrade.requirements.outside_titan_engagement`
- While carried: the comrade holds the carrier's Position and changes Position with the carrier's move; never airborne on their own gear, and being carried is not ODM use; their turns still happen, their move changes nothing, and a carried comrade who is not Down keeps their action for entries that need no move; they count for the carrier as 5 plus their own items, which still count against their own limit and which only they can drop or pass, though while they are Down anyone at that Position can take one; one comrade each way; carrying goes on after the Titan Engagement ends. C 424-429; CR `carrying_a_comrade`
- Carrying ends on each of the eleven rows: set down or leaving; the carrier Down while neither airborne nor mounted, set down at their Position; the carrier Down while airborne or mounted, carrying lasting to step 2 of the carrier's own fall, where the comrade falls from the same band; the carrier Grabbed, set down at their Position and falling if the carrier was airborne; the carrier falling for any other reason, the comrade falling too; the carrier dying, set down at the Position held and falling if the carrier was airborne or mounted; the carrier retiring, with no fall; the comrade Grabbed, with no fall; the carrier Pinned, the comrade Pinned too with their own Crush Critical Injury at their own rolled location and their own pinning Body Part; the comrade dying. C 433-442; CR `carrying_a_comrade.ends`
- Outside a Titan Engagement nobody holds a Position, so a comrade set down there holds none. C 444; CR `set_down_outside_titan_engagement`
- Passing: the passer is not Down and the receiver is a living comrade at the passer's Position, who may be Down or Grabbed; two soldiers who have both left count as one Position; outside a fight the receiver takes part in the same procedure; it spends the action in a Titan Engagement and nothing outside one; one item moves and counts against the receiver's limit, and against their carrier's too; not rolled, so never Helped or Covered; no rule passes an item to another Position. C 452-457; CR `passing_items`
- What can be passed: a spare canister with its Gas Rating; the fitted canister if it still has gas after the Gas Roll the pass calls for, leaving the passer at Gas Rating 0 with none fitted and reaching the receiver as a spare; a Blade Set outside the handles; a medical kit; a tool kit; a flintlock pistol or musket, loaded or empty, each keeping its rating and current rating. What cannot: ODM Gear, the Blade Set in the handles, a horse, a carried comrade, and Squad Supply. C 454-455; CR `passing_items.passable`, `not_passable` (rendered)
- The passer makes any owed Gas Roll when they declare the pass of their fitted canister, and an emptied canister is discarded with nothing passed and nothing spent. C 458; CR `passing_items.gas_roll`
- Taking: from a Down comrade who is not Grabbed, one item that comrade could pass; or, in a Titan Engagement only, one of a dead comrade's left items where they lie. The taker is not Down and holds that Position, two soldiers who have both left counting as one; it spends the action in a Titan Engagement and nothing outside one. A fitted canister taken leaves the comrade at Gas Rating 0 with none fitted and reaches the taker as a spare, with the same owed Gas Roll rule. Not rolled, and no rule takes at another Position. C 464-469; CR `taking_items`
- Outside a Titan Engagement left items are never taken, they are shared out; players who disagree about a take use the roll-off, and the Down comrade's player has no veto. C 467; CR `taking_items.requirements.outside_titan_engagement`
- Death: the dead soldier's ODM Gear, horse, and fitted prosthetics leave play; everything else is left for the Squad, spare canisters, the fitted canister as a spare if it still has gas, every Blade Set including the one in the handles, every kit, and every firearm. C 649; CR `leaving_play.death`
- Left items lie at the Position of a soldier who died in a Titan Engagement while holding one and not Grabbed, recorded with the Focus Titan, until the fight ends; otherwise at no Position. C 650; CR `where_left`
- During the procedure a left item is taken only with Take Item, by a soldier at the Position where it lies. C 651; CR `during_the_procedure`
- Sharing out when that procedure ends, at the moment promotion is timed and before any promotion: the players give each item not taken to a living soldier who took part, which for a Titan Engagement means anyone who held a Position at any point during it, choosing together with the usual roll-off; every item nobody takes leaves play. C 652-654; CR `shared_out`
- The left items of a soldier who dies left behind when a Titan Engagement ends are never shared out and leave play with the ground; an item that soldier passed before the ending is the comrade's and unchanged. C 655; CR `shared_out.left_behind`
- Retirement takes a soldier's gear out of play with them; a promoted Squadmate keeps its own; a soldier who dies or retires while carried stops being carried, and a carrier who dies or retires sets their comrade down. C 656-657; CR `leaving_play.retirement`, `carried_comrade`, `carrier`, `promotion`
- Squad Supply belongs to the Squad, so no death and no Retirement takes any of it away. C 659; SS `held_by`

## Standard Issue and Squad Supply

- Standard Issue is the gear every soldier receives on joining the Squad and whenever a rule issues it, scaled by Funding, which runs 1 to 6 and is 3 until the Funding rules are written. C 511-515; SI `funding` (the table's "in use now" row is read from `until_funding_rules`)
- When it is received: a new player character at the finish step of the Lifepath; a starting Squadmate, and anyone who joins later; the whole Squad as a full issue before each Expedition and on reaching a depot Waypoint; the whole Squad as the interim issue at the start of each session while no Expedition and no Downtime is under way, after the interim day and its care window, including that window's Field Repair rolls. C 519-522; SI `when_received`, `interim_issue.when`
- A session that opens partway through a procedure gives the interim issue once that procedure has ended, after its last step, and so after sharing out a dead soldier's gear, Retirement, and promotion. While an Expedition or Downtime is under way it never happens. C 522; SI `interim_issue.when`
- Promotion gives no Standard Issue: a promoted Squadmate keeps its gear and from then on receives every issue the Squad receives. C 523; SI `not_received`
- The interim issue's three differences: ODM Gear replaced only when the soldier has none or its rating is below the row's, so a Jammed or worn harness rated at least the row's is kept; the Specialty item given only to a soldier holding none of that kind, so a kit at 0 is kept; the horse step is the full issue's, and the decline covers it. C 525; SI `interim_issue.differences`
- By Funding: ODM Gear rating, one full fitted canister, spare canisters, Blade Sets counting the one in the handles each rated 1, and a horse rating, per row 1 to 6. C 537-544; SI `by_funding`, `every_row` (rendered; the "one, full" fitted canister is checked in code)
- By Specialty: a Medic receives a medical kit rated 1 and an Engineer a tool kit rated 1; no other Specialty receives an item; a Squadmate uses its own Specialty. C 548-553; SI `by_specialty` (rendered)
- The six receiving steps, in order, as the table gives them: ODM Gear; canisters; Blade Sets; horse; Specialty item; declining. C 560-565; SI `receiving.steps` (rendered as the numbered list)
- Standard Issue never restores the current rating of an item the soldier keeps; Field Repair, restocking, and Maintain Gear do. C 567; SI `receiving.kept_items`
- At a step that would replace a kept item the soldier may hand it in and take the issue, and keeping it is the default. C 567; SI `receiving.kept_item_exchange`
- When every soldier in the Squad receives Standard Issue together, Squad Supply is restocked too. C 567; SI `receiving.squad_supply`
- Squad Supply is held by the Squad as a whole, carried by no soldier, and counts as no item. C 605; SS `held_by`
- Four kinds, each a whole number of units never below 0: rations, flares, medical supplies, and shot, with what spends each. A use needs a unit of its kind and cannot be made with none left, except where the Expedition rules say what a Leg or a Night Camp with no rations costs. C 606-611; SS `kinds`, `units` (rendered)
- Stock by Funding: rations 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12; flares 1, 2, 2, 3, 3, 4; medical 1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 3; shot 0, 0, 6, 6, 8, 10. C 622-629; SS `stock.by_funding` (rendered)
- Each kind is set to its row when the Squad forms, and rises to its row whenever every soldier receives Standard Issue together, the interim issue included; none is ever lowered, so Requisition units above the stock stay. C 618-619; SS `stock.when_set`
- Medical supplies on a care-window roll: declared before a Treat Injury roll in a care window, 1 unit for 1 Bonus Die, at most 1 unit a roll, never in a Titan Engagement and never on an aftermath roll. C 636; SS `medical_uses.care-bonus`
- Restocking a medical kit: at any point in a care window, including before the first roll and between rolls, any living soldier in scope who is not Down spends 1 unit to raise one medical kit held by a soldier in scope to its rating; each kit once per window; not a roll and it spends nobody's roll. C 637; SS `medical_uses.restock-kit`
- A ruling's reach over gear and supply: a called roll's stakes may name a fall (low or high as named, never extreme, low if none), one carried item lost, or 1 unit of one kind of Squad Supply spent. C 37, 322; SS `gm`, `spending`
- A successful called search of a place the GM named with the stakes may find at most 1 unit per success, of one kind that place could plausibly hold; a place yields to one called search only, whatever it rolls, and the others present search it by Helping that roll; a place is one building, wagon, cache, or the like searched in one go; a Waypoint scene yields to at most two called searches whatever the number of places; no called search is made in a Titan Engagement or a Skirmish, so a place at hand is searched once the fight has ended. C 611; SS `spending`
- An object that is not a gear item may count toward the Circumstances the GM names, and Field Repair takes Circumstances like any attribute roll. C 37, 43, 485; IT `rating_rules.other_objects`; FR `circumstances`
- Everything else is applied as written: wear from a Pushed Gear Die showing 1 only; Gear Dice only from a rated gear item; what an object counts as for carrying; the Gas Roll and the fall roll, neither taking Circumstances; the fall bands and tables; Standard Issue and when it is received; every other spend and restock of Squad Supply. No ruling gives a gear item: one comes only by Standard Issue, a Requisition, or a rule that moves an item between soldiers. C 37; SI `receiving.gm`; FA `after_the_fall.gm`; SS `gm`

## The Compendium's Gear section
