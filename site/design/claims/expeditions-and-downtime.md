## Expeditions

- An Expedition is a Survey Corps operation beyond the Walls, ridden as a chain of Legs between Waypoints; the Squad always reaches the next Waypoint; a failed Leg costs more rations and worsens the hazard but never stops the ride. C 43; L `expedition.meaning`
- The route is set once before the Expedition begins and nothing changes it afterwards. C 47; RT `route.set_when`
- The Mission Brief's route names: the Waypoints in order, each with its kind and whether it is a Depot, the last a gate; for the Leg to each Waypoint its Distance Band and Formation Post; the days Command orders a Hard Ride; the Abnormal the Abnormal rows name when it is not the Sprinting Abnormal. C 49-52; RT `route.mission_brief_fields`
- Bounds: 4 to 6 Legs; first and last Near, second and second-to-last Near or Far, every Leg between them may be Deep; at most one Depot and never the gate; a Hard Ride at most once a day and only as the day's third Leg; the route sets Distance Band, Post, and Pace, and once the Expedition begins no ruling changes them. C 54; RT `route.bounds` (the Leg count and the one-Depot cap are read from `bounds` in code)
- A Waypoint's kind sets the Anchor Rating of a Titan Engagement on the Leg that leads to it; the gate sets none, so a fight on the Leg to the gate rolls its Anchor Rating on the setup table. C 77, 87; RT `waypoint_kinds`, `kind_rules`; AR `ratings` (names read from it)
- A Depot keeps its kind; arriving there gives every soldier a full Standard Issue together and restocks Squad Supply. C 90; RT `depot`
- Reaching the gate ends the Expedition at the end of that Leg, with no further Leg and no Night Camp; Downtime begins. C 91; L `expedition.ends`
- Before the Expedition begins, every soldier receives a full Standard Issue together and Squad Supply is restocked, rations included. C 95; L `expedition.before_it_begins`
- The interim day and the interim issue never happen while an Expedition is under way; the Night Camp's day, the issue before the Expedition, and a Depot's issue take their place. C 96; L `expedition.interim_rows`
- No Gas Roll on a Leg or at a Night Camp; gas is spent only in a Titan Engagement. C 97; L `expedition.gas`
- Canisters, Blade Sets, and Squad Supply come back only through a full Standard Issue, so what a fight uses up stays used up until the next Depot or the next Expedition, except the Squad Supply a successful called search finds within Gear & ODM's caps. C 97; L `expedition.gear_back`
- On a Leg every soldier who is not Down and whose own horse is not lame is mounted; at a Night Camp everyone is dismounted; a Down soldier, and one whose horse is lame, rides in the wagon or across a saddle and is not mounted. C 98; L `expedition.mounted`
- A Down soldier cannot be the Lead, Help a Leg roll or the camp roll, or make the camp roll. C 99; L `expedition.down_soldiers`
- With no soldier who can make a Leg roll or the camp roll, that roll fails, with no Help and no Push; the Squad still reaches the Waypoint, the Down ride in the wagon, and nothing else changes; a hazard row that names the Lead does nothing when there is no Lead. C 99; L `expedition.every_soldier_down`, `rolls` notes
- Every living soldier takes part; a new character who joins during an Expedition joins at the next Waypoint. C 100; L `expedition.taking_part`
- Promotion and Retirement due on a Leg or at a Night Camp happen when that Leg or Night Camp ends, after any fight, care window, or day passing inside it. C 101; L `expedition.deaths`
- A Talent limited to once per Titan Engagement can also be used once per Leg and once per Night Camp where its trigger can occur (Sure Seat on a Hard Ride); a fight nested in a Leg or a Night Camp keeps its own use, and neither use spends the other; a Drive's turn tests count no Leg roll and no camp roll. C 102; L `expedition.drives_and_talents`
- A day holds two Legs and a Night Camp; a third Leg is ridden only as a Hard Ride Command orders; a day's first or second Leg is never a Hard Ride; Legs are never ridden at night. C 110-113; L `day.steps`, `day.night`, `pace.hard-ride.when` (Fig. 1)
- Every Leg ridden counts toward the day's Legs, including one ridden again after a retreat, whether the retreat came on a Leg, in a Waypoint scene, or at a Night Camp. C 116; L `day.counted_legs`
- Pace: Steady is every Leg that is not a Hard Ride, rolls the Post's entry, spends rations as the Leg roll gives, adds +0; a Hard Ride rolls Ride with the horse's Gear Dice, doubles the rations, and adds +1. C 121-122; L `pace` (the Pace table; the entry name is read from `CAT`)
- The whole Squad rides at one Formation Post, which changes only at a Waypoint; the Post names the Leg roll's entry on a Steady Leg and what it adds to the hazard. C 129; L `formation_posts`
- The six Posts, their entries, their hazard modifiers, and the two with a rule of their own (Signal Relay's flare for -2 once a Leg before the roll; Supply Wagon losing 2 units in place of 1 on the supplies row). C 134-139; L `formation_posts` (the Posts table; a Post that gains or loses its own rule fails the build)
- Riding a Leg, in order: take the Post; the Leg roll; arrive; the hazard, the Signal Relay's flare first; harm; a Titan Engagement the row begins; the Waypoint. C 146-152; L `leg_steps` (Fig. 2)
- Arriving: on a success spend 1 ration and the hazard gets -1 for each success beyond the first; on a failure spend 2 rations and the hazard gets +1; a Hard Ride multiplies the rations by 2; a Squad short of the spend meets hunger. C 148; L `leg_outcome`, `pace.rations_multiplier`
- Harm a row inflicts outside a Titan Engagement opens the care window held after harm outside a fight, with every soldier on the Expedition in its scope. C 150; L `leg_steps.harm`
- A Titan Engagement a row begins takes its Anchor Rating from the kind of the Waypoint this Leg leads to, everything else from the setup table; every living soldier takes part, mounted or not as they rode; a retreat falls back to the Waypoint this Leg started from and rides this Leg again from step 1, with a new Leg roll and a new hazard. C 151; L `leg_steps.titan-engagement`
- The Leg ends at its Waypoint; a Depot issues; the gate ends the Expedition. C 152; L `leg_steps.waypoint`
- The Leg roll: the Lead is one soldier on the Expedition who is not Down, chosen by the players with the roll-off; on a Hard Ride the Lead must be mounted on their own horse; the entry is the Post's on a Steady Leg and Ride on a Hard Ride; it needs 1 success; up to 3 comrades on the Expedition who are not Down Help, spending nothing, each at most once a Leg; Push and Cover as usual; never tried again, and a Leg ridden again makes a new roll. C 170-175; L `rolls.leg-roll`
- The hazard roll and the night hazard roll are table rolls: no pool, no Push, no Help, no Circumstances, never tried again, rolled in the open, and the row that comes up is never softened or swapped. C 181; H `table_rolls`, `choices`, `rolls.hazard-roll`
- A row that lets the players choose is a shared choice, settled by the roll-off. C 181; H `choices`
- What the Leg's hazard counts: the Distance Band (Near +0, Far +1, Deep +2); +1 for each night already camped; the Formation Post; the Pace; +1 for a failed Leg roll; -1 for each success beyond the first; -2 for the Signal Relay's flare. C 186-192; H `leg_hazards.modifiers`; L `leg_outcome`, `pace`, `formation_posts` (the hazard table; every amount is read from the tables)
- What the night counts: the Distance Band of the day's last Leg ridden and +1 if the camp roll failed, and nothing else. C 231, 236; H `night.modifiers`, `night.unused_modifiers`
- The Straggler picks its victim at random: D6, on 1 to 4 a Squadmate and on 5 or 6 a player character, drawn from living soldiers on the Expedition who are not Down; with none of that kind use the other; with neither, the row resolves as the plain Titan attack on the formation. C 216; H `straggler.victim.kind_roll`, `pool`
- The soldiers of that kind are numbered 1 upward in Squad sheet order and a D6 is rolled, rolling again any result above the count, so each is equally likely and the sheet's order chooses nothing. C 217; H `straggler.victim.pick`
- The victim's Endure roll needs 1 success, cannot be Helped or Covered, can be Pushed, and is never tried again. C 218; H `rolls.straggler-endure`
- On a failure, a Bite Critical Injury at a rolled Injury Location, which can be lethal; it is a Titan's harm but not a card, so no Attack Dice, no Reaction, and no Net Success rider on the Critical Injury roll; it counts as gained as the row's Titan Engagement begins, so no separate care window is held and its time limit runs as one gained in that fight. C 219; H `straggler.on_failure`, `timing`
- Then the Titan Engagement begins, the victim at In Reach and every other soldier at Distant. C 220; H `straggler.then`
- The Night Camp, in order: the camp roll; 1 ration; Camp Relief; the night hazard; a day passes. C 226-233; L `night_camp.steps` (Fig. 3)
- The camp roll is Survive by one soldier on the Expedition who is not Down, chosen by the players; it needs 1 success; up to 3 comrades who are not Down Help, spending nothing; Push and Cover; never tried again; with nobody to roll it, it fails. C 228; L `rolls.camp-roll`, `expedition.every_soldier_down`
- Camp Relief: if the camp roll succeeded and the camp did not meet hunger, every soldier on the Expedition loses 1 Stress. C 230; L `night_camp.steps.camp-relief`; `data/core/stress-changes.yaml` `camp-relief`
- The night's Titan Engagement takes its Anchor Rating from the kind of the Waypoint the camp is made at; Titans do not walk at night, and only an Abnormal comes. C 231; L `night_camp.at_waypoint`; H `night.anchor_rating`, the Night table's note
- A retreat at night: fall back overnight to the Waypoint the day's last Leg started from; the camp goes on there at the day that passes, with no new camp roll, ration, or night hazard; it still counts as a Night Camp made; falling back is not riding a Leg, so no Leg roll, no spend, no Leg hazard; the next day's first Leg rides that Leg again from step 1 with a new roll and a new hazard and counts toward that day; any other ending leaves the camp where it was; a camp already at that Waypoint breaks off where it is, counts as the night already counted, and rides the queued Leg next morning at Steady. C 232; L `night_camp.retreat`
- A day passes at the end of the Night Camp: a care window with every soldier on the Expedition in scope and Field Repair rolled in it, the Death Rolls of day limits, all Health lost to damage back, and a day of healing. C 233; L `night_camp.steps.day-passes`
- Rations: Squad Supply holds them by Funding; a Leg spends by its Leg roll and Pace, a Night Camp 1, and two hazard rows spend or spoil them. C 254; L `rations`
- Hunger: short of the spend, the Squad spends every ration it holds and every soldier on the Expedition gains 1 Stress with no roll; a Night Camp that meets hunger gives no Camp Relief. C 256; L `rations.hunger`
- Waypoint scenes: framed by the GM after a Leg ends at a Waypoint that is not the gate, before the next Leg or the Night Camp; run on called rolls with Circumstances and stakes named before the dice; a called search adds Squad Supply only as Gear & ODM caps it, and a scene yields to at most two searches; a Skirmish or a Titan Engagement may begin in one; harm opens the outside-harm care window with the Expedition in scope. C 158-162; L `day.waypoint_scene`
- A retreat from a fight begun in a scene ends the scene and falls back as a hazard's retreat does, riding that Leg again from step 1 and applying the arrival again, a Depot's issue included; the Leg takes the day's next slot (second at Steady after the first; third only if a Hard Ride is ordered, and then as a Hard Ride); with no slot left, camp at the Waypoint that Leg started from and ride it again as the next day's first, at Steady. C 163; L `day.waypoint_scene.retreat`
- A scene rides no Leg: no Leg roll, no camp roll, no hazard read, no ration unless stakes name one, and it never stands in place of a hazard roll. C 164; L `day.waypoint_scene.never`
- Circumstances reach the Leg roll, the camp roll, the Straggler's Endure, and every called roll in a Waypoint scene; the two hazard rolls take none. C 276; H `rolls`, `table_rolls`

## Downtime

- Downtime is the time the Squad spends inside the Walls between Expeditions; it begins when the Squad reaches the gate that ends an Expedition and runs until the next Expedition begins. C 285, 289; D `downtime.meaning`, `begins`, `ends`
- It spans seven days, which pass at the infirmary; nothing else in Downtime takes days. C 290; D `downtime.days`, `infirmary.each_day.count` (the page's day count is read from both, and a disagreement fails the build)
- The interim day and the interim issue never happen while Downtime runs. C 291; D `downtime.interim_rows`
- Every soldier in the Squad takes part; Stress never falls below a soldier's minimum, and Grief never below 0. C 292; D `downtime.scope`, `stress_minimum`, `grief_floor`
- The steps, in order: the infirmary; one Downtime Action per player character, in the order the players choose, with the Squadmate relief for each Squadmate; one Squad Action for the Squad; then the after-actions step. C 298-301; D `steps` (Fig. 4)
- After the actions, in order: granted Requisitions arrive; prosthetics are fitted; medical Retirement is offered again; then every Retirement and promotion due resolves. C 301; D `steps.after-actions`
- The infirmary holds one care window with every soldier in the Squad in scope, and Field Repair is not rolled in it: Maintain Gear restores gear in Downtime. C 309; D `infirmary.care_window`
- Each of the seven days, in order: one infirmary roll for every lethal Critical Injury on a day limit, a success stabilizing it without treating it; a Death Roll for every lethal Critical Injury that still has a day limit; all Health lost to damage back for every living soldier; every held Critical Injury's healing time down 1 day, one reaching 0 healing, an untreated injury whose rider stops it healing keeping its time, and a lethal one never healing while lethal. C 311-314; D `infirmary.each_day.steps` (rendered; the wording is keyed by each line's first five words, so a rewording fails the build)
- The seven days hold no care window of their own. C 316; D `infirmary.each_day.no_care_window`
- A death on an infirmary day causes no Fear Roll; its Grief is the Grief of a day passing, and promotion is timed as for a day passing. C 316; D `infirmary.each_day.deaths`
- The infirmary roll: rolled for the patient, a Down patient included; 4 base dice and nothing else, not an attribute roll and taking no Circumstances; needs 1 success; never Helped or Pushed; once per Critical Injury per infirmary day, and again on each later day while it is still lethal. C 311, 347; D `rolls.infirmary-roll`
- Downtime Actions: Recover (lose 2 Stress); Visit Haven (lose 2 Stress and 2 Grief); Requisition (make one, or Help a comrade's Requisition roll with it); Maintain Gear (every rated item held returns to its rating, ODM Gear unjammed, a lame horse sound, a worn kit or firearm whole, a ruined Blade Set still ruined). C 47-50 of the rendered block, C 322-325; D `downtime_actions.rows`
- Squadmates take no Downtime Action and lose 2 Stress and 1 Grief. C 327; D `squadmates` (the page reads both numbers from it)
- Squad Actions: Honoring the Fallen (every soldier in the Squad loses 1 Grief); Recruit (new Squadmates join until the Squad holds 6 counting the player characters, each with a template, a name, and Standard Issue). C 333-334; D `squad_actions.rows`
- Train, Investigate, Contribute to Research, lobbying for Funding, HQ Upgrades, XP, Commendations, and Faction Standing are not yet written. C 339; D `downtime_actions.later`, `squad_actions.later` (the page says only that they are not yet written into these rules)

## Requisition

- Requisition is how a soldier gets gear beyond Standard Issue; there are no prices; Funding decides what Command will consider, and the item's Scarcity and the roll decide the rest. C 364; RQ `when`
- It is a Downtime Action, one per player character per Downtime, never before a campaign's first Expedition and never outside Downtime. C 368; RQ `when`
- Scarcity sets the successes before the ledger: Standard 1, Limited 2, Rare 3. C 99-101 of the rendered block; RQ `scarcity`
- The Funding gate: 1 to 2 reaches Standard; 3 to 4 Standard and Limited; 5 to 6 all three. Funding is 3 until the Funding rules are written. C 107-109 of the rendered block, C 372; RQ `funding_gate`; `data/gear/standard-issue.yaml` `funding.until_funding_rules` (the page derives each Scarcity's Funding reach from the gate rows and marks which are open at the current Funding)
- A row locked behind a Discovery cannot be named until the Squad holds it; no row on the list is locked. C 373; RQ `funding_gate.locked_rows`
- The roll: name one row the gate allows, with any choice it asks for and the soldier it is for; roll Persuade or Recall, chosen before rolling; needs the row's Scarcity plus the ledger. C 384-387; RQ `procedure`, `rolls.requisition-roll` (Fig. 5)
- Help: up to 3 comrades in the Squad who are not Down; a player character Helps by taking Requisition as their own Downtime Action and spending it on the Help; a Squadmate Helps at most once a Downtime and spends nothing. C 388; RQ `rolls.requisition-roll.help`
- Circumstances may be named for leverage (a favour owed, a Commendation, a captured specimen); Scarcity and the ledger are needs and never change. C 389; RQ `rolls.requisition-roll.notes`
- Push and Cover as usual, and Stress gained rides out on the next Expedition; never tried again this Downtime. C 390-391; RQ `rolls.requisition-roll.push`, `retry`
- On a success Command grants the row and the ledger rises by 1; on a failure Command says no; successes beyond the needs do nothing. C 392; RQ `procedure.result`
- The ledger is Command's patience within one Downtime; it starts at 0 and counts the Requisitions granted this Downtime. C 396; RQ `ledger`
- Size Up on the quartermaster: once a Downtime, before any Requisition roll; one soldier in the Squad who is not Down, a Squadmate included, chosen by the players; it is not a Downtime Action; needs 1 success; never Helped or Covered; can be Pushed; never tried again; on a success the ledger counts 1 lower for the whole Squad for the rest of the Downtime, never below 0. C 397; RQ `ledger.size_up`, `ledger.value`, `rolls.size-up-for-the-ledger`
- A granted item belongs to the named soldier and is a kept item: it counts as items, can be passed, taken, and shared out, and Standard Issue never takes it away unless it is handed in at an exchange; a Blade Set above rating 1 is still ruined by any wear; ODM Gear or a horse replaces the soldier's own, which leaves play. C 403; RQ `grant.items`
- Squad Supply units are added above the stock and are not kept items. C 404; RQ `grant.supply`
- A grant arrives at the after-actions step of this Downtime, before prosthetics are fitted and before the next Standard Issue, so a limb healed during the seven days can take a Requisitioned prosthetic the same Downtime. C 405, 301; RQ `grant.arrives`; D `steps.after-actions`
- The fifteen rows of the list, with their Scarcity and what each row says (the spare canister and the rating 1 Blade Set are one more than the Funding row gives; the kits Standard Issue gives only to a Medic or an Engineer; the supply and shot rows added above the stock; the firearms empty, the musket 2 items; the prosthetic one per Requisition, of a lost side with no prosthetic on it, only once the loss has healed, a Squadmate nameable as its soldier, fitted at a Downtime; ODM Gear 3 and horse 3 replacing the soldier's own; every Blade Set above 1 still ruined by any wear). C 149-163 of the rendered block; RQ `list.rows` (rendered, grouped by Scarcity; a new row fails the build until it has wording)
- The GM may add a row that meets the charter, at a Scarcity the era allows; an added item that rates a measured roll is at least Limited; the Funding gate, the ledger, and one Requisition per Downtime stand. C 144; RQ `list.gm_may_add` (stated in the list table's note, without the charter's name)
