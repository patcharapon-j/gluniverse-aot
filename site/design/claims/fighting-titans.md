## How a Titan Fight Works

- A Titan Engagement runs in rounds. A Titan tracked in full is a Focus Titan, labelled A then B; at most two are alive at once. Anything else closing in is a Background Titan, run as a public clock. C 185, 887; POS 32-35; BG 865-873
- Who takes part: the soldiers the starting rule names; if it names none, every living soldier of the Squad. C 104; POS `placement`, `who_takes_part`
- Everyone starts Distant relative to Focus Titan A unless the starting rule names other Positions for named soldiers. Mounted stay mounted, dismounted stay dismounted, no one airborne; a dismounted soldier's horse holds Distant. C 105-107; POS `placement`, `default`
- A starting Position is placed by a rule, not the soldier's own move, so it meets no Drive Position trigger until the soldier's own move changes it. C 108; POS `placement`, `drives`
- The Focus Titan starts with Intact Body Parts, no Openings, an empty Regeneration clock, and its hidden Next Behavior rolled. C 95
- It reads its Attention Ladder with no cards dealt; unless a rung picks out one soldier, nothing holds its Attention until its first card. C 97; ATT `changes`, `titan-becomes-focus`
- The Fear Rolls for a first Titan Engagement and for an Abnormal are made at the start. C 98
- The Squad's Squad Tactics become unused, then round 1 begins. C 99-100
- Round steps, in order: Wings, deal, swap, play the cards lowest first, end steps. RND `round_steps` (rendered by `RoundDiagram`)
- Twenty cards numbered 1 to 20, dealt face up, no two alike: one to each living soldier except a Squadmate on a Wing, and as many to each living Focus Titan as its Tempo. A soldier who is Down, Grabbed, carried, or has left is still dealt one. C 303; RND `initiative_cards`, `round_steps`, `deal`
- Wings are assigned at the first wings step and stand; they may change only if, since the last wings step, a soldier died, left, went Down, or was Grabbed, or a Focus Titan entered or died. A Wing holds at most one Squadmate; on a Wing it is dealt no card and acts right after its player character, even on a turn spent in advance. C 305-312; RND `wings`
- Swap: two soldiers each holding a card, neither Down nor Grabbed, at the same Position or one step apart, or both having left; each takes part in at most one swap; a Titan's card is never swapped; not an action. C 314-320; RND `swapping`
- Turn: one move and one action, in either order, never split around each other, except under a retreat where the forced move comes first. C 325; RND `turn_order`
- End steps in order: Gas Rolls together for everyone who used ODM Gear (made at once, before the end-of-fight steps, if the fight ends mid-round); Regeneration 1 segment on each living Focus Titan; Background clocks 1 each and full clocks resolve, then the retreat clock 1, and if full the fight becomes a retreat; round ends, unspent moves and actions lost. In a retreat no Background clock and no retreat clock fills; Regeneration still does. C 332-338; RND `end_steps`
- A Reaction spends one whole turn, both move and action. C 328
- Circumstances in a fight are Standard unless the GM names a step for something no rule prices; a step reaches every attribute roll a soldier makes and never reaches the Titan, whose Attack Dice, cards, Attention, and tables take no step. A mired Titan makes the dodge against it Easy, named before its card comes up, and it loses no Attack Dice. C 66, 83, 508
- Improvised act: an act that is not an entry taken as written; the GM names the model entry before the pool is built and that ruling stands; it spends the action and produces only that entry's effect at that entry's size. C 436-440
- The four worked examples: a cut-loose wagon is a Break Attention decoy; a swung lantern is Draw Attention (unrolled, never from Distant, sets the loudest mark); a dropped chimney is a Body Part strike on that leg from the Position a leg is struck from; a dive behind a chimney stack is a move or nothing, never modelled on the dodge and never making a later dodge easier. C 443-446
- An act modelled on the Nape strike never kills: it resolves as a strike that falls short whatever its successes, each success makes 1 Opening, it sets the struck-the-Nape mark, and it lowers no Nape Depth. C 448
- A called roll in a fight: only where no entry of kind Action could model it and it would change nothing the fight tracks; the granary doors example; spends the action, made on the soldier's turn, never stands in for a Reaction. C 450

## Positions and Moves

- The four Positions and their meanings, and that a soldier holds one relative to each Focus Titan and each corpse; holding one is the test for being in the Titan Engagement; the dead and those who left hold none. C 185; POS `positions`, `relative_to`, `holding_a_position`
- Comparison: relative to the Titan the act is made against (strike, Break Attention, Draw Attention, Read, Break Free, dodge, and Help or Covering on them); relative to the body for Heave and the strike on a pinning Body Part; otherwise relative to the earliest-labelled living Focus Titan, or the corpse of the Focus Titan that died last. C 187-190; POS `comparison`
- Position steps by Anchor Rating, with on foot, mounted, ODM, and the Fly roll needs and failure Positions. Two Positions joined by a row are one step apart either way. C 194-230; ANC `ratings` (rendered)
- A move changes the Position relative to one named Focus Titan by one step its kind can make, or changes none; it can include one mount or dismount; it is the only way a soldier changes their own Position, every other change coming from a rule that names it. C 245; POS `moves`, `what_a_move_does`
- Mounted: a mounted step, or an ODM move that dismounts first. Down: only In Reach to Distant, on foot, while not Grabbed and not carried. Grabbed or carried: nothing. Pinned: nothing, and no rule moves them. C 249-252; POS `moves`
- Letting go: a move at On Body or Blind Spot, not Grabbed or carried; the soldier falls from that height. C 253; POS `moves`, `letting_go`
- A Fear Roll's forced step: one step at the start of the next turn, toward Distant or the nearest comrade; a result, not the move; spends nothing; happens on a turn spent in advance; never for a soldier who is Down, Grabbed, or carried, or with no such step; never makes a soldier leave or let go. C 255; POS `moves`, `forced_step`
- Grounded: every step joining In Reach, On Body, and Blind Spot can be made on foot and none mounted; at Open a grounded Titan gains an On Body to Blind Spot step, on foot or by ODM; when it stands, a soldier at Open holding Blind Spot holds On Body instead, with no fall. C 257-259; ANC `grounded_titan`
- Where a fall lands: the reference Titan is the one whose Position is closest, in the order On Body, Blind Spot, In Reach, Distant; on a tie the Titan whose card, Grab, or effect caused it, otherwise the earliest label. A fall from On Body or Blind Spot lands In Reach relative to it; from In Reach or Distant the Position is kept; it changes no Position relative to another Focus Titan and is never the soldier's move. C 263; POS `falls_land`
- Two Focus Titans: a second arrives with everyone Distant; a move changes one Titan's Position; the close rule; a Grabbed soldier holds On Body relative to the holding Titan and the close rule applies; when a Focus Titan dies, Positions relative to it end and the last Position becomes the Position relative to its corpse. C 269-276; POS `two_focus_titans`
- A corpse: its Positions (On Body and Blind Spot read In Reach; a soldier who held none holds none); it counts as a grounded Titan for steps and a move may name it; its Positions count for every rule that reads one; it holds no Attention, plays no cards, has no clock, and is no fall's reference Titan; Heave and the strike on a pinning Body Part are the only acts against it. C 280-285; POS `corpse`; ANC `grounded_titan`, `corpse`
- Horses: change Position only with their rider's move and with Horse Whistle; a successful riderless-horse decoy leaves the fight and holds no Position; no ladder chooses a horse and no behavior affects one; no rule moves a dead soldier's left items. C 289-291; POS `horses`

## Reading a Titan

- Stat block: Size Class and Abnormal, Tempo, Nape Depth, Regeneration clock, Heave rating, Body Parts in order with Toughness (the order breaks Regeneration ties and decides which arm Grabs), Attention Ladder. C 392-394; C6 52-63; FMT `stat_block`
- A standard Titan takes every number from its Size Class row, has exactly the five standard Body Parts in order, and uses the standard ladder; its numbers are public. An Abnormal lists all its values and keeps Toughness, Nape Depth, Regeneration clock length, and ladder hidden until a Read. C 396; C6 62-63; FMT `stat_block`, `standard_titan`
- Size Class table: height, Tempo, Nape Depth, Regeneration clock, Heave rating, and Toughness per Body Part kind, with the Large Titan raising every fall band. SZ `classes` (rendered)
- Behavior Table: D6; results escalate, 1 and 2 terrorize, 3 and 4 control, 5 and 6 kill; one Thrash entry, never rolled, for when nothing else can happen. C 400-402; FMT `behavior_table`
- Tier limits: terrorize only Stress and a telegraph; control adds a knock loose and a Critical Injury that cannot be lethal; kill adds a lethal Critical Injury and the Grab, and a Grab entry has no other harming effect; Thrash works at any Position, uses no Body Part, inflicts nothing lethal, and rolls the control pool. C 415-419; FMT `tier_rules`
- Row fields: results and tier, targets (holder, or holder and everyone else at their Position who is not Grabbed), the holder's Position requirement, Body Parts used, Attack Dice, effects in order, fallback, and the Grab mark. C 404-413; C6 65-80; FMT `entry_fields`
- An entry that uses a leg needs both legs, so a grounded Titan cannot resolve it. C6 71, 136
- Attack Dice per Size Class and tier, with Thrash on the control pool and a telegraph-only entry rolling none. SZ `attack_dice`; FMT `entry_fields`, `attack_dice` (rendered)
- Titan Dice succeed on 5 or 6, are rolled by the GM in the open in a colour no soldier's pool uses, never Push, and never take Bonus Dice, Help, Gear Dice, Stress Dice, or Circumstances; the successes are the card's Severity, the same for every target. C 423; FMT `titan_dice`
- Effect types, a closed list: Stress (once, whatever the Net Successes); a Critical Injury at the listed location and type, lethal or not as listed, its roll adding 1 for each Net Success beyond the first; a knock loose (airborne, On Body, or Blind Spot falls; a mounted target is not affected; the same whatever the Net Successes); a Grab (the same whatever the Net Successes); a telegraph (reveals the next Next Behavior, whether the behavior landed or whiffed). C 426-430; FMT `effect_types`
- No entry names death; a Titan kills only through a lethal Critical Injury, the Grab's devour step, or a fall one of its effects causes. C 421; C6 92-95

## When a Titan Acts

- A Focus Titan always has a hidden Next Behavior, rolled out of sight before it is needed and standing once rolled. C 462; PROC `next_behavior`
- Resolving a card, in order: a dead Titan's card does nothing; a Titan holding a living Grabbed soldier resolves nothing, does not read the ladder, keeps its Next Behavior, and leaves every mark standing; a decoy's card resolves nothing, takes one card off the hold, and spends the Next Behavior (it becomes the previous behavior, any Call It ends, a new one is rolled), with only the hold's last card ending the hold and reading the ladder, marks standing and decoys in a row unchanged; otherwise the ladder is read, and with nobody holding Attention the card resolves nothing and marks stand; the entry is chosen, Thrash if the Body Parts are missing, the fallback if the holder's Position does not fit, Thrash if the fallback repeats the previous behavior or fails either test; the name, tier, and targets are announced and the Attack Dice rolled in the open for the Severity; on 0 successes the card whiffs against everyone and nobody dodges, otherwise each target dodges or cancels with an earlier dodge and the card lands on 1 or more Net Successes; effects apply in order, targets in card order, only a Critical Injury reading the Net Successes, a telegraph applying either way, and a target who dies receiving none of the card's later effects while every other target still does; then the behavior becomes the previous one, decoys in a row returns to 0, every mark for that Titan clears, a new Next Behavior is rolled, and a telegraph reveals it. C 479-497; PROC `resolving_a_card`
- Dodging: the only Reaction against a Titan, Agility with Gear Dice from ODM Gear or the horse while mounted, one item; made after the Titan's roll is announced; one per Titan per round, its successes cancelling against every later card that Titan resolves against the soldier that round, each separately, without being used up; it spends the earliest wholly unspent turn; a Down soldier and a soldier under a state or result that forbids Reactions cancel nothing; the Circumstances step is the one in force when the card came up and is never named or changed afterwards; a Called behavior gives 2 Bonus Dice; a landed Critical Injury adds 1 to its roll for each Net Success beyond the first, while a Grab, a knock loose, and a Stress gain take nothing extra. C 502-507, 634 (`01-core-rules.md`)

## Attention

- Attention is held by one soldier, a decoy, or nothing, and is public; the holder cannot make a Nape strike against that Titan. C 522; ATT `holder`
- It changes only when the Titan becomes a Focus Titan; when one of its cards comes up, except a card leaving a decoy's hold with cards left and a card while it holds a Grabbed soldier; when a Break Attention places a decoy; when its Grab lands; and when the holder dies, leaves, is freed, is Grabbed by another Focus Titan, or stops holding a Position relative to it, after which nothing holds it until the next reading. Between those moments the holder does not change. C 524-531; ATT `changes`
- Candidates are the living soldiers holding a Position relative to it; never a soldier who has left, a soldier held by another Titan, or a horse. A Down or carried soldier is a candidate but meets only the rungs their row allows, which on the standard ladder is nearest alone. C 535-538, 560; ATT `candidates`
- The standard ladder's five rungs, highest first, with which a Down soldier can meet. C 540-545; ATT `ladders`, `tests` (rendered)
- Reading it: the highest rung anyone meets, with nearest tying only those at the closest Position; the struck-the-Nape narrowing at the hooked-into-its-body rung; narrowing by each lower rung, nearest keeping the closest Position; the current holder keeps a tie at equal distance; otherwise the lowest card this round, a Wing Squadmate counting just after its player character, skipped when no cards have been dealt and at an end step; otherwise nothing holds it until its next card. C 547-553; ATT `evaluation`
- The three marks and what sets each. C 556-558; ATT `flags` (rendered)
- A mark lasts until the end of that Titan's next card that resolves a behavior and clears at that card's last step; a card that resolves nothing leaves every mark standing, including a decoy hold's last card, a card while the Titan holds a Grabbed soldier, and a card with nobody holding its Attention. C 555; ATT `flag_duration`
- Draw Attention: an unrolled action, needing a Position other than Distant and not Grabbed, spending the action and setting the loudest mark; a Fear Roll result sets the same mark with no action under the same Position requirement, lasting and read the same way. C 564-566; ATT `draw_attention`
- Break Attention: Perception with Gear Dice from ODM Gear or the horse, or none for a Feint on foot; taken by a soldier holding a Position relative to the Titan, not Grabbed, naming a decoy whose requirement they meet, while no decoy already holds that Titan's Attention. C 572-573; ATT `break_attention`
- Successes needed: 1 for the holder, 2 for anyone else including while nothing holds it, 2 against a Titan holding a Grabbed soldier whoever rolls, plus 1 for a Feint and plus 1 for each decoy in a row. C 574-579; ATT `break_attention`, `needs` (rendered)
- Decoys in a row: those that have held its Attention since the last of its cards that resolved a behavior, whoever placed them; 0 when it becomes a Focus Titan, up 1 whenever a decoy takes its Attention, back to 0 when one of its cards resolves a behavior, unchanged by a card that resolves nothing. C 579; ATT `needs_rule`
- On a success: the decoy holds Attention for as many of its next cards as its Tempo, one round of its cards however the deal falls, decoys in a row rises by 1, each of those cards resolves nothing and spends the Next Behavior, only the last ends the hold and reads the ladder, no decoy can be laid over one that holds; each success beyond the whole need makes 1 Opening created by the roller; a soldier the Titan holds is freed. C 582-584; ATT `on_success`
- The four decoys with their requirements, what each spends, and their extra effects: the flare filling 1 segment of every Background Titan's clock once the Break Attention and any Hook and Cut are resolved, whether it succeeded or not; the riderless horse leaving the fight; the cloak gone for the rest of the fight; the Feint spending nothing, needing 1 more, and naming its own gear. C 586-601; ATT `decoys` (rendered)
- Every Attention Ladder begins with hooked into its body and ends with nearest, so nothing turns a Titan off a blade at its Nape or a soldier On Body; an Abnormal may name its own between them. C 605

## Cutting Titans Down

- Titans have no Health. C 625
- Body Part kinds, the Positions each is struck from, and what Breaking each does. C 629-631, 649-651; HRM `body_part_kinds` (rendered)
- A Body Part strike is Strength with Gear Dice from a Blade Set, needing the Position its kind names, the part not already Broken, the striker not Grabbed, and working ODM Gear from On Body or Blind Spot unless the Titan is grounded. C 633-637; HRM `body_part_strikes`
- Counts and states: each success adds 1 to the count; at the Toughness the part moves one state toward Broken and the count returns to 0; once Broken each remaining success makes 1 Opening created by the striker; a strike with at least 1 success sets the just-hurt mark; states, counts, and a standard Titan's Toughness are public and an Abnormal's is revealed by a Read. C 639-644; HRM `states`
- An entry cannot happen while the Titan lacks enough unbroken Body Parts of each kind it uses. C 648
- Grounded: 2 Bonus Dice on Nape strikes; no working ODM Gear needed for Nape or Body Part strikes; every Body Part struck from In Reach, On Body, or Blind Spot except an arm holding a Grabbed soldier; close steps on foot; a Feint on foot with no gear item. Grounded until no leg is Broken; on becoming grounded the body comes down, and on standing again every pinned soldier is freed and the heave count returns to 0. C 651-658; HRM `grounded`
- Nape strike: Strength with a Blade Set, needing Blind Spot, not holding its Attention, not Grabbed, working ODM Gear unless grounded, and no retreat under way. C 662-666; HRM `nape_strikes`
- Its Bonus Dice come from Openings, the grounded Titan, and Help, within the cap of 4; every Nape strike sets the struck-the-Nape mark whatever its result; reaching the Nape Depth kills; falling short turns each success into 1 Opening created by the striker, and Relentless adds 1; a strike's successes never add to a later Nape strike except as Openings. C 668-673
- Openings: a public token recording its creator, from successes beyond Breaking a part, extra Break Attention successes, each success of a short Nape strike, and any rule that names one; spent as Bonus Dice 1 die each on a Nape strike; never spendable by their creator; erased by Regeneration and by the Titan's death. C 677-685; HRM `openings`
- Regeneration fills 1 segment at the end of each round, retreat or not; when full, in order: Openings erased, counts to 0, the most damaged Body Part one state toward Intact (Broken before Wounded, then the one listed first), steam on every soldier On Body if a part moved, the Titan standing and freeing its pinned soldiers with the heave count to 0 if it was the last Broken leg, then the clock empties. C 693-698; HRM `regeneration`
- A Titan's death, in order: the Nape-kill Stress relief to everyone holding a Position; a held soldier freed, at On Body relative to the dying body and not airborne, with a fall from the release resolved first; steam on everyone at On Body or Blind Spot; the body comes down unless it was already grounded, in which case it falls no second time and its pinned soldiers stay Pinned under the corpse with corpse heat beginning; Openings, Next Behavior, clock, marks, and remaining cards removed; Positions relative to it end and each last Position is recorded; the body stays as a corpse; the Titan Engagement ends if no Focus Titan is alive and no soldier is Pinned. C 707-715; HRM `titan_death`
- Steam is Burn damage, reaching the Critical Injury tables only at 0 current Health; it rolls no Attack Dice and no Reaction answers it; at a kill it catches everyone at On Body or Blind Spot including a soldier the death freed from the hand, and at a Regeneration fill that moves a Body Part it catches everyone On Body, a fill that moves nothing releasing none; each rolls D6 once, and In Reach or Distant takes nothing. C 719-722; HRM `steam`, `table` (rendered)
- The falling body: the path is On Body and Blind Spot; an airborne soldier swings clear with no roll and keeps their Position; In Reach and Distant are outside it, and so is a soldier Grabbed in another Titan's hand and one this Titan holds at its grounding, who stays Grabbed; a soldier its death freed is in the path at On Body. C 727; HRM `falling_titan`, `path`
- Leap Clear: Agility with Gear Dice from ODM Gear or the horse while mounted, needing 1; not an action and not a Reaction, so it spends no turn; comrades at the same Position may Help, each spending their action, and it can be Pushed; success clears the soldier to In Reach, failure pins them. A Down soldier in the path is pinned with no roll; a carried soldier follows their carrier, both clearing or both Pinned with their own Critical Injury and pinning Body Part. C 728-729; HRM `leap_clear`
- Pinned: a crushing Critical Injury at a rolled location that cannot be lethal and takes no Net Success rider, an arm or leg result being a limb pin and a torso or head result a body pin; the pinning Body Part is the Titan's part of the same kind and side, the other of its kind if that is Broken, and none if both are, in which case only a Heave or the Titan standing frees the soldier; a body pin names no part, and the Position at the fall plays no part; the soldier holds In Reach relative to the body, is not airborne, cannot move or be moved, and Lift Comrade never takes them; a card lands on them as on anyone who cannot react, a Grab on them lands only its crush, and a pending forced strike waits. C 733-736; HRM `pinned`
- What each pin still allows and forbids, and that Help, Covering, and Reactions come from the pin itself. C 738-743; EFF `pinned` (rendered)
- Corpse heat: a burning Critical Injury at the start of each of the soldier's turns, from the first turn that begins after the fall, at the pinned limb and side or the torso for a body pin; it can be lethal and takes worsening; no heat under a living grounded Titan, and heat beginning at the soldier's first turn after that Titan dies; a limb-pinned soldier who loses the pinned limb stays limb-pinned with the same pinning Body Part. C 745; HRM `corpse_heat`
- Heave: an action, Strength with no Gear Dice, from In Reach or On Body relative to a body that pins someone, by a soldier not Down, Grabbed, carried, or body-pinned; a limb-pinned soldier may Heave the body pinning them; comrades at the same Position may Help; each success adds 1 to the public heave count, cleared only when a living Titan stands; at the Heave rating every soldier the body pins is freed; a Heave against a corpse costs the heaver 1 Burn damage after the roll whatever the result; 0 successes frees no one. C 750; HRM `heave`
- Cutting free: a Body Part strike against the pinning Body Part from In Reach or On Body relative to the body, needing no working ODM Gear; the limb-pinned soldier may strike it; successes count toward Toughness and Breaking it frees everyone it pins; on a living Titan it sets the just-hurt mark and extra successes make Openings; a corpse keeps its states and counts, never regenerates, sets no mark, and makes no Opening; a body pin is never cut free. C 751; HRM `cut_free`
- A living Titan that stops being grounded frees every soldier it pins, and the end of a Titan Engagement frees no one. C 752-754
- The worked Nape kill follows C 1160-1216, with the Bonus Dice, the Push, the Openings created, and the Gas Rolls at the end.

## Read and Call It

- Read: an Instinct action with no gear item, for a soldier holding a Position relative to that Focus Titan and not Grabbed; needs 1; 2 Bonus Dice from Distant; for each success the player picks one fact, each once per Read, and the GM reveals it to every soldier. C 766-771; RD `read`
- The closed fact list, which Titan kinds each is for, and how long each stands. C 772-773; RD `facts` (rendered)
- What is public without a Read: Size Class and whether it is an Abnormal, Body Part states and counts, filled clock segments, Openings, the Attention holder and the marks, the previous behavior, Background clocks, and the state of a Grab; against a standard Titan the Next Behavior is the one thing a Read can learn. C 775; RD `public_without_a_read`
- Call It: an option of a Read that revealed the Next Behavior, taking 2 of that Read's successes including the one that revealed it, or 1 with Sharp Call; every target other than the reader who dodges the card that resolves the Called behavior gains 2 Bonus Dice on it, even when it resolves as its fallback or as Thrash; the reader gains nothing; a target cancelling with an earlier dodge gains nothing; a Next Behavior can be Called once; it ends when that card is done, when a decoy's card spends the behavior, or when the Titan dies. C 779-785; RD `call_it`

## The Grab

- Grabbed forbids Help, Covering, and Reactions, and the soldier can still Push; they hold On Body relative to the holding Titan by a rule, not their own move; they stop being airborne, are dismounted with no fall, and stop carrying anyone; their move changes nothing and Break Free is their only action; they hold that Titan's Attention and no other Titan can choose them; becoming Down does not end it. C 797-804; GRB `grabbed_state`
- A Grab lands on 1 or more Net Successes and takes no rider. Steps: the failed-dodge refund; the hold by the first unbroken arm, whose Toughness is 1 and count 0 while it holds, its state unchanged, with a carried comrade set down and falling if the target was airborne; the crush, a torso crushing Critical Injury that cannot be lethal and crosses off a Health box, leaving a soldier whose last box it takes Down in the hand without a fall; Attention; and every witness's Fear Roll. C 808-813; GRB `grab_lands`
- A Grab cannot take hold of a Pinned soldier: only the crush lands, with no hold, no Attention, no Fear Rolls, and no countdown, and the soldier stays Pinned. C 815; GRB `pinned_target`
- The countdown counts the Grabbed soldier's own turns after the Grab lands, a turn spent in advance and a turn taken while Down included; the Grab spends the action of the first counted turn whether or not anything else already did; a dodge against the Grab's card, or an earlier dodge that cancelled against it, that spent a later turn has that turn given back and the first counted turn counts as spent instead. C 819; GRB `countdown`
- Lifted at the end of the first counted turn; devoured at the end of the second, the soldier dying and every witness making a Fear Roll. Neither is a behavior or a Titan attack: neither rolls Attack Dice, each happens whenever its turn ends with the soldier still Grabbed, and no Reaction answers either. C 820-823; GRB `countdown`, `steps`
- While the Titan holds a living soldier each of its cards resolves nothing and its Next Behavior waits; if the Grabbed soldier dies by any rule the Grab ends at once, the arm returns to normal, the body does not fall, and the next card reads the ladder and can act. C 825; GRB `while_holding`, `grabbed_soldier_dies`
- Every way out, with who, what it takes, and the reach and penalty before and after the lift. C 831-837; GRB `escapes` (rendered)
- Break Free is Strength with Gear Dice from a Blade Set with Help as usual, helpers comparing Position relative to the holding Titan; a strike from On Body or Blind Spot needs working ODM Gear unless the Titan is grounded; the reach holds whether or not it is grounded, and only Clear the Hand widens it. C 839; GRB `escapes`
- Release: before the lift, In Reach with no fall; after the lift, a fall from On Body's height landing In Reach; either way the arm's Toughness returns with a count of 0 and the Titan's Attention is held by nothing until its next card, unless a decoy freed the soldier. C 842-844; GRB `release`
- Witness: every soldier who, once the event is fully resolved, is alive and holds a Position in the Titan Engagement, other than the victim; a soldier who has left is not one; a Down witness makes no Fear Roll; one event gives each soldier at most one Fear Roll. C 855; FLW `witnesses`

## Retreat and Ending the Fight

- A Background Titan's Size Class, stat block, and clock are public and nobody holds a Position relative to it; its clock fills 1 at the end of each round and 1 for each flare spent on a Break Attention, once that Break Attention and any Hook and Cut are resolved. C 867-869; BG `clocks`, `ticks`
- Entering: with a full clock and fewer than two Focus Titans alive it takes the next label, starts clean, everyone holds Distant relative to it, it reads its Attention, the second-Focus-Titan or Abnormal Fear Roll is made, and it is dealt cards from the next round. C 879-883; BG `full_clock`
- The retreat clock: every Titan Engagement carries one, starting empty, filling 1 at the background-clocks end step after every Background clock has filled and every full clock resolved; a flare never fills it; when full the fight becomes a retreat. C 873; BG `retreat_clock`
- A retreat also starts when a Background Titan's clock fills while two Focus Titans are alive, and that Titan does not enter; at most two Focus Titans are ever in play. C 887
- In a retreat no clock fills and no Titan enters, and each living Focus Titan's Regeneration clock still fills. C 889; BG `retreat`, `effects`, `no-entries`
- Forced moves, the four options, and the comparisons for options 3 and 4; a soldier who can do none may let go or change nothing. C 890-896; BG `retreat`, `moves`
- The stay limit: options 3 and 4 open only on the retreat's first rounds, as many as its retreat clock has segments, counted from the round after the retreat began; it covers a comrade Pinned under a corpse in every retreat, and every comrade while no Focus Titan is alive; from the next round each such move is option 1 or 2; while a Focus Titan is alive a Down or Grabbed comrade, or one Pinned under a living Titan, has no such limit. C 897-901; BG `retreat`, `moves`
- Order: the move before the action, except a stay-with-a-comrade move after the action taken for that comrade, and a move after Lift Comrade. C 902-904; BG `retreat`, `order`
- No return during a retreat; actions are not limited, only ordered, except that no Nape strike is made during a retreat, on a soldier's turn or through Hook and Cut, while Body Part strikes, Break Attention, Pry Loose, Treat Injury, and Lift Comrade are still taken. C 905-906; BG `retreat`, `no-return`, `actions`
- Leaving: a soldier Distant relative to every Focus Titan and every corpse, not Down, Grabbed, or carried, uses their move to leave; a carried comrade leaves with them, and their horse if they ride it; a later move can bring them back at Distant relative to every Focus Titan and every corpse, but not during a retreat. C 916-918; POS `leaving`
- After leaving: turns still taken and counted; the move can only return; the action can be any entry needing no Position relative to a Titan; all soldiers who have left count as sharing one Position and none of them is at, or one step from, a soldier still in the fight; no Titan targets them and no ladder chooses them; their ODM use still makes a Gas Roll. C 920-926; POS `leaving`, `after_leaving`
- The two ending tests, checked once the event that made one true is fully resolved, and that the GM never ends a Titan Engagement. C 932-940; FLW `ending`
- The first test and the run-on while a soldier lies Pinned under a corpse. C 933; FLW `ending`, `run_on`
- The second test, including every soldier having left, a body-pinned soldier not standing and a limb-pinned soldier who is not Down standing, and the lost-limb reading past the stay limit that reads the grade and not the turn, keeping the soldier's Position, turns, and actions and leaving the lift open. C 934; FLW `no-soldier-standing`, `past_the_stay_limit`
- The returner rule: no Position held at all or no returner ends it at once; otherwise it ends at the round-ends step of the next round if still nobody is standing, or at once if the last returner is gone, and someone standing again erases the count. C 936-938
- Ending by the second test while a Titan lives: every soldier still holding a Position dies, left to the Titans, with no witnesses and no Fear Roll but counting for Grief, and their left items leave play; every soldier still Pinned dies under the body whether or not a Titan lives; with no Focus Titan alive the others go through the end steps as after any kill. C 940
- A Titan Engagement always ends: by a kill, by no soldier standing, or by the retreat its retreat clock forces, and the Squad may leave earlier by choice. C 942; FLW `always_ends`

## Squad Tactics

- A Squad Tactic belongs to the Squad; the Squad holds two, chosen together before its first Titan Engagement with the usual roll-off on disagreement; each can be used once per Titan Engagement when its condition is met. C 952-954; SQT `rules`
- Using one is an option, not an action, and a use is spent once declared; each names who declares it and when; several declarers who disagree settle it with the usual roll-off. C 952; SQT `rules`
- The four tactics, with their conditions, declarers, moments, and effects. C 956-961; SQT `tactics` (rendered)
