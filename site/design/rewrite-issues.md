# Rewrite issues

Disagreements and unclear rules found while rewriting pages. The page follows the tables where they disagree with chapter prose; nothing in `docs/rules/` or `data/` was edited.

## The Rules of Play (`site/src/content/rules/rules-of-play.mdx`)

1. **Who cannot be Helped.** `docs/rules/01-core-rules.md` 268 names the Death Roll's and the passive roll's exception rows as excluding Help, but not the performance roll's. `data/core/bonus-dice-sources.yaml` (help, `applies_to`) and `data/core/dice-pool.yaml` (performance-roll, `bonus_sources_excluded`) exclude Help from it too. Page follows the tables: Help requirements and the Bonus Dice table name all three rolls.
2. **Stress triggers beyond the chapter.** `01-core-rules.md` 221-236 lists the core triggers, defers Chapter 7 amounts, and omits two rows. `data/core/stress-changes.yaml` gives the amounts (camp relief 1, Skirmish end 1, Downtime 2) and adds `graduation-exam-ends` (all Stress) and Fear contagion under `named-gain`. Page renders the table, so all of these appear.
3. **Unclear: a Push that "would re-roll no die".** `01-core-rules.md` 197 and `stress-changes.yaml` (cover, `condition`) say such a Push cannot be Covered. An uncovered Push always adds and rolls a new Stress Die (159), so the test only works if it means "no existing base die or Stress Die would be rolled again". Page keeps the source wording ("because every base die and Stress Die already shows 6").
4. **Voice guide conflict.** `site/design/voice-guide.md` asks for "Field report" callouts but bans the word "field". Page text avoids "field": examples use the report component with the default "Example" eyebrow, and quotes cite "Survey Corps manual".
5. **DiceKey retypes a table.** `site/src/components/DiceKey.astro` hardcodes the die-type rows instead of rendering `dice-pool.yaml` `die_types`. The wording agrees with the table today. It also labels Titan Dice "Titan Attack Dice", which `CONTEXT.md` does not use as a term. Not changed in this rewrite.
6. **Glossary pop-up uses an avoid word.** The existing `Titan` entry in `site/src/lib/glossary.ts` calls a Titan "a mindless giant"; `CONTEXT.md` lists "giant" under Avoid. This page does not attach a pop-up to "Titan" for that reason. Not changed in this rewrite.
7. **The reach of Circumstances reads backwards in the table.** `data/core/circumstances.yaml` (`applies_to`) says "Every attribute roll a soldier or a Squadmate makes, except the rolls under never: a called roll, an action from the Action Catalog, a Reaction, and every roll a rule calls for." Read literally, the list after the colon is the exception, which would exempt every roll there is. `never` lists only the Death Roll, the performance roll, and the Graduation Exam's Trials, and `01-core-rules.md` 105 gives the same four kinds as the rolls that *do* take a step. Page follows `never` and the chapter: the list is what Circumstances reach.
8. **The exceptions table cannot show the passive roll's real exceptions.** `dice-pool.yaml` (passive-roll) excludes no pool component, so the "Leaves out" cell reads "Nothing". What actually sets the roll apart is that it is made out of sight, takes only the `circumstances` Bonus Dice source, and gives no Stress Response on a Stress Die showing 1. The page states those in prose under *Passive rolls*; the table shows only the attribute, Help, and Push columns.
9. **A Bonus Dice source whose dice depend on a named step.** `bonus-dice-sources.yaml` (circumstances) gives `dice_per_unit` as a map of step to dice rather than a number, unlike every other source. `site/src/lib/core-tables.ts` renders that case by reading the step names from `circumstances.yaml`, so the cell reads "1 at Easy, 2 at Routine, or 3 at Effortless". A later source shaped this way needs no new code; a source with an unknown step id fails the build.
10. **The failure menu is rendered, not retyped.** The closed menu of what a failed called roll costs is `dice-pool.yaml` (`called_roll`, `failure_menu`). The page renders it as a table through `core-tables.ts`, with player wording keyed by row, so a new cost row fails the build until it has wording. The two lists beside it, `failure_never` and `success_gives`/`success_never`, are plain sentences in the table and are written as prose on the page.

### What this chapter needs from the others

The Rules of Play states these rules and links out for the rest. Nothing below was edited from this page.

- **Compendium, Actions** must mark which entries a called roll can use (the entries whose `rolled` is `when_called`: Spot, Size Up, Survive, Persuade, Endure, Sneak, Recall, Ride, Fly). The page sends readers there for the list rather than naming or counting them.
- **Making Your Soldier** must keep the steps for an act outside the Catalog as the default order outside a fight, say that the list of values they name is an index and not a gate, and say that the steps are not used in a Titan Engagement or a Skirmish.
- **Fighting Titans** and **Skirmishes** must carry the worked examples of improvised acts and model entries this page points at, and Fighting Titans must state that the Circumstances of a fight are Standard unless the GM names a step.
- **Gear & ODM** must let a ruling name a fall (low or high, never extreme, no Height step raising it, inside a fight as outside one), and give the caps on Squad Supply found on a successful called roll.
- **Wounds & Fear** must carry the one Fear Roll trigger the GM reads: outside a Titan Engagement, an event at least as horrifying as a listed trigger.
- **Expeditions & Downtime** must state that a Leg Hazard's or a Night row's own roll takes the step in force when the hazard is rolled, and that no step is named for the danger the row itself describes.

## Resync after the called-roll revision (2026-09-16)

Chapter 2, the character tables, and the Compendium wording were resynced to the revision that makes GM judgment valid (called rolls, Circumstances, improvised acts). Notes from that pass:

1. **The values an improvised action names are no longer a gate.** `action-catalog.yaml` (`tracked_values`) now calls the list an index kept for Talents and for the effects an improvised act can take, not a gate, and the outside-Catalog steps are "the default order" outside a fight. The page keeps the ban on "tracked value", so it says "one of the values the rules name" and then states in its own words that the values are an index of what soldiers change by acting, not a gate on what a player can try.
2. **Circumstances belongs to The Rules of Play.** The ladder, its steps, and the stakes menu are that chapter's. Making Your Soldier states only what Chapter 2 owns (no Circumstances on a Trial roll, no Talent changes a step, a called roll's Circumstances named before the pool) and links to that chapter for the rest. The glossary entries for Circumstances, called roll, passive roll, stakes, and ruling are added with that chapter, so this page uses the terms in plain text and leaves the pop-ups to the Compendium cards, which glossify by themselves.
3. **Relentless on a modelled Nape strike.** `titan-harm.yaml` (`nape_strikes`, `modelled`) spells out that Relentless adds 1 more Opening on at least 1 success. The page says the act is resolved as a Nape strike that falls short, which is the Talent's own trigger, and does not name the Talent again. No disagreement; noted so a reviewer does not flag it missing.
4. **Unclear: Help on a called roll made by a Squadmate.** The Catalog's called-roll Help rows say "up to three comrades who are present and able to help concretely may Help, as the GM rules". Nothing says whether a Squadmate is one of them, as the Requisition rule does for its own Help. The page and the cards keep the source wording ("comrades"), which reads as including Squadmates.
5. **Unclear: a called roll's retry.** `dice-pool.yaml` (`called_roll`, `retries`) covers retries, and in a fight says the act is tried again on a later turn. The Compendium cards say what a called roll needs and who can Help, and leave retries to The Rules of Play, where the retry rule lives.
6. **Called-roll examples in the entry cards are the site's own.** The Catalog gives no example act per entry. Each card's "such as ..." (a rooftop for Fly, a ruined house for Spot, a watch post for Sneak) is written for players from the entry's own meaning, not lifted from a row. Owner to confirm the tone.
7. **The Actions page gained a "Called roll" tag.** `shared-data.ts` reads `rolled` and marks the nine entries; an unknown `rolled` value now fails the build, as an unknown `kind` already did. The Compendium claims file records it.

## The GM's Guide Titan pages (`site/src/content/gm/running-a-titan-fight.mdx`, `titans.mdx`, `tuning-the-fight.mdx`)

1. **The specimen report drops four Behavior Table columns.** `site/src/components/TitanSpecimen.astro` renders a Behavior Table of five columns (d6, behavior, tier, Attack Dice, what happens). Every Behavior Table in `docs/rules/06-standard-titans.md` also carries Targets, Holder's Position, Body Parts used, Fallback, and the Grab mark, and a GM cannot resolve a card without them: the fallback and the move-up both read those cells. The GM's Titans page renders the missing columns beside each dossier, from the Titan's own table through `site/src/lib/gm-tables.ts` (`behaviorRequirements`). The Compendium's squad-facing Titans page and `/gm/titan-dossiers/` still show the short table. Owner to decide whether the Compendium should carry them too, or whether the specimen report should grow the columns and the GM table then goes away.
2. **"Flag" is a banned word, and the Attention rules are built on flags.** `data/engagement/round.yaml` (`gm_tracker`, `focus_titan_row`) tracks "flags this Titan has set, by soldier (hooked-by-strike, just-hurt, loudest)", and `data/titans/index.yaml` (`ladders`) names the rungs that read them. The GM tracker row is worded "What it has noticed", and the three are named in plain words ("hooked into it by a strike", "having just hurt it", "loudest"). Fighting Titans must use the same plain words for the same three things, or the two pages will read as different rules.
3. **The lone-strike and Jam headlines are left off Tuning the Fight.** `docs/rules/05-titan-engagement.md` 1034 (a lone soldier's Nape strike after Break Attention) and 1097 (the Jam test) are headline expectations that cannot be stated without a percentage, which the page's brief excludes. The page carries the Grab's "about 1 in 3, about 2 in 3" because `ADR-0014` states it in words. The Large Titan's "no kill in at most 15% of fights" is on the page as "a real share of fights", and the Large Titan's template row as "far more fights with no kill".
4. **The Abnormal share is computed, not copied.** `docs/rules/05-titan-engagement.md` 155 and `data/titans/index.yaml` both state "1 Titan Engagement in 12". The page derives it from the Medium share of the Size Class roll and the Abnormal share of the second roll, so a change to either row moves the sentence instead of leaving it stale.
5. **Tracker wording is keyed by the opening words of each entry.** `gm-tables.ts` keys the GM tracker's wording map by the line plus the first five words of the table's own text for that entry, so a reworded or added entry loses its wording and fails the build. That is deliberate, and it means a small rewording in `round.yaml` needs a matching key here even when the meaning has not changed.
6. **Round time is a target, not a measurement.** `data/engagement/round.yaml` (`round_time`) states the band as a design target and names the test that will measure it. The page gives the band as plain guidance ("12 to 20 minutes", "at most 30 for two Focus Titans") and says nothing about the test.
7. **The lethality guidance will move.** `data/titans/tuning.yaml` records that the deaths band was re-set twice and is to be re-set again. Tuning the Fight is written in words that survive a re-set ("no single standard fight is expected to kill"), but it should be re-read when that band changes.
8. **What the GM pages need from Fighting Titans.** Nothing on a player page links here. The GM pages link out to `/guide/fighting-titans/` for the card's resolution and the dodge, the Read facts and their prices, Attention and the standard ladder, the Grab, and the two tests that end a fight; and to `/guide/rules-of-play/` for the Circumstances ladder, the called-roll procedure, the closed stake menu, and the full list of what a ruling never touches. Those sections must exist on those two pages.
