## New findings

### M1. Major: the Help source of truth, the glossary, and Chapter 1's Covering rule still give a called roll in a Skirmish the GM-ruled Help that 9-36 replaced

- **Location:** `data/core/bonus-dice-sources.yaml`, the `help` row (lines 14 to 46), keys `condition_outside_titan_engagement` ("On a called roll (Chapter 1, section 1.1), the helper is present and able to help concretely, as the GM rules") and `spends_outside_titan_engagement` ("On a called roll, nothing, unless the GM names before the dice a cost of time or position"); `CONTEXT.md:168`, **Help** ("outside one ... on a called roll the GM rules who is present and able, with Help spending nothing unless the GM names a cost of time or position"); `docs/rules/01-core-rules.md:195`, the *Covering* bullet ("Outside a Titan Engagement, they qualify to Help that roll: under the rule that called for it, or, on a called roll, as the GM rules"). Against decision batch 9, 9-36 item 2, ADR-0024 limit 7 as amended, `dice-pool.yaml` `called_roll.help`, `01:290`, `07:524`, and `skirmish.yaml` `rolls` `called-roll.help`.
- **Problem:** a Skirmish is outside a Titan Engagement, so every sentence written as "outside a Titan Engagement" now reaches it. 9-36 took Help on a called roll in a Skirmish out of the GM's hands and made it the Skirmish's Help: up to 3 comrades taking part who are not Down, each spending their action, with no cost the GM names. Chapter 1 section 1.8 and Chapter 7 say so. The three places above still say the opposite, and one of them is the file `dice-pool.yaml` `called_roll.help` points at for the fight's Help, so the pointer leads from the corrected rule to the uncorrected one. This is also the direction that matters: the stale reading hands the GM a judgment (who may Help, and whether Help costs anything) that a closed rule now names, which is the shape limit 7 exists to prevent.
- **Scenario:** round 2 of a Skirmish against a Military Police patrol in a granary. Private Wendt tries to wedge the loft door shut so the troopers below cannot follow, which no entry of kind `action` models and which changes nothing the Skirmish tracks, so the GM calls a roll. Two comrades want to Help. Chapter 7 says each spends their action, which costs the Squad two attacks this round. The glossary and `bonus-dice-sources.yaml` say the GM rules them present and able and that Help spends nothing, so the same two comrades Help and still Fight. The dice are the same either way (2 Bonus Dice), but the action economy is not: by simulation a need of 1 at Empathy 3 and Stress 1 goes from 74.9% alone to 89.2% with three helpers, and under the stale reading that gain is free.
- **Fix options:**
  1. Give the `help` row the fight's clause, as 9-36 words it in `dice-pool.yaml`: add to `condition_outside_titan_engagement` and `spends_outside_titan_engagement` that in a Skirmish (and in a Titan Engagement) Help on a called roll is the fight's Help, each helper spending their action, with no cost the GM names, and narrow the existing sentences to "outside a Titan Engagement and a Skirmish".
  2. Make the same narrowing in `CONTEXT.md` **Help** and in `01:195`, so Covering a called roll in a Skirmish reads the Skirmish's Help eligibility rather than a ruling.
  3. If the deciders would rather keep one sentence, replace all three with a pointer to `dice-pool.yaml` `called_roll.help`, which already carries every case.

### M2. Major: a called roll in a Skirmish may be named for Fly, which section 7.4 forbids outright

- **Location:** `data/skirmish/skirmish.yaml` `rolls` `called-roll.entry` ("the entry marked when_called that the GM names, or the attribute alone") and `actions.called_roll`; `docs/rules/07-playtest-rules.md:524` and the rendered row at `:709`; `data/character/action-catalog.yaml` `fly` (lines 646 to 658: `kind: roll`, `rolled: when_called`, `context: any`, gear `odm-gear`, "Also made as a called roll outside a Titan Engagement, when the GM calls one for an act this entry fits, such as reaching a rooftop"); against `07:483` ("a Skirmish has no Positions, no Anchor Rating, no ODM move, no Fly roll, and no airborne soldier") and `07:520` ("No entry that is used only in a Titan Engagement can be taken", which does not exclude `fly`, whose context is `any`).
- **Problem:** before 9-36 the Skirmish had no called roll, so `fly`'s "as a called roll outside a Titan Engagement" and the Skirmish's "no Fly roll" never met. They meet now, and they say opposite things about the most characteristic act a Survey Corps soldier can attempt in a town fight. Nothing in Chapter 7 or `skirmish.yaml` narrows the `when_called` set, and nothing in the Catalog excludes a Skirmish. The same question follows the answer: if the roll happens, is the soldier airborne, which `07:483` says a Skirmish has none of, and does Chapter 4's Jam and fall procedure run when the ODM Gear reaches 0.
- **Scenario:** a Skirmish against three Bandits in an abandoned town. Private Brandt says she is taking the ODM Gear up to the hayloft to cut the rope holding the loft beam, which is not a move (she ends Apart from every Bandit either way), models no entry of kind `action` the Skirmish allows, and changes nothing the Skirmish tracks. The GM reaches for the closest `when_called` entry and finds Fly, with the ODM Gear's Gear Dice. Chapter 7 then says a Skirmish has no Fly roll and no airborne soldier. One GM rolls Fly at 2 Gear Dice and wears the harness on a Push; another names Agility alone, which by the same rule takes no Gear Dice at all. The pools differ by 2 dice for the same act.
- **Fix options:**
  1. Say once, in `skirmish.yaml` `actions.called_roll` and at `07:524`, which entries a called roll in a Skirmish may be named for: any entry marked `when_called` except `fly`, since a Skirmish has no airborne soldier, with Agility alone in its place.
  2. Or open it deliberately: add to `07:483` that a called roll the GM names for Fly is the one ODM roll a Skirmish allows, that it makes no soldier airborne, and that it is rolled with the ODM Gear's Gear Dice and wears them like any other roll.
  3. Either way, add the answer to `fly`'s `requirements`, which today says "outside a Titan Engagement" and so reads as permission in a Skirmish.

### M3. Major: a fall staked on a called roll in a fight moves the roller's Position, which the same rule says a called roll never touches

- **Location:** `docs/rules/05-titan-engagement.md:450` ("A fall it stakes is low or high as the GM names, never extreme ... and success gives the act, information, or an object that is not a gear item, never a Position or an effect an entry has"); `docs/rules/01-core-rules.md:38, 41`; `data/core/dice-pool.yaml` `called_roll.in_a_fight` ("would change nothing the fight tracks: no value in `tracked_values`, no Position ...") and `failure_menu.fall`; `data/engagement/positions.yaml` `falls_land` (lines 160 to 169: a soldier who falls from On Body or Blind Spot "holds in-reach relative to that Titan once the fall is resolved"); `docs/rules/04-gear.md:368`; decision batch 9, 9-36 item 3 and 9-38.
- **Problem:** the menu 9-36 admits into a fight includes a fall, and Chapter 4's procedure ends every fall by reading `falls_land`, which moves a soldier at On Body or Blind Spot to In Reach and ends their airborne state. So the one act the rule defines as changing nothing the fight tracks changes the roller's Position, the first tracked value the same rule names, whenever the GM stakes a fall on a soldier who is on the Titan. Nothing says which sentence governs. A GM who reads `falls_land` moves the striker; a GM who reads "no Position" leaves them at Blind Spot holding a fall's damage, which no other fall in the game does.
- **Scenario:** round 3, Anchor Rating Urban, a Large Focus Titan. Private Brandt holds Blind Spot with 4 current Health and is waiting on an Opening. She says she is kicking the church bell off its mount so it drops between the Titan and the wagon of civilians below: no entry of kind `action` models it and it changes nothing the fight tracks, so the GM calls a roll on Strength, names it Hard, states her current Health, and stakes a fall, high. She fails. The high band deals 1, 2, or 3 damage evenly by simulation, which she survives. Then the table has to decide whether she is still at Blind Spot, one Nape strike from a kill, or at In Reach, where the Grab and the Bite entries apply and a move must be spent to climb back. The whole value of the turn turns on a sentence neither chapter has.
- **Fix options:**
  1. Say in `05:450` and in `dice-pool.yaml` `failure_menu.fall` that a fall a called roll stakes is a fall in full: it takes Chapter 4's damage and lands the soldier as `positions.yaml` `falls_land` gives, and that the "nothing the fight tracks" limit binds the act, the time or position cost, and what success gives, not the fall's own procedure.
  2. Or close the case at the stake: the GM does not stake a fall on a soldier at On Body or Blind Spot in a Titan Engagement, since only a rule that names a fall reaches those Positions; a called roll there stakes Stress, damage, a lost item, or time and position instead.
  3. Whichever is taken, add one line to `01:38`, the **A fall** bullet, so a GM staking a fall in a fight reads the answer where the menu is.
