# Wings of Freedom Phase 1 playtest packet: review, round 2 (Codex)

## Verdict

| Severity | Count |
|---|---:|
| Critical | 2 |
| Major | 1 |
| Minor | 1 |

The packet is complete enough for its first playtest. A group can create soldiers, complete an interim day, set up and run a Titan Engagement against each of the four Titans, retreat, resolve the ending and aftermath, and record feedback without another document. The full player and GM procedures are faithful. All four open rules findings are confined to the quick reference or one reserved Talent description.

The quick reference must be corrected before publication. Its Jam and Dry summaries contradict the grounded-Titan strike exception, its fall summary can raise a horse fall that must stay low, and its retreat summary names only one of the two retreat triggers.

## Scope and checks

I reviewed the 2,893-line packet against Chapters 1 to 6, their rendered tables, the `data/` YAML, `CONTEXT.md`, the decisions through item 5-19, the owner's Structure and Voice requirements, and every finding in round 1. I did not read or run the simulator, its reports, or the parallel round 2 review.

- Sampled every one of the packet's 55 rules and reference tables against its chapter and YAML source. The four Behavior Tables match row for row. The only table-content drift is Minor 1 below. The other 15 tables are printable sheets, tracking forms, or feedback layout.
- Walked soldier creation and graduation, the interim day and interim issue, engagement setup, the round, Attention evaluation, Break Attention and the Feint, Body Part and Nape strikes, a Grab through rescue or death, Critical Injuries and Down, retreat, leaving, the engagement ending, and aftermath.
- Walked each standard Titan and the Sprinting Abnormal from its public stat block through every Behavior Table row. Toughness, Nape Depth, Tempo, Severity, Reactions, Grab use, Regeneration, and the Abnormal's hidden values agree with the sources.
- Confirmed that player material precedes the quick reference and GM material; glossary and feedback follow the GM material. No Abnormal hidden value appears in the player section.
- Checked all 98 in-page links and found no missing or duplicate target. All 70 tables are inside horizontally scrolling wrappers. The phone-width rule stacks stat blocks at 480 px. Print rules insert breaks before the quick reference, GM band, Titans, glossary, and feedback; the first Titan correctly shares the GM opening page.
- Found no em dash or developer vocabulary named by the brief. Rules use second person for players, address the GM as the GM, and use the capitalised terms in `CONTEXT.md` consistently apart from Minor 1.

No odds question arose from an open finding, so no dice simulation was needed.

## Changes beyond round 1

### Removed timing fallback paragraph: supported

Chapter 5's fallback list is a test and revision protocol, not a rule the GM applies during play. Removing it from the rules section satisfies the Voice requirement without changing the round-time target or the feedback test. The current packet still states both timing targets and asks the group to record the required measurements.

### Titan pages labelled GM section: supported

Chapter 6 makes standard stat blocks, all Behavior Tables, and the Sprinting Abnormal's Size Class, Tempo, Behavior Table, ladder, public Body Part States, and counts public. Only the Abnormal's Toughness, Nape Depth, Regeneration length, and ladder are hidden until Read. The packet follows that division: the tables live in the GM section for running convenience and the GM must show public tables on request; the Hidden values page alone is marked GM only. “GM section” is more accurate than “GM only” on the Titan pages.

### Fifteen glossary entries moved under Terms for later rules: supported

The entries remain defined, and the heading describes where their full procedures belong, not whether their names can appear in this packet. Moving them separates usable Phase 1 vocabulary from terms retained for later rules without changing a definition.

## Findings, most severe first

### Critical 1. The quick reference contradicts the grounded-Titan strike exception and omits the ordinary Body Part strike requirement

**Location.** Quick reference, lines 2147, 2149, and 2194 to 2195.

**Problem.** The Nape-strike line correctly says working ODM Gear is required unless the Titan is grounded. The Gear lines then state without exception that Jammed or dry gear allows no Nape strike. That forbids a strike the source explicitly permits against a grounded Titan. In the other direction, the Body Part line never says that a strike from On Body or Blind Spot needs working ODM Gear unless the Titan is grounded, so the quick reference appears to permit such strikes against a standing Titan with Jammed or dry gear. Chapter 5 sections 5.7 and 5.9 and `titan-harm.yaml` apply the same grounded exception to Nape and Body Part strikes.

**Scenario.** A soldier at Blind Spot has dry, Jammed ODM Gear beside a grounded Medium Titan. The Nape line permits the strike, but the Gear lines forbid it. If that Titan stands, the Body Part line still appears to permit an eyes strike, although the source now forbids it.

**Fix options.**

1. Change the Gear summaries to “no ODM moves; no Nape strike or close Body Part strike unless the Titan is grounded”, and add the working-ODM requirement to the Body Part line.
2. Alternatively, remove strike permissions from the Gear summaries and point to the two complete strike lines, provided the Body Part line gains its missing condition.

### Critical 2. The quick reference can raise a horse fall that must always stay low

**Location.** Quick reference, line 2203.

**Problem.** “Horse or Distant or In Reach low” is followed by an unqualified sentence that Giant Forest or a Large Titan raises the band. Chapter 4 section 4.6 and `falls.yaml` say a fall from a horse is low and no later step changes it; only a fall that is not from a horse can be raised.

**Scenario.** A rider's horse goes lame in Giant Forest beside a Large Titan. The quick reference directs the group to raise the low fall to high and roll D6 +2. The source requires a low fall at D6 +0.

**Fix option.** Write: “A fall from a horse is always low. Otherwise, Distant or In Reach is low; On Body or Blind Spot is high. Giant Forest or a Large reference Titan raises a non-horse fall one step, to extreme at most.”

### Major 1. The quick reference omits the Background-clock retreat trigger

**Location.** Quick reference, line 2205.

**Problem.** The summary says a retreat begins when the retreat clock fills. Chapter 5 section 5.10 and `background-titans.yaml` give two triggers: the retreat clock fills, or a Background Titan's clock fills while two Focus Titans are alive. The full packet states both, but the one-page procedure drops the second condition at the point most likely to be used during play.

**Scenario.** Two Focus Titans are alive when a flare fills a Background Titan's last clock segment in round 3. A GM running from the quick reference continues the engagement until round 8 instead of starting the retreat immediately.

**Fix option.** Change the opening to: “Retreat when the retreat clock fills, or when a Background Titan's clock fills while two Focus Titans are alive.”

### Minor 1. Hand-to-Hand changes blades to Blade Sets

**Location.** Dice Talents table, line 664.

**Problem.** The packet says Hand-to-Hand covers fighting “with Blade Sets or bare hands.” Chapter 2 line 763 and `talents.yaml` say “with blades or bare hands.” Blade Set is a specific Gear item; blades is the source's broader ordinary word. The Talent's actions are reserved in Phase 1, so the mismatch does not affect this playtest, but it changes the table row and could narrow the Talent when those actions become active.

**Scenario.** A later Fight rule allows a knife or sword that is not a Blade Set. The source Talent applies; the packet description says it does not.

**Fix option.** Restore “with blades or bare hands.” If Blade Set is the intended restriction, change the chapter and YAML through the rules process first.
