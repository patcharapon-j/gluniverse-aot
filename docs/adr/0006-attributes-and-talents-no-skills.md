# Attributes and Talents, no skill list

Characters roll an attribute plus one Talent, following Coriolis: The Great Dark, rather than Alien's attribute plus skill, even though the push and stress model comes from Alien. An untrained soldier still rolls their full attribute, so anyone can attempt any job in the chaos of a Titan Engagement, and narrow Talents carry the specialization. Because deciding which Talent applies could become GM judgment, every Talent names the actions it applies to from the Action Catalog (see ADR-0003). A roll may use at most one Talent that adds dice, while Talents that bend a rule stack whenever their trigger occurs. The six attributes are Strength, Agility, Wits, Perception, Instinct, and Empathy, rated 2 to 5, with the Specialty's key attribute able to reach 6. Talents run 0 to 3 (2 at most at creation), Gear Dice 1 to 3 from one gear item per roll, and Bonus Dice from Help, Openings, and situational advantages add at most 4, so pools stay in a range the push decision still matters for.

## Considered Options

- Alien's 4 attributes and 12 skills: more legible at a glance and closer to Training Corps rankings, but broad skills flatten specialization and Alien's career-locked talents shut characters out of roles.
