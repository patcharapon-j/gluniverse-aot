# Attributes and Talents, no skill list

Characters roll an attribute plus one Talent, following Coriolis: The Great Dark, rather than Alien's attribute plus skill, even though the push and stress model comes from Alien. An untrained soldier still rolls their full attribute, so anyone can attempt any job in the chaos of a Titan Engagement, and narrow Talents carry the specialization. Because deciding which Talent applies could become GM judgment, every Talent names the actions it applies to from the Action Catalog (see ADR-0024, limits 9 and 12). A roll may use at most one Talent that adds dice, while Talents that bend a rule stack whenever their trigger occurs. The six attributes are Strength, Agility, Wits, Perception, Instinct, and Empathy, rated 2 to 5, with the Specialty's key attribute able to reach 6. Talents run 0 to 3 (2 at most at creation), Gear Dice 1 to 3 from one gear item per roll, and Bonus Dice from Help, Openings, and situational advantages add at most 4, so pools stay in a range the push decision still matters for.

## Considered Options

- Alien's 4 attributes and 12 skills: more legible at a glance and closer to Training Corps rankings, but broad skills flatten specialization and Alien's career-locked talents shut characters out of roles.

## Amended

After decision batch 9 (2026-09-16, GM judgment; ADR-0024): the sentence "Because deciding which Talent applies could become GM judgment, every Talent names the actions it applies to" keeps its rule and changes its reason. GM judgment is now valid (ADR-0024), and a Talent still names the Catalog entries it applies to so that it stays legible, checkable, and computable in the Foundry system: for a called roll the GM names the entry, and the Talent follows (9-12). Menace's condition reads "on a roll made as a threat" and Stand Down's "against a person" (9-13). The Bonus Dice cap of 4 stands, and the GM's Circumstances are a source under it: Easy, Routine, and Effortless add 1, 2, or 3 Bonus Dice from one source, `circumstances`, so the GM's generosity cannot stack past teamwork's ceiling; Hard, Harsh, and Desperate are penalties after the cap, base dice only, never below 1 base die (9-2). No Talent changes, ignores, or lowers a Circumstances step (ADR-0016, Talent guardrail 10). The record's citation of ADR-0003 reads ADR-0024, limits 9 and 12, as 9-18 gives (the GM judgment review round 3, 2026-09-16).
