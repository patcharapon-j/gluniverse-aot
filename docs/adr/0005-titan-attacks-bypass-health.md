# Titan attacks bypass Health

Humans track a small Health pool plus Critical Injuries rolled by Injury Location, and Titan attacks skip Health entirely and inflict Critical Injuries directly. This keeps Titan World's body-part injuries with only one tracked number, and stops a soldier from shrugging off a Titan's swat because they happen to have Health left.

## Considered Options

- Titan World's per-location wound states with no pool: rejected, too much bookkeeping per hit.
- Alien's Health pool with a generic critical injury table: rejected, loses the body-part identity the injuries need.

## Amended

After the owner decision on Health boxes (2026-09-14): Health is half of Strength plus Agility, rounded up, kept as a row of boxes. Each untreated Critical Injury crosses off one box and treating it gives the box back; Titan attacks still inflict Critical Injuries directly and cause no Health loss of their own. Damage marks the boxes left. A soldier whose current Health is 0, from crossed-off boxes, damage, or both, is Down, which replaces the earlier rule that three untreated Critical Injuries put a soldier Down; a row that says Down still does. Damage that brings current Health to 0 still inflicts one immediate Critical Injury; a Critical Injury that crosses off the last box does not. A day passing restores Health lost to damage, not crossed-off boxes.

After decision batch 7 (2026-09-15, the playtest packet feedback round 1; ADR-0017): the Injury Location is sided (left arm, right arm, left leg, right leg, torso, head), so worsening counts by location and side, and every Critical Injury carries an Injury Type named by the rule that inflicts it; a Titan's Bite is Bite and every other Titan behavior, the Grab's crush, and every fall is Crush. Damage also carries the Injury Type of its source, which the Critical Injury it inflicts at 0 Health records; human weapons deal typed damage and never a Critical Injury directly (ADR-0018). A Titan attack is the harm a Behavior Table entry inflicts, or a Critical Injury that a hazard row of the Expedition rules names as a Titan's (the Straggler row, Chapter 7): it lands with no Reaction, as ADR-0015 says, and it is the first Titan harm not dealt by a Behavior Table entry.

After decision batch 8 (2026-09-15, the playtest packet feedback round 1; ADR-0019): a Titan attack is rolled and the Reaction cancels, and this decision is kept: a landed Titan attack still inflicts its Critical Injury directly and causes no Health loss of its own. One sentence is added: the Critical Injury roll adds 1 for every net success beyond the first, with the cap row and the instant-death rows still applying, and a Grab's crush, a knock loose, and a Stress gain take no rider (8-2). The batch 7 paragraph's "it lands with no Reaction, as ADR-0015 says" reads "it lands with no Reaction and rolls no Attack Dice, since it is not a card; its own test is the Endure roll". Two more Titan harms outside the Behavior Tables join the Straggler: steam, at a kill or a Regeneration fill, is damage of Injury Type Burn and reaches the tables only at 0 Health, and corpse heat, on a soldier Pinned under a dead Titan, is a Burn Critical Injury each turn (8-7, 8-9). The founding reason stands: a soldier still cannot shrug off a Titan's swat because they have Health left, and steam is damage precisely because a Burn Critical Injury at every kill would break the Critical Injuries band.

After decision batch 9 (2026-09-16, GM judgment; ADR-0024): a ruling may harm a soldier only as the stakes of a called roll, named before the roll, and only as damage of 1 to 3 of a named Injury Type or as a fall by Chapter 4's procedure with the band the GM names (9-5). Damage a ruling names reaches the Critical Injury tables only at 0 Health, as all human harm does; a ruling never inflicts a Critical Injury directly, never picks a location, a row, or a time limit, and never touches a Death Roll, a Health box, or healing time. Titan attacks alone bypass Health, as this decision says, and the bell curve ADR-0017 rests on is untouched.

After decision batch 9, item 9-30 (2026-09-16, the GM judgment review round 1): a fall a ruling names is low or high, never extreme, so no harm a ruling names reaches 4 damage; the extreme band stays for the falls other rules and the height steps name. Whether damage a ruling names may bring a wounded soldier to 0 Health, with the immediate Critical Injury this decision's Health box paragraph gives, was put to the owner (OQ-177) and answered as item 9-35: decided (c), the Health box paragraph applies. The rule stands, so that damage makes the soldier Down with an immediate Critical Injury as all damage does, and when a called roll's stakes name damage or a fall the GM states the soldier's current Health with them, so the table sees the risk before the dice. The playtest tags every Critical Injury that damage a ruling named caused, and OQ-177 is read again at the retune.
