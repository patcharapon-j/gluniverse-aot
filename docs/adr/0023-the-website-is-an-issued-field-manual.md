# The website is an issued field manual

The site's look is the Survey Corps Field Manual, picked by the owner from three concepts (Field Manual, Beyond the Walls, Recruitment Poster); the concept page is kept at `site/design/concept-field-manual.html` as the reference. The rulebook is presented as an in-world object: aged paper and fibre grain, iron-gall ink, Survey Corps green and wax-seal red, stencil display type, typewriter labels and stamps, drafted diagrams with leader lines, and the Wings of Freedom emblem as a pressed seal. The tagline is "Ride out together. Come back if you can."

- **Theme:** paper only, one committed look; colour tokens stay structured so a later night mode needs no redesign.
- **Type:** Big Shoulders Stencil for display, Spectral for body text and all stat numbers (tabular figures), Courier Prime only for stamps, labels, and field reports. Self-hosted.
- **Reading pages:** opener pages carry full grain, stamps, and a colour plate; rules pages use lighter grain, a column of about 68 characters, and margin notes for examples, cross-references, and glossary pop-ups. Stamps and marginalia are signposts, used sparingly.
- **Illustrations:** full-colour plates mounted with a paper border and a plate caption for the hero, chapter openers, and Titan portraits; ink-wash vignettes for small scenes inside rules pages. Both are the same semi-realistic anime style (ADR-0022).
- **Hero:** three.js dust drifting through a beam of raking light, with the Wings emblem pressed into the cover and lit as the pointer moves; the still hero under reduced motion.
- **Dice tray:** a wooden field box lined with Survey Corps green cloth. Base dice are bone with inked pips, Gear Dice blued gunmetal, Stress Dice wax-seal red lacquer, Titan Attack Dice raw flesh. The Wings emblem replaces the 6, and the 5 and 6 on Titan dice. Kinds differ in material and value, not colour alone.
- **GM's Guide:** the Commander's copy. The same manual under a red "RESTRICTED: COMMANDING OFFICERS" band, blue carbon-copy typewriter text, manila folder tabs, and redaction bars over hidden Titan values that lift on hover.
- **Game icons:** stamp style, ink on paper like a rubber stamp or woodcut, one ink plus one accent, with a fixed frame per family: round for dice, shield for Talents, diamond for Titan tiers, square for gear.
- **Interface icons:** Phosphor, whose thin line sits beside the engraved diagrams.

## Considered Options

- Beyond the Walls, a dark cinematic site with a three.js Titan in fog: not picked.
- Recruitment Poster, constructivist red, black, and cream: not picked.
- A 3D manual opening on a field desk as the hero: rejected as heavy and a gimmick after the first visit.
- A lantern night mode in v1: deferred.
- Full-colour art everywhere, or ink wash everywhere: rejected for the mix, colour where it should hit and ink where the page must stay calm.
- Font Awesome Free: rejected; its solid glyphs read modern against engraved line work.
