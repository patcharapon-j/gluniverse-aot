# A Push costs both Stress and gear

Pushing follows one fixed order:

1. Roll. If any Stress Die shows a 1, the soldier suffers a Stress Response and cannot Push.
2. Otherwise they may Push: gain 1 Stress, add the new Stress Die, and re-roll base and Stress Dice that did not show a 6.
3. 1s on Gear Dice stay locked, and each wears that gear down.
4. After the Push, any 1 on a Stress Die causes one Stress Response. A roll never causes more than one.

Stress Dice add successes while risking a Stress Response (the Alien RPG Evolved model, including its ban on pushing after a Stress-die 1), and gear wears through player choice (the Coriolis: The Great Dark model, kept only on Gear Dice where a locked 1 carries a cost). Fear Rolls are triggered by events, not by the dice. We merged the two because AoT's central trade is spending both nerve and equipment.

## Amended

After the final design review (2026-09-14), replaced "all 1s stay locked" with the ordered procedure above. Locked base-die 1s cost nothing, and pushing after a Stress-die 1 was undefined.
