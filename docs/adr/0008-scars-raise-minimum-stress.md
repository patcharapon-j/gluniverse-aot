# Scars raise minimum Stress and Resolve

Each Scar permanently raises a soldier's minimum Stress and their Resolve by 1, so veterans always roll Stress Dice but resist Stress Responses better. A soldier with five Scars must retire, becoming an NPC instructor or Squad contact. In the field, Stress drops by 1 when a Titan Engagement ends and by 1 on a Nape kill.

Because Stress Dice add successes as well as risk, veterans become both more capable and more likely to break, like Levi or Erwin. The final design review showed that raising minimum Stress alone slides veterans past the point of best performance (around 3 Stress) into a spiral of failures, so Resolve rises with it and retirement gives long campaigns an ending. Scars that were purely narrative were rejected because trauma would then have no weight at the table.
