# Critical Injuries carry a sided Injury Location and an Injury Type

A Critical Injury lands at one of six Injury Locations, left arm, right arm, left leg, right leg, torso, or head, and carries one of five Injury Types: Crush, Bite, Burn, Cut, or Pierce. The location die keeps every weight it had (1 left arm, 2 right arm, 3 left leg, 4 right leg, 5 torso, 6 head), and worsening counts the Critical Injuries held or permanently marked at the same Injury Location and side. Every rule that inflicts a Critical Injury or damage names its Injury Type: a Titan's Bite is Bite, every other Titan behavior, the Grab's crush, and every fall is Crush, a blade is Cut, a shot is Pierce, and Burn waits for its sources. The tuned rows of each location table are shared by every type; each row carries one name per type, and a short rider block per type changes only the fields the fiction demands (a Bite's lethal limb rows bleed on a turn limit). Blast is a delivery, not a type: when it arrives it rolls a Burn and a Crush at two rolled locations. The physical tables carry no mental rows; lasting harm to the mind lives in Stress Responses, Fear Rolls, Scars, and Grief. Recorded after the owner's playtest packet feedback (2026-09-15, decision batch 7, 7-4 to 7-6).

This keeps ADR-0005's body-part identity, which four unsided locations underdelivered (the same arm could be lost twice, and a Swat could gash a forearm), without giving up the 2D6 bell curve that every lethality figure rests on. Losing a limb is graded, not a ban: one arm or one leg makes the soldier's work harder, and both is severe, with the exact effects in `data/harm/critical-injuries.yaml`.

## Considered Options

- Full tables per type and location (about 128 tuned rows, 16 tables): rejected before the playtest, because every row is a simulator input and the first playtest should run on the tuned rows; a Phase 2 candidate if Cut, Pierce, and Burn earn real sources.
- A D66 table per type with the tens die as location: rejected, because it throws away the 2D6 curve, makes instant death reachable on a first roll, and needs about 144 tuned entries.
- Location tables without sides, as OQ-41 decided: superseded, because untracked sides let the same limb be lost twice and hid the six-part body the owner asked for; the `repeat_row` device stays only for a second roll at a side already lost.
- "Damage Type" as the term: rejected, because damage is already the harm kind that marks Health boxes.
- Bludgeoning: rejected in favor of Crush, because Titans crush, pin, swat, and trample.
- Mental Critical Injuries on the physical tables: rejected by the owner, because Scars, Fear Rolls, Stress Responses, and Grief already cover lasting harm to the mind.

## Amended

After decision batch 8 (2026-09-15, the playtest packet feedback round 1): "Burn waits for its sources" is withdrawn; the owner reversed batch 7's deferral, and Burn ships in the first playtest (8-7, 8-8; OQ-138 decided). Burn's sources are steam at a kill and at a Regeneration fill (damage, On Body and Blind Spot for the kill, On Body for the fill), corpse heat on a soldier Pinned under a dead Titan (a Burn Critical Injury each turn) and 1 damage per Heave against a corpse, a Bandit's firebrand, and a flare fired at a person; every one is data-shaped with no Reaction and no GM choice, and steam is damage so that a kill does not add a Critical Injury to the band. The Burn rider on every table: healing doubled, lethal rows on a day limit, Treat Injury only with a medical kit or medical supplies; Cut and Pierce carry no rider. The falling Titan is Crush: a soldier in the path who fails to Leap Clear is Pinned with a Crush Critical Injury that cannot be lethal (8-9). Limb loss gains its alternative to medical Retirement: a Prosthetic (an era item obtained by Requisition at Limited Scarcity or, in Phase 2, a Discovery) lowers the lost-limb grade one step per prosthetic, never past the one-limb grade for two, and never removes the 13+ rows' own permanent penalties, which stack per side (8-10, 8-11). The rider on the Critical Injury roll for net successes is ADR-0019's and ADR-0005's, not a type rider.

After decision batch 8, item 8-15 (2026-09-15): "Cut and Pierce carry no rider" is withdrawn. The owner overrode that default, and both types carry a rider in the Bite rider's shape (`type_riders` per table, rows plus the fields set), on all four tables. The Cut rider: every lethal row whose limit is engagement has a turn limit for a Cut, so a cut bleeds and the Death Roll comes at the end of each of the soldier's turns until it is treated; a Skirmish's turns count for turn limits, as its end step already turns them into engagement limits. The Pierce rider: on every row from total 7 up, Treat Injury on the Critical Injury takes a penalty of 1 base die and the Critical Injury does not heal while untreated, so a lodged ball is dug out or stays; the graze and clip rows carry no rider, and there is no second infection clock, which is Burn's. Neither type has a Titan source (Cut and Pierce arrive only as damage at 0 Health from a Foe's blade or shot), so no Titan figure moves; the Skirmish probe reports the push, which is upward: a lethal Cut rolls each turn instead of once, and a lethal Pierce is a little harder to save in the aftermath.
