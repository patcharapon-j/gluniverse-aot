# Content beyond canon follows the charter

The game may add gear, Titans, and Talents that the show never depicts, because a story needs few props and a game needs decisions. Every addition passes the charter below, so that it still reads as Attack on Titan in 845 to 850 and still fits the system the simulator has tuned. Recorded after the owner's playtest packet feedback (2026-09-15, decision batch 7, 7-3 and 7-10).

## The setting

1. **Era and technology.** Paradis, 845 to 850. Black powder, steam, iron and steel, horses and wagons, signal flares, cannon, ODM Gear. No internal combustion, no radio, no Marleyan technology, nothing from after the Campaign Year range. A Garrison, Military Police, or Survey Corps workshop must plausibly be able to make it. There is no Marley module, and the charter leaves no door for one.
2. **Dated inventions are Canon Clock content.** Thunder Spears, anti-personnel ODM, the special target restraining weapon, and any other canon invention with a date are Discoveries gated by the Canon Clock or bought with Research Points, each with a Divergence Hook. Never Standard Issue.
3. **Invented items enter play only through Requisition or a Discovery.** Standard Issue holds only what the show issues.
4. **Titans die only at the Nape** (ADR-0007), for every item and every Titan. New gear may Break Body Parts, create Openings, change Attention, or spend a Titan's card. Cannon wound; blades kill.
5. **Shifters stay Shifters.** Hardening, intelligence, and targeting riders from the air are Shifter traits (ADR-0002, ADR-0013). An Abnormal may be strange; it may not be smart. The Sprinting Abnormal's rewrite is the precedent.
6. **Every row has a rule that reads it** (ADR-0024 limits 9 and 12, ADR-0012). An item names the Catalog entry it rates or the tracked value it changes; a Titan entry uses the closed effect list and the closed ladder tests, or a Chapter 5 decision adds to those lists first. No flavor-only rows on a live sheet.
7. **Titan-fight content is measured before it ships** (ADR-0014). Any item or Titan that a target, band, or bar reads runs the simulator and is judged on its readings. Expedition, Requisition, and Downtime content needs no rerun.
8. **Access is Funding and Scarcity, not coin.** Standard Issue by Funding, Requisition against Scarcity (Standard, Limited, Rare). No price list. Branch variants are the same items at a different rating or with one named quirk, never new mechanics.
9. **Small tables, reviewed like chapters.** Each new category is 5 to 8 rows and each new Titan is one section, through the same draft, review, fix cycle. Growth by batches, not by a catalogue.
10. **Glossary language.** New names follow `CONTEXT.md` and its Avoid lists.

**Order of work.** Titans before gear in Phase 2, because the Behavior Table format, the Abnormal bar, and the simulator are ready for them and every Titan Engagement reads a Titan. Nothing new in gear or Titans before the first playtest, with one exception the owner accepted: the flintlock pistol, the musket, and the shot kind of Squad Supply, which the Skirmish rules need (decision batch 7, 7-17).

## Talents

Talents may go beyond canon, provided they describe how a soldier trained rather than what superhuman thing they do. The test is whether a Talent could be a paragraph in a Training Corps manual. Guardrails:

1. A Talent names live Action Catalog entries and grants no permission (ADR-0006); it never lets a soldier do something others cannot attempt.
2. No supernatural or Titan-derived effect (ADR-0002). Ackerman-grade ability is a 6 and a Talent at 3, never a Talent with "innate" in its name.
3. No attribute raise (ADR-0011), and no Talent raises Health or Resolve. A Talent may count Resolve as higher for one named roll, as Iron Nerve does.
4. Every Talent that touches a roll a target measures, or a fixed roll (the Gas Roll, the Fear Roll, the Stress Response roll, the Death Roll), gets a sensitivity row in the simulator's report before it ships, or is listed as unmeasured and marked as a playtest rule.
5. Period technology only (item 1 above). A Talent tied to a Discovery's gear waits for that gear's row.
6. Rule Talents over dice Talents, because every live rolled entry already has a dice Talent; conditional dice Talents (the Horsemanship pattern, dice only while mounted) are the exception.
7. Teamwork levers first (ADR-0010): Openings, Help, Covering, Wings, Attention, Squad Tactics. Solo power is what ADR-0014's solo targets guard.
8. Once per Titan Engagement is the default limit for anything that changes a cost or a roll count; once per Leg, per night camp, per Skirmish, or per Downtime for the procedures Chapter 7 adds.
9. Names in the setting's register: plain soldier's English, with the Avoid lists respected.

A Talent that names only a Leg, camp, Requisition, or Skirmish roll carries no dice in any ADR-0014 baseline (ADR-0014 as amended in batch 7) and needs no sensitivity row unless it also names a Titan Engagement roll.

## Considered Options

- No charter, expansion ad hoc: rejected, because it is how an item list gets a science-fiction flavor and a Titan roster gets a Shifter in disguise, which the Sprinting Abnormal's first draft did.
- A charter that leaves a door for a later Marley module: deferred by the owner ("no Marley for now"); the era lock is written without the door, and a later decision may open one.
- Coin prices, as Coriolis: The Great Dark uses: rejected, because Funding and Scarcity are the glossary's acquisition terms and a price list would fight them.
- Talent guardrails as a Chapter 2 design note only: rejected, because future drafters need one test to cite.

## Amended

After decision batch 9 (2026-09-16, GM judgment; ADR-0024, 9-13 and 9-16): items 6 and 7 gate published data. A table's own item, Titan, or Foe kind is played as unmeasured and marked so: a GM may place a charter-compliant item on the Requisition list at a Scarcity the era rules allow (an item that rates a measured roll at least Limited), may build a Foe kind on the Foe row fields with Attack Dice and Guard no higher than the Military Police trooper's 7 and 4, and, in Phase 2, may build an Abnormal in the Chapter 5 format with the closed lists. None of these ships in the first playtest. Talent guardrail 10: no Talent changes, ignores, or lowers a Circumstances step; a Talent that helps against bad conditions adds dice, because a step is worth 20% of failure at every pool and a Talent that erased one would outrank every other.
