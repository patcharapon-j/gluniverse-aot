# Titan attacks are rolled, and the Reaction cancels

A Behavior Table entry that harms or terrorizes carries Attack Dice. When the entry resolves, the GM rolls them in the open as Titan Dice, which succeed on 5 or 6, never Push, and take no other dice; the successes are the card's Severity. Each success of the target's Reaction cancels one, and the card lands on 1 or more net successes. A roll of 0 successes whiffs against everyone, a soldier who cannot react included; against such a soldier the card lands on 1 or more Titan successes. A landed Critical Injury adds 1 to its roll for every net success beyond the first, with the cap row and the instant-death rows still applying; a Grab, a knock loose, and a Stress gain take no rider, and Critical Injuries stay direct (ADR-0005). Human attacks share the shape: Block or Dodge cancels one for one, and damage is the weapon's base plus 1 per net success beyond the first (ADR-0018). Chapter 1 defines the procedure once, with the acting side's roll final before the reacting side rolls and the reacting side Pushing last. Recorded after the owner's playtest packet feedback (2026-09-15, decision batch 8, 8-1 to 8-6, OQ-145).

Kept from ADR-0015, which this record supersedes in full: one Reaction covers every card from that Titan in the round, its successes cancelling against each later card separately; Titans cannot be blocked; the dodge takes its Gear Dice from ODM Gear, or from the horse while mounted, one item per roll; a Grab counts down in the victim's turns, lifted after their next turn and devoured after the one after, and the devour always lands; while holding someone the grabbing hand has Toughness 1; the Grab's crushing Critical Injury is Crush at the torso and cannot be lethal. New here beside the roll: the whiff, the rider, no Push for Titans, and the roll made in the open when the card comes up, so the GM chooses nothing (ADR-0024, limit 8).

Attack Dice are keyed to the former Severity values, 3 dice per point (3, 6, 9, 12), which the simulator measured to reproduce every ADR-0014 target at today's Health with the narrowest spread per hit of any rolled shape; the counts are tuned against the reference Rookie's dodge as Severity was, and the ADR-0014 targets are unchanged. Titan harm outside a Behavior Table entry, the Straggler's Bite, steam, corpse heat, and a falling Titan, rolls no Attack Dice: each has its own test or none (decision batch 8, 8-7 and 8-9).

## Considered Options

- Fixed Severity that the Reaction must meet, with no Titan roll (ADR-0015): superseded. Its founding sentence, that rolled attacks roughly doubled lethality, paraphrased a review that compared the rolled shape with the target, not with a tuned fixed-Severity baseline; measured against today's rules, cancellation at Alien-range dice is the gentler shape, and matched counts reproduce every target.
- Cancellation with tiers (a glancing net 1, as written at net 2): rejected, because it halves deaths at equal dice, needs about 22 kill dice to match today, and adds a third outcome per card.
- Damage and a crit threshold into Health, as Coriolis: The Great Dark resolves creature attacks: rejected, because on this engine lethality lives in the Critical Injury tables and only the landing rate reaches them; every setting at small pools fell far short of today, at today's Health it overshot and needed Health 6 to come back, and it reversed ADR-0005 for no measured gain.
- Titan dice succeeding on 6 at 6, 12, 18, and 24: rejected in favor of the 5-or-6 die at half the counts, which has the same mean, a narrower spread, and pools inside Alien's range.
- A rider on the Grab's crush: rejected, because it breaks the six Grab cells and the lone Grab band.
- A devour that can whiff: rejected; the countdown's two turns are the rescue window by design.
- No Push on the Reaction: rejected, because the Push is the player's one lever and the source of the Jam test's meaning.
