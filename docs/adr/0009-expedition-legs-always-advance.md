# Expedition Legs always advance

An Expedition is a pointcrawl of Legs between Waypoints, and every Leg reaches the next Waypoint even on a failed roll. Failure instead costs double Squad Supply and worsens the hazard, which grows harsher with distance from the Walls and with days outside (+1 per night camp). Adapted from Coriolis: The Great Dark's delve procedure, this models a formation that cannot stop mid-ride and avoids hexcrawl bookkeeping.

The guarantee covers navigation, not survival: a Squad that retreats from a Chase or Titan Engagement falls back to the previous Waypoint. Legs are never ridden at night, and night camps use calmer hazard results, except against Abnormals.

## Amended

After the final design review (2026-09-14): failed Legs no longer cost gas (horses carry the Squad; gas drains only in Titan Engagements and Chases), and retreat and night rules were added.

After decision batch 7 (2026-09-15, the playtest packet feedback round 1): implemented as the playtest minimum in Chapter 7. "Arrives faster" for a Hard Ride reads as a third Leg squeezed in before dark; "worsens the hazard" is +1 on the hazard roll, and a success beyond the first lowers it by 1; "+1 per night camp" is the nights-outside modifier; "night camps use calmer hazard results, except against Abnormals" reads as a separate Night table whose only Titan is an Abnormal. A retreat from a Titan Engagement falls back to the previous Waypoint, and the Leg is ridden again. Nothing in the decision changes.

After decision batch 8, items 8-35 and 8-37 (2026-09-15, the round 1 final review): the guarantee holds when no soldier can roll. A Leg roll or camp roll that no soldier on the Expedition can make, because every living soldier is Down, fails, and the Squad still reaches the Waypoint at a failed Leg's cost (8-35). A retreat from a Titan Engagement the Night table begins falls back that night to the Waypoint the day's last Leg started from, without riding a Leg: no Leg roll, spend, or Leg hazard. The camp goes on there with no new camp roll, ration, or night hazard, and the next day's first Leg rides that Leg again, counted among that day's Legs (8-27 (b)). This is the batch 7 paragraph's fall back and ride again, applied at night, and "Legs are never ridden at night" stands (8-37).

After decision batch 9 (2026-09-16, GM judgment; ADR-0024, 9-15): the GM as Command authors the Mission Brief (route, Waypoints, Posts, Pace, one complication) and may frame a Waypoint scene between Legs, run on called rolls with the closed menu of costs, in which a Skirmish or a Titan Engagement may begin by the fiction. The guarantee, the Leg roll, the hazard and Night tables and their modifiers, "always advance", rations, and the Straggler's random victim are unchanged and applied as written: a rolled hazard row is described, never softened or swapped, and authored dangers belong to Waypoint scenes, never in place of a roll.

After decision batch 9, item 9-37 (2026-09-16, the GM judgment review round 2; OQ-179): the batch 9 paragraph's "their modifiers" reads the modifiers' amounts and their application, which the route the Mission Brief sets, the night camps made, the Leg roll's outcome, and the Signal Relay's flare decide and no ruling adds, removes, or changes; the route's own Distance Bands, Formation Posts, and Hard Ride days are Command's, set once before the Expedition and read by the hazard roll as written, so the Brief and the closed modifiers are one number authored once and read once. A Brief's route keeps within the interim route's envelope in the first playtest: 4 to 6 Legs, no Leg deeper than the interim route gives its place (the first and last Legs Near, the second and second-to-last Near or Far, every Leg between them Near, Far, or Deep), and at most one Depot, never the gate, so that no authored Expedition plays above the distribution the hazard tables ship for and the attrition this record rests on (canisters, Blade Sets, and Squad Supply return only through a Depot's full Standard Issue) survives an authored route. Formation Posts and Hard Ride days are bounded by the day rules and the fiction, and the playtest log tags every route a Brief set. The guarantee, the Leg roll, the tables, "always advance", rations, and the Straggler's random victim are unchanged. Phase 2 may widen the bounds (OQ-141).

Amended by ADR-0029 (decision batch 16, 2026-09-21; zone combat): a Waypoint's kind now names the **field rating** of a Titan Engagement on the Leg that leads to it, the rating the field is generated from, rather than one Anchor Rating for the whole fight (16-6). A retreat from a Titan Engagement ends when no standing soldier holds a Position, as before; under zones a standing soldier leaves by stepping off field from an edge zone, and in a retreat may do so from any edge zone, whatever Titan stands in it, so every retreat still ends (16-26). The guarantee, the fall back to the previous Waypoint, and every night rule are unchanged.
