# Expedition Legs always advance

An Expedition is a pointcrawl of Legs between Waypoints, and every Leg reaches the next Waypoint even on a failed roll. Failure instead costs double Squad Supply and worsens the hazard, which grows harsher with distance from the Walls and with days outside (+1 per night camp). Adapted from Coriolis: The Great Dark's delve procedure, this models a formation that cannot stop mid-ride and avoids hexcrawl bookkeeping.

The guarantee covers navigation, not survival: a Squad that retreats from a Chase or Titan Engagement falls back to the previous Waypoint. Legs are never ridden at night, and night camps use calmer hazard results, except against Abnormals.

## Amended

After the final design review (2026-09-14): failed Legs no longer cost gas (horses carry the Squad; gas drains only in Titan Engagements and Chases), and retreat and night rules were added.

After decision batch 7 (2026-09-15, the playtest packet feedback round 1): implemented as the playtest minimum in Chapter 7. "Arrives faster" for a Hard Ride reads as a third Leg squeezed in before dark; "worsens the hazard" is +1 on the hazard roll, and a success beyond the first lowers it by 1; "+1 per night camp" is the nights-outside modifier; "night camps use calmer hazard results, except against Abnormals" reads as a separate Night table whose only Titan is an Abnormal. A retreat from a Titan Engagement falls back to the previous Waypoint, and the Leg is ridden again. Nothing in the decision changes.

After decision batch 8, items 8-35 and 8-37 (2026-09-15, the round 1 final review): the guarantee holds when no soldier can roll. A Leg roll or camp roll that no soldier on the Expedition can make, because every living soldier is Down, fails, and the Squad still reaches the Waypoint at a failed Leg's cost (8-35). A retreat from a Titan Engagement the Night table begins falls back that night to the Waypoint the day's last Leg started from, without riding a Leg: no Leg roll, spend, or Leg hazard. The camp goes on there with no new camp roll, ration, or night hazard, and the next day's first Leg rides that Leg again, counted among that day's Legs (8-27 (b)). This is the batch 7 paragraph's fall back and ride again, applied at night, and "Legs are never ridden at night" stands (8-37).
