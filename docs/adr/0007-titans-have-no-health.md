# Titans have no Health

A Titan cannot be worn down. Strikes against a Body Part accumulate successes toward its Toughness, moving it through Intact, Wounded, and Broken, and Regeneration undoes that damage on a clock. Only a single Nape strike that reaches the Nape Depth kills. A Nape strike that falls short turns its successes into Openings for the next striker, and a Broken leg drops the Titan to the ground, giving +2 dice on Nape strikes.

This makes setup the reliable path to a kill while keeping the Nape the one clean cut from canon, and it preserves the moment where one soldier's failed cut lets a comrade finish the Titan.

## Considered Options

- A Health pool with an armored Nape: rejected, chip damage contradicts canon.
- Nape cuts that accumulate across strikes: rejected in the final design review, because it made piling onto the Nape strictly better than setting up.
