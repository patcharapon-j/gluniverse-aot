# The GM rules where the rules are silent; procedures stay data

The GM's judgment is valid and is part of the rules. In the owner's words, GM judgment "is what TTRPG is and what makes it flexible and creative and makes TTRPG special, which is having the GM help adjudicate things. We can trust the GM." When a soldier attempts something no rule covers, the GM calls a roll or says it happens or cannot be done; before the dice are rolled the GM names the roll's Catalog entry, its Circumstances on a seven-step ladder (Effortless +3 to Desperate -3, in dice, never in successes needed), and what failure costs from a closed menu; the GM runs people with reasons; and the GM frames scenes as Command would. Every procedure is still written as tables, tracks, counters, and explicit triggers in YAML (ADR-0012), so that the Foundry VTT system is a translation and the paper game plays in full: a ruling is a prompt at the table, not a missing rule, and it needs no row. The line this record draws is that **the GM rules before the dice, never after them**: a rolled die, a table row, a Titan's card, and a Foe's open dice stand as they fell. Recorded after the owner's decisions of 2026-09-16 (`docs/research/gm-judgment/OWNER-DECISIONS.md`; decision batch 9, 9-1 to 9-20). Supersedes ADR-0003 in full.

## Limits

These keep ADR-0003's numbering so that every citation of "ADR-0003, item n" reads "ADR-0024, limit n". Items marked *kept* are ADR-0003's words; the others are rewritten here.

1. Fear Roll triggers are a closed list. The list has one row the GM reads, `gm-horror`: outside a Titan Engagement, an event at least as horrifying as a listed trigger, for the witnesses the GM names. Inside a Titan Engagement the closed list governs and no ruling calls a Fear Roll (9-6).
2. *Kept.* A flag on a Focus Titan, such as Draw Attention's, lasts until the end of the Titan's next card that resolves a behavior; a card that resolves nothing leaves it standing. A card whose Attack Dice whiff has still resolved a behavior (ADR-0019).
3. *Kept.* Every Drive is written as a trigger ("When I ..."). A called roll counts as the soldier's own act for a Drive's act test (9-6).
4. *Kept.* Preparation Points have a price list.
5. *Kept.* Every Mission Brief and Operation Frame lists its Commendation triggers.
6. *Kept.* Research Point awards come from a table.
7. *Kept.* Help states its Position requirement. On a called roll outside a Titan Engagement and a Skirmish, Help is as the GM rules: up to 3 comrades present and able (9-12). On a called roll inside a Titan Engagement or a Skirmish, Help is the fight's Help, each helper spending their action, and the GM names no cost for it (9-36).
8. The rulebook forbids the GM from overriding table rolls. A Titan's Attack Dice are rolled in the open and never re-rolled or adjusted (ADR-0019); so are a Foe's Attack Dice and Guard, the hazard and Night tables, and the setup table. Behavior Tables, the Attention Ladder, the Reaction procedure, and every lethality table are applied as written. Foes act by the foe rule unless the GM directs one with a reason said aloud (ADR-0018 as amended; 9-7).
9. An act no rule covers is a called roll: the GM names the closest Catalog entry marked `when_called`, or the attribute alone, and the Circumstances and the stakes, before the pool is built (9-1). In a Titan Engagement or a Skirmish an improvised act uses one existing entry as its model and produces only that entry's effect at its size, never a kill (9-8). In a fight Chapter 2's steps for acts outside the Catalog are not used: an act that is not a Catalog entry taken as written is an improvised act even when a tracked value it would change matches an entry; its model entry is an entry of kind `action` the soldier could take in that fight, never a Reaction, a roll, a fixed roll, or an option; and only a Nape strike taken as its entry is written kills a Titan (9-27). A called roll can be made in a Titan Engagement or a Skirmish only for an act that no entry of kind `action` in that fight could model and that would change nothing the fight tracks (no tracked value, Position, Attention, Opening, card, clock, or Foe value); the model entry is tried first, the called roll spends the action, it never gives an effect an entry has, and it is tried again on a later turn (9-36).
10. *Kept.* Faction Standing changes and Mission Brief complications come from tables. A table is the default where the GM frames nothing (9-15).
11. *Kept.* Every Behavior Table entry lists its Position requirement, Body Parts used, Attack Dice (none on a telegraph-only entry), and fallback, every harming effect lists its Injury Type (ADR-0017), and every table has a Thrash entry.
12. The tracked values are an index of what soldiers change by acting, kept for Talents, the Foundry system, and the in-fight effect menu; every rule that creates a clock, counter, or obstacle still adds its value with the entries that change it. The index is no longer a gate: an act that matches no value is ruled on, not inert (9-12).
13. *Kept.* A state or result that forbids Pushing, Helping, Covering, or Reactions names what it forbids on its own row. The core eligibility rules carry one shared hook for state and result bans; core requirements, roll-specific bans, and Squadmate restrictions still apply.

Four limits are new:

14. **Results stand.** Once the dice are rolled, the GM never overrides, re-rolls, or ignores a die or a table result, and never changes the stakes named before the roll. Judgment acts before the roll: whether to roll, what it needs from the fiction, how the circumstances tilt the pool, what failure costs. The only soldier's roll made out of sight is the passive roll, for noticing outside a fight; it is not a called roll and has no stakes (9-10, 9-32). The Next Behavior is the Titan's one hidden roll, rolled once, and it stands.
15. **Needs come from the fiction.** A roll needs 1 success unless its rule names a quantity in the world or a tuned value (Nape Depth, Toughness, a Foe's Parley value and Watch, Scarcity and the ledger, a Titan's or Foe's successes). The GM never raises or lowers a need; Circumstances change dice, never needs (9-2, 9-3).
16. **Circumstances reach the soldiers' pools only.** A plus step is a Bonus Dice source under the cap of 4; a minus step is a penalty after the cap, base dice only, never below 1 base die, never Gear or Stress Dice. In a Titan Engagement the core assumption is Standard, and the GM names another step only for something no rule already prices; the Titan's dice, cards, Attention, and tables take none (9-4). A Reaction takes the step in force when the attacker's card comes up, and no step is named, raised, or lowered for a Reaction once that card has come up (9-25). Each roll has one step: when a further condition touches a roll under a lasting step, the GM weighs both into one step for that roll, before its pool is built or its card comes up, and steps never add (9-26).
17. **Failure costs what was staked**, from the closed menu: Stress 1, a fall (low or high, never extreme, 9-30, inside a Titan Engagement as outside one, with no Height step raising it, 9-36, and landing as the fall rule gives, a change of Position the fall rule makes and not the ruling, 9-44), damage of 1 to 3 typed, a lost item or unit, or time and position; never a direct Critical Injury, a Death Roll, a Scar, Grief, Stress relief, or healing (9-5). A cost the GM names for Help or for a retry is time or position only, named before the dice and falling when taken on (9-28). In a fight, a time or position cost, and what success gives, touch nothing the fight tracks: never a Position, a card, a turn, a move, an action beyond the one the act spent, Attention, an Opening, or a clock (9-36). Success gives the act, information, a position, an object that is not a gear item, or found Squad Supply capped by the roll and by the place, and never a gear item, Stress relief, healing, a Commendation, a Research Point, or a Faction Standing change (9-29, 9-31).

## Considered Options

- Amend ADR-0003 in place: rejected, because its founding sentence ("GM discretion may appear as flavor but never as the rule itself") is the thing reversed, and thirteen items under a preamble that contradicts them would mislead every drafter who reads the top of the file.
- Difficulty through successes needed (1, 2, or 3), as Tales from the Loop: rejected by the owner for dice. A raised need is harsher than any dice step at every real pool, sets a different difficulty for every soldier without the GM knowing it, erases luck at small pools, consumes the extra successes eight rules spend, and roughly doubles the Push rate the Stress and gas economy is tuned on (`docs/research/gm-judgment/difficulty-mechanics.md`, sections 2.3 to 2.5).
- A short ladder (+1 to -3): rejected by the owner for Coriolis's seven steps. Routine and Effortless mostly serve a soldier who carries penalties.
- Overflow (a penalty past the floor raises the need) or negative dice (Mutant: Year Zero): rejected, because each turns dice back into needs on exactly the weakest rolls, and negative dice add a fourth die colour.
- The GM may adjust or ignore a rolled result when the story calls for it (Coriolis: The Great Dark, p. 250): rejected. Titan dread and the players' trust rest on open dice that stand.
- Rulings everywhere except the Titan Engagement (the ripple audit's recommendation): rejected by the owner, who opened the Titan Engagement to difficulty. This record reads that opening as the soldiers' own pools, Standard by default, and keeps the Titan's side closed (9-4).
- Circumstances in a Titan Engagement only after simulator sensitivity rows: deferred to the target B retune (OQ-140, OQ-167); the playtest logs every non-Standard step instead.
