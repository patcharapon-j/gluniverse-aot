# Prior Art Survey: Attack on Titan TTRPG on the Year Zero Engine

Research date: 2026-09-14. Facts are from the linked sources. Where a source was thin or sources disagreed, that is flagged with **[unverified]** or **[conflict]**. This is not legal advice.

---

## 0. Headline findings

1. **An official, licensed Attack on Titan TTRPG on the Year Zero Engine is in development.** Need Games! (publisher of Fabula Ultima) and Panini announced it at Gen Con 2025. Kodansha owns the IP. It is set among the Survey Corps in the five years between the Colossal Titan's appearances. The target was 2026, hopefully by Gen Con 2026. My searches found no confirmed release as of Sept 2026. It is a direct competitor, and it means the AoT IP is actively licensed.
2. **The YZE Free Tabletop License (FTL) v1.1 (31 Mar 2026)** is royalty-free, perpetual, and irrevocable while you comply. It allows commercial or free games, including VTT modules. It covers **only the SRD mechanics text**. It grants nothing for Free League brands or art, and obviously nothing for Attack on Titan. v1.1 adds a **generative-AI disclosure** clause.
3. **The SRD already contains most of the chassis we need:** supply rolls, ammo dice, degrading gear bonus, initiative cards, a stress-dice push option with a sample panic table, conditions, armor and cover degradation, and travel tasks (keep watch, forage, hunt, make camp). It does **not** contain monster attack tables, hit locations, step-down resource dice, Ark/HQ development, or promotion points. Those exist only in individual Free League books, so we would write our own versions.

## A. Existing Attack on Titan tabletop games and hacks

### A1. Official: Attack on Titan TTRPG (Need Games! / Panini, Year Zero Engine)
- **Announced:** Gen Con 2025 (Italian press dated 1 Aug 2025; Need Games X post). The TTRPG Insider summary rendered this as "August 2024". That is **[conflict]**, and the Italian press and X post support 2025.
- **Designer:** Matteo Pedroni (per TTRPG Insider interview).
- **System:** Year Zero Engine. Chosen to deliver "terror and helplessness", with comparisons to *Alien RPG*.
- **Setting and roles:** average Survey Corps members, not the manga protagonists, in the five years between the Colossal Titan's appearances.
- **Mechanics hinted:** Titans are treated as "forces of nature" rather than standard enemies. They regenerate unless killed "through specific steps". Lethality is high and players swap in new characters on death. There are "specific mechanics" for spotlighting a character's death. No ODM, gas, or blade details have been published.
- **Business:** no crowdfunding. Kodansha approval loops slowed development. Target was 2026, hoped for Gen Con 2026, with no committed date. No release confirmation found in Sept 2026 searches.
- Sources: https://www.ttrpginsider.news/p/attack-on-titan-ttrpg · https://x.com/NeedGamesRPG/status/1950970664342659376 · https://www.nerdevil.it/2025/08/01/attack-on-titan-gioco-di-ruolo-annuncio/ · https://www.senzalinea.it/giornale/lattacco-dei-giganti-il-gioco-di-ruolo/ · https://www.akibagamers.it/05-08-2025/attack-on-titan-annunciato-un-gioco-di-ruolo-da-tavolo/

### A2. Titan World (fan hack of Apocalypse World, PbtA)
- **Author:** "InfiniteJesters" per RPG Writeups. It was distributed via Tumblr and /tg/ PDF in the 2013–2015 era **[exact date unverified]**.
- **Core roll:** 2d6 + stat. 10+ is a full success, 7–9 a partial, 6- a GM move.
- **Stats:** Agility (maneuvering and reaching attack positions), Execution (killing blows), Awareness (tactics), Discipline (holding the team together, sanity), Rage (hatred of titans).
- **ODM movement:** abstracted into the Agility move **Get Into Position**, which builds **Advantage** for a follow-up. Avoid Harm is also Agility.
- **Titan fights:** a two-step **setup then kill** structure. Get Into Position builds Advantage, then **Strike to Kill** (Execution) targets the nape. Titans regenerate everything except a nape hit, though lots of smaller trauma can still kill. Non-nape hits slow titans to set up strikes. Failure means gear damage, injury, or death. Titan variants: abnormal movement, oversized, fast regeneration, hardened skin, intelligent. Other moves: Escape Death (Rage, break a titan's grip) and Desperate Rescue (Discipline, with brutal costs on failure).
- **Gas and blades:** binary persistent tags, **"Low on Gas"** and **"Low on Blades"**, each a disadvantage. No granular tracking.
- **Playbooks:** Rookie (no special moves), Natural (Agility), Warrior (ignores setup requirements), Tactician, Leader, Shifter (titan form, high risk and high reward).
- **Praise:** very rules-light, "a really good way to introduce people to ApocWorld games", RP-focused ("no need for counters").
- **Criticism:** no art, very no-frills, "XCOM-level casualty rates". Early play is win or die en masse, with no harm clocks.
- Sources: https://writeups.letsyouandhimfight.com/infinitejesters/titan-world/ · https://app.roll20.net/forum/permalink/3778631/ · https://i.4pcdn.org/tg/1427990201632.pdf (403 on fetch)

### A3. Attack on Titan: Unauthorized and Unofficial (Platinum RPG / Strife)
- Free itch.io sourcebook for Platinum RPG and *Strife: The Scalable Wargame*, with conversion notes for d20, BRP, and Palladium.
- The creator notes that "it was pretty difficult to get the scaling correct". Human-vs-15m-titan scale is the recurring design problem.
- ODM, gas, and blade specifics are not visible on the store page.
- Source: https://sts-gamer.itch.io/attack-on-titan

### A4. Attack on Titan: The Last Stand (Cryptozoic board game, 1 vs. many)
- **Structure:** one player is the Titan and up to four are Heroes. A 3D titan standee has slide-in platforms creating **eight vertical levels** (0 is ground, 8 is near the head), plus a tower with plastic cannons. Levels 3 and 5 are structures where heroes can fire cannons for extra damage.
- **ODM equivalent:** heroes roll action dice with rerolls, spending results to move between levels, attack, fire cannons, and dodge. **Titan-head results rolled by heroes are claimed by the Titan player** and fuel titan powers (heal, attack, eat citizens). This is a push-your-luck economy.
- **Titan fight:** heroes can attack the titan directly only from levels on the titan. Victory requires completing public **Tactics cards**, which need specific levels and dice faces. The Titan plays two action cards each round, one face-up and one face-down. Attack cards target specific levels and require dodge results. Event cards cause bad effects such as eating a citizen or smashing a cannon.
- **Gas and blades:** no gas or blade resource tracking described in the reviews.
- **Praise:** looks impressive, solid hero decisions, a unique 1-vs-all experience, a good amount of content.
- **Criticism:** the Titan player "feels more like a facilitator". Balance problems: a tactic card forces the Titan to play both cards face-up for the rest of the game, and a hero power denies the Titan its head results. Component quality is "not up to scratch". House rules are needed.
- Sources: https://www.meeplemountain.com/reviews/attack-on-titan-the-last-stand/ · https://zatu.com/attack-on-titan-review/ · https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3267766/attack-on-titan-the-last-stand-review-by-board-gam · https://fathergeek.com/attack-on-titan-the-last-stand/

### A5. Attack on Titan Deck-Building Game (Cryptozoic, Cerberus Engine; designer Matt Hyra)
- **Co-op, 1–5 players (original).** Heroes start with 7 "Courage" (+1 Power) and 3 "Thrust" (Move 2). **Location matters.** You buy cards inside the walls and fight titans outside.
- **ODM equivalent:** Thrust and move cards. **Maneuver** cards give big power boosts when you share a space with a titan.
- **Titan fights:** normal titans are defeated by paying their Power cost. **Archenemy Titans** have hit points, a Power cost, and card draws that can cancel abilities. Titans attack walls at the end of the round: five walls, two health tiles each. Heroes can absorb damage as **Wound** cards, and extra wounds on a wounded hero kill them.
- **Gas and blades:** abstracted into Equipment cards. No tracked consumables.
- **Praise:** easy to learn, fast, strong thematic split between inside and outside the walls.
- **Criticism:** "dead hands" from too few movement cards (the mobility bottleneck), not enough "epic" moments, over two hours long, and Archenemy Titan 2 is overpowered early. GeekDad summed it up as "fun until you die a horrible, painful, frustrating death". Helplessness felt frustrating rather than dramatic.
- **New edition:** *Attack on Titan Deck-Building Game: Season 1*, released 14 Apr 2026, 1–4 players, about 60 minutes, "updated mechanics and quicker gameplay".
- Sources: https://coopboardgames.com/cooperative-board-game-reviews/attack-on-titan-deck-building-game-review/ · https://geekdad.com/2016/12/attack-on-titan-game/ · https://cryptozoic.com/products/attack-on-titan-deck-building-game-season-1

### A6. Not found
- No substantive d20/5e, Fate, Savage Worlds, Genesys, or fan YZE AoT hacks surfaced in searches. Scattered forum and Facebook threads exist without usable rules. "The Titan Attacks!" (5e, Rook and Role) is an unrelated war-machine adventure. "Titan Effect" (YZE, Knight Errant Media) is an unrelated super-soldier IP.

## B. Year Zero Engine mechanics relevant to AoT

### B0. What the YZE SRD v1.0 itself contains (licensed text)
Chapters: Introduction, Player Characters, Skills & Specialties, Combat & Damage, Magic, Travel. It includes **both dice-pool and step-dice** variants.
- **Push options (pick one per game):** (a) Damage & Stress: 1s on base dice deal damage or stress, optionally to your tool instead. (b) **Stress dice:** each push adds 1 stress, you roll extra d6 equal to stress, 6s help, and any 1 triggers a panic roll. (c) **Conditions:** take a condition per push. (d) **Doom Points:** the GM gains a point per push to spend on misfortunes.
- **Sample panic table** (D6 + stress): 7 Nervous Twitch, 8 Tremble, 9 Drop Item, 10 Freeze, 11 Seek Cover, 12 Scream (others roll panic), 13 Flee, 14 Berserk, 15+ Catatonic.
- **Consumables:** either track them exactly, or use **Supply rolls**. Roll d6 equal to your Supply rating (max 6), and each 1 lowers the rating by 1. At 0 you are out.
- **Gear bonus / reliability** degrades when pushing. At 0 the item is broken and must be repaired with Crafting. **Artifact dice** also appear.
- **Ammo dice:** add d6 up to RoF, or magazine rounds minus 1. Only base dice decide the hit. Each 6 on an ammo die adds +1 damage or an extra hit on the same or a second target in the zone. Ammo spent = sum of ammo dice + 1.
- **Initiative cards 1–10,** drawn once per combat. You may **exchange initiative** with a character who hasn't acted, and they must act immediately. Surprise lets you pick your card. Specialty *Fast Reflexes* draws two cards. NPC groups share a card. Hidden initiative is a variant.
- **Actions:** one slow + one fast, or two fast. Helping costs an action of the same type.
- **Stressful situations table** (1–3 stress). You roll Insight to reduce stress.
- **Armor and cover:** armor rolls dice to cancel damage, and banes degrade the armor rating. Cover works like armor against ranged attacks (Stone Wall 8/A).
- **Conditions variant:** physical (Exhausted, Battered, Wounded) and mental (Angry, Scared, Disheartened). Broken at a set number of conditions. Only critical injuries kill.
- **Travel:** hex maps (10 km), shifts, tasks (marching, keeping watch, foraging, hunting, fishing, making camp, cooking, resting, sleeping, driving, gathering), forced march, and mishap tables.
- **Also included:** vehicles, chases, and magic mishaps.
- **Not in the SRD** (grep verified): monster attack tables, hit locations, step-down resource dice, base/HQ development, promotion points.
- Source: https://freeleaguepublishing.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/YZE-Standard-Reference-Document.pdf

### B1. Twilight: 2000 4e (step-dice YZE)
- **Step dice:** ratings A–D map to d12/d10/d8/d6. You roll attribute + skill die. 6+ is a success, and 10+ faces count as two successes. Modifiers step dice up or down, keeping the two dice close in size.
- **Push:** re-roll dice that are neither successes nor 1s. 1s cause stress, damage, or gear damage.
- **Ammo dice:** d6s up to rate of fire. Hit is determined by base dice only. Ammo-die 6s add damage or hit a second target. In the preview example, **rounds spent = sum of ammo dice** ("6 and 3 … remove 9 bullets"). Two or more 1s on ammo dice cause a **jam**.
- **Hit locations:** a separate **d6 Hit Location Die**, with head and torso 4 in 6 per commentary. Aimed attacks let you pick the location at a penalty. Critical results can reduce armor at that location by 1 until repaired.
- **Suppression:** ranged hits, autofire, hits on your cover, and explosives force a **Coolness Under Fire** (CUF) roll. On failure you fall prone, lose both actions next turn, and take stress. A **Unit Morale** die is added when near teammates.
- **Initiative:** cards, with one fast and one slow action (Dice Adventurer preview). The widely quoted "CUF roll → fast/slow phase" is a **De Fenestra house rule**, not RAW.
- **Praise:** suppression tied to autofire and cover hits "done so well", and quick turns.
- Sources: https://diceandink.com/a-twilight-2000-4th-edition-story/tw2k-dice-mechanics/ · https://diceadventurer.wordpress.com/2020/12/01/twilight-2000-preview-3-combat/ · https://libellus.de-fenestra.com/?p=268 · https://www.blackgate.com/2021/02/01/twilight-2000-roleplaying-in-a-post-nuclear-holocaust-world/ · https://rpggeek.com/thread/3257068/lets-read-twilight-2000/page/5

### B2. Forbidden Lands
- **Resource dice:** Food, Water, Arrows, and Torches each have a die (d12/d10/d8/d6). Roll when consumed. On a 1–2 the die steps down, and a 1–2 on a d6 means you are out.
- **Journeys:** quarter-days (morning, day, evening, night). Each PC picks one activity: hike, **lead the way**, **keep watch**, forage, hunt, fish, make camp, rest, sleep, explore. One pathfinder rolls Survival per hex. Successes mainly avoid mishaps, so every PC has a travel role.
- **Monsters:** each has a **d6 random attack table** (area, single, double, fear, magical). The GM rolls instead of picking. Fear attacks hit Wits, and 0 Wits means a horror critical. Lore checks can reveal vulnerabilities (Book of Beasts).
- **Strongholds:** you build functions with Crafting (bakery, mill, guard tower, moat, library, shrine). Hirelings include a Master Builder (auto-succeeds) and Guards. **Reputation** rises from great or terrible deeds and **attracts unwelcome visitors** to the stronghold.
- **Willpower Points:** gained from pushing, spent on powers (the SRD includes this).
- **Criticism:** Book of Beasts attack tables feel repetitive ("area, single, fear, double, magical").
- Sources: https://www.strangeassembly.com/2019/review-forbidden-lands · https://hws3.wordpress.com/2024/01/13/review-forbidden-lands/ · https://forum.rpg.net/threads/wir-forbidden-lands.853129/page-2 · https://forbiddenlandsopentable.obsidianportal.com/wikis/the-stronghold · https://www.enworld.org/threads/forbidden-lands-book-of-beasts-a-played-it-review.698383/

### B3. Dragonbane (Free League, own license, not YZE)
- **Monsters never roll to hit.** The GM rolls on the monster's attack table and the attack auto-hits. PCs choose to **dodge or parry at the cost of their own turn**, or tank it. Monster attacks can be dodged but not parried. **Fear attacks** force an immediate WIL roll (pushable, not an action) or you suffer a condition, lose WP, or are paralyzed.
- Sources: https://whatdoiknowjr.com/2024/03/08/what-do-i-know-about-gameplay-dragonbane/ · https://www.threads.com/@theaarpgs/post/C09gUDgr9Tf

### B4. Mutant: Year Zero
- **Ark:** four Development Levels (Food Supply, Culture, Technology, Warfare), roughly 0–40, with 12 points to start. Each session the group picks a **Project** (windmill, militia, library, government). Projects need **Work Points** generated by the two listed skills and scaled to party size. Warfare helps defend against attacks. Threats to the Ark come from GM tables.
- **Rot:** most Zone sectors give 1 Rot point per day. When you gain Rot, roll base dice equal to total Rot, and 1s become damage. Some Rot becomes **permanent**. The Stalker can scout a sector's Rot level and back out.
- **Mutation Points:** gained by pushing (1s on attribute dice) and spent to trigger mutations. **Misfires** are temporary or permanent penalties plus new mutations.
- **Criticism:** the party balance depends on having a Stalker, and some roles (Fixer, Boss, Slave) are thin.
- Sources: https://www.strangeassembly.com/2019/review-mutant-year-zero · https://www.blackgate.com/2020/11/09/mutants-and-the-end-of-days-mutant-year-zero/ · https://ludovic.chabant.com/blog/2024/11/23/some-notes-on-mutant-year-zero/

### B5. Blade Runner RPG
- **Step dice A–D.** Won two Gold ENNIEs in 2023.
- **Promotion Points** are earned by doing the job the LAPD way (retiring replicants, closing cases) and spent on specialties and police connections. **Humanity Points** are earned by humane acts, even against orders, and per reviews spent on skills **[spend details unverified]**. The two are **deliberately in tension**.
- **Investigation:** time runs in **Shifts** of about 6 hours, with one location per shift. A **Countdown** scaled to player count triggers escalating events, so the case resolves even if players stall. Combat can kill in one shot.
- **Criticism:** chase rules one-sided and confusing with multiple pursuers.
- Sources: https://www.cgmagonline.com/review/tabletop/blade-runner-rpg-review/ · https://gnomestew.com/blade-runner-the-roleplaying-game-review/ · https://fisherinthevoid.com/blade-runner-rpg-investigating-humanity/ · https://forum.frialigan.se/viewtopic.php?t=12989

### B6. Vaesen
- **Conditions instead of HP:** physical Exhausted, Battered, Wounded and mental Angry, Frightened, Hopeless. Each gives −1 die to that domain. A 4th condition on a track means **Broken** plus a critical injury, which may be fatal. **Pushing costs a condition.**
- **Headquarters:** Castle Gyllencreutz in Upsala. After each mystery the Society earns **Development Points**, spent on **Facilities** (occult library, botanical garden), **Contacts** (banker, constable, psychiatrist), and **Personnel** (caretaker, chef, mystic). **Each purchase triggers a hidden GM roll for a new threat** in or around the castle, so upgrading generates adventure.
- Sources: https://morganhua.blogspot.com/2023/06/vaesen-nordic-horror-roleplaying-review.html · https://frank-mitchell.com/posts/vaesen-preview/ · https://freeleaguepublishing.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Vaesen-Character-Sheet-2505.pdf

### B7. Tales from the Loop / Things from the Flood
- **Loop:** kids **cannot die**. Conditions (Upset, Scared, Exhausted, Injured) each give −1 die, and **Broken** takes you out of the current Trouble. Pride and Anchors provide support.
- **Flood:** teens **can** die. Becoming Broken gives a permanent **Scar**, which can be invoked once as a bonus. **From the second Scar on, each new Scar risks removal from play** (death, or being taken away), and more Scars mean higher risk. Shame replaces Pride.
- Sources: https://www.rpg.net/reviews/archive/17/17582.phtml · https://www.tabletopgaming.co.uk/reviews/things-from-the-flood-rpg-review/ · https://www.strangeassembly.com/2019/review-things-from-the-flood

### B8. Alien RPG (YZE, most tonally relevant)
- **Stress dice:** 6s count as successes, but **any 1 means a panic roll** (D6 + stress). At high stress this cascades through the party. This is the SRD's "stress dice" push option.
- **Signature attacks:** xenomorphs don't use normal attacks. The GM rolls a **d6 signature attack table** whose results can disarm, force panic, or nearly kill outright.
- Sources: https://www.rpg.net/reviews/archive/18/18421.phtml · https://www.starshipsandsteel.com/2019/11/alien-rpg-by-free-league.html

## C. Giant monsters as environments and multi-part bosses

### C1. Heart: The City Beneath (Rowan, Rook & Decard)
- **Delves are opposed NPCs with Resistance** (a terrain "HP pool"). Delve rolls inflict stress on the delve: d4 without gear, d6–d8 with good gear, up to d12 with boons. Resting and side trips add banes. When Resistance hits 0 you arrive at the Landmark.
- **Five stress domains:** Blood, Mind, Echo, Fortune, Supplies. When you take stress the GM rolls d12. If the roll is ≤ your total stress you suffer **Fallout**: minor if the roll is ≤6, **major** if 7+ (which clears stress). Two minors combine into a major, and two majors into a critical (death or removal).
- **Havens** heal and restock, capped by the Haunt's die size. **Connections** stabilize a route permanently, making later trips safer.
- **Setup then execution:** the route itself is a multi-step obstacle, and preparation (gear die size, boons) directly speeds it up.
- Sources: https://writeups.letsyouandhimfight.com/lazyangel/heart-the-city-beneath/ · https://thedicepool.com/2024/07/15/heart-the-city-beneath/

### C2. Blades in the Dark (One Seven Design)
- **Planning is abstract.** Pick a plan type (Assault, Deception, Stealth, Occult, Social, Transport) and provide one detail (point of attack, route).
- **Engagement roll:** 1d plus 1d per major advantage, minus 1d per major disadvantage. 1–3 desperate, 4–5 risky, 6 controlled, critical skips the first obstacle.
- **Flashbacks** handle setup mid-score. They cost 0 stress (ordinary), 1 (complex), or 2+ (elaborate) and cannot undo established fiction.
- **Load:** pick light, normal, or heavy and declare specific items later.
- This is the canonical **"skip the planning, resolve prep at the moment of need"** pattern.
- Sources: https://bladesinthedark.com/planning-engagement · https://komodogames.substack.com/p/steal-this-mechanic-engagement-rolls

### C3. Lancer (Massif Press)
- Default NPCs have 1 Structure and 1 Stress. **Elite, Veteran, and Ultra templates** add Structure and Stress. Such NPCs **roll on the Structure Damage and Overheating tables like PCs**, so each lost structure point produces a table result.
- **Ultra** is the solo-boss template. The multi-bar HP with a table result at each threshold works as a phase change.
- Community supplements (NPCs Rebaked, Maximum Threat) exist because Ultra blanket immunities can frustrate players.
- Sources: https://lancer-faq.netlify.app/ · https://kaitave.itch.io/lancer-npcs-rebaked · https://foundryvtt.com/packages/ilysen-lancer-npc-rebake-structure

### C4. Monster Hunter adaptations
- **Monster Hunter World: The Board Game (Steamforged):** monsters act from **Behaviour cards whose backs telegraph the attacking body part**, giving players readable intent. **Breaking body parts** reduces specific attacks (for example, breaking a tail weakens a Thunder attack, and breaking a stomach stops healing) and yields crafting materials. A Gathering phase before the hunt ramps tension. The main criticism is complexity.
- **GM Binder "Monster Hunter" 5e ruleset:** zones have a durability threshold. At 0 the part is broken, disabling abilities (head: breath or roar; wings: flight; tail: tail attacks) and dropping carves.
- Sources: https://www.gamesradar.com/tabletop-gaming/monster-hunter-world-the-board-game-wildspire-waste-review/ · https://www.boardgamequest.com/monster-hunter-world-the-board-game-review/ · https://www.gmbinder.com/share/-NRFvAgf4zwQ0r7UgNrg

### C5. Shadow of the Colossus / colossal-creature rules
- **EN World "Steal This Rule: Fighting Colossal Creatures" (2013):** three abstract zones (Melee, Close, Far). The creature has **infinite HP** and a set number of **wounds** (9–12). Players must **exploit a weakness to open its defenses before wounds land**, and weaknesses reset after use. **Reaction triggers** fire when the creature is wounded, when defenses reset, and at wound thresholds.
- **Kobold Stew "Inspirations III":** make colossi immune to normal damage. You must climb (Athletics or Acrobatics) and keep your grip (Con saves) to reach sigil weak points, discover weaknesses through environment and behavior, and treat the fight as a puzzle.
- **To Kill a Kaiju** (2025 jam one-pager): a climbing/damage table ties **your height on the kaiju** to damage dealt and taken, so the neck and shoulders are optimal.
- Sources: https://www.enworld.org/threads/steal-this-rule-fighting-colossal-creatures.661815/ · https://mrpuddins.wixsite.com/koboldstew/post/inspirations-iii-shadow-of-the-colossus-designing-truly-epic-monster-confrontations · https://cmdrapollo.itch.io/to-kill-a-kaiju

### C6. Monster as natural disaster
- **Kaiju Incorporated (Evil Hat, Fate):** players aren't fighting kaiju. They are companies profiting from cleanup, seen from a ground-level view. The GM uses a "Kaiju attack checklist" to plot sessions. This is a pure hazard and aftermath framing.
- **Official AoT TTRPG** also describes titans as "forces of nature" (A1).
- **Alien RPG** treats the xenomorph as an unstoppable hazard with signature tables (B8).
- Sources: https://evilhat.com/product/kaiju-incorporated-the-rpg/ · http://oneyardhex.blogspot.com/2017/03/review-kaiju-incorporated-rpg.html

### C7. Mothership (Tuesday Knight Games)
- **Stress** starts at 2 and caps at 20, rising from failed saves and horror. A **Panic Check** rolls d20: above your Stress you keep calm, otherwise you roll on the Panic table. Panic is inevitable over time.
- Sources: https://www.thealexandrian.net/creations/mothership/mothership-cheat-sheet-v1.pdf · https://skaldforge.wordpress.com/2022/02/14/changes-to-stress-and-panic-in-mothership/

### C8. Symbaroum (Free League)
- **Corruption** is temporary or permanent. If total corruption exceeds your threshold (Resolute), your PC becomes an **Abomination** and is lost to the player. Reviewers call it scarier than death. This could model titan-shifter or serum risk.
- Sources: https://writeups.letsyouandhimfight.com/wrestlepig/symbaroum/ · https://illmetbymorrslieb.wordpress.com/2026/02/11/review-symbaroum-core-rulebook/

### C9. Fabula Ultima (Need Games!, publisher of the official AoT RPG)
- **Multi-part boss** advice: a main body (elite or champion) plus **limbs as separate combatants**. **Multi-phase villains** transform, and the first phase is advised to be a warm-up. This design culture may inform the official AoT game **[speculation]**.
- Sources: http://fabulaultima.wikidot.com/villains-and-bosses · https://www.belloflostsouls.net/2026/09/rpg-fabula-ultimas-bestiary-adds-more-than-40-boss-level-monsters-to-the-game.html

## E. Most transferable mechanics (for design)

1. **Gas and blades as consumables.** Use SRD Supply rolls (licensed), or a Forbidden Lands-style step-down die written in our own words. Roll gas after each maneuver burst and blades after each strike. A 1 steps the die down, which is a lot less bookkeeping than exact counts. Titan World's binary tags were too coarse.
2. **Thrust dice modeled on ammo dice.** Add d6s to a maneuver roll. Extra 6s buy position, speed, or a second anchor, and the pip sum drains gas. Multiple 1s mean a jammed or snapped anchor. This gives the "burn gas for a big move" choice.
3. **Titan behavior tables.** Draw on Forbidden Lands, Dragonbane, and Alien signature attacks. Give each titan class a d6 behavior table with auto-hit grabs and bites that PCs must dodge using their action. Abnormals roll twice or hidden. Take telegraphing from Monster Hunter World or The Last Stand's face-up card.
4. **Titan hit zones with break effects.** Combine the T2K hit-location die, Monster Hunter part-breaking, and Lancer structure thresholds. Zones: ankles and tendons (titan falls, nape lowers), eyes (blinded), arms and hands (can't grab), nape (only killing zone). Broken zones regenerate on a countdown.
5. **Setup then execution.** Combine Titan World Advantage, the EN World "weakness before wounds" rule, Blades flashbacks, and SRD help and initiative exchange. A nape strike needs Openings created by teammates. Initiative exchange lets the setup PC hand the killer an immediate turn, and flashback-style prep resolves Survey Corps planning.
6. **Titan fear.** Use SRD stress dice and the panic table (Alien), with Mothership's inevitability. Fold T2K suppression into it: being near a titan attack forces a Coolness-style roll or you freeze. Vaesen or Flood-style conditions and Scars cover survivors.
7. **Expeditions outside the Walls.** SRD travel tasks cover scouting formation roles (keep watch, lead). Add a Heart-style route Resistance, MYZ-style Rot as exposure or attrition, and Heart Connections or Forbidden Lands stronghold functions as forward supply depots.
8. **HQ and promotion.** Vaesen HQ Development Points (each upgrade spawns a threat) and MYZ Ark projects work for a Survey Corps HQ funded by the Crown or Military Police. Blade Runner's Promotion vs. Humanity maps to Commendation (orders, results) vs. Conscience (saving comrades, disobeying).

## F. Common criticisms of existing AoT attempts
- **Mobility bottleneck:** movement scarcity creates dead turns (deck-builder). ODM must feel fast, not rationed into paralysis.
- **Helplessness that frustrates rather than thrills:** overpowered early titans and "fun until you die" (deck-builder). Titan World's XCOM-level wipes too. Lethality needs player agency and replacement pipelines.
- **Titan side is passive:** the Titan player is "a facilitator" (The Last Stand). GM-side titan behavior should be interesting to run.
- **Balance exploits** that nullify the titan (Last Stand tactic cards and hero powers).
- **Scale is hard to model** (Unauthorized and Unofficial creator).
- **Too abstract or no-frills:** binary gas and blade tags, no art (Titan World). Too long or not epic enough: two-plus hours without big moments (deck-builder).
- **Licensing friction:** Kodansha approval loops slowed the official game.
- Caveat: formal reviews of AoT *RPGs* are scarce. Most detailed criticism comes from the board game reviews.
