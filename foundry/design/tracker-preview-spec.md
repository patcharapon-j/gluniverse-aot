# Engagement tracker previews (milestone 4)

Owner decisions: the tracker is BOTH (a) a dedicated always-visible HUD strip along the TOP of the screen (NOT in the Foundry sidebar: the GM keeps the sidebar on the Chat tab), compact: round step, the card order with the current card, Titan cards, Wings, swap cue, Retreat clock, and a button to open the board; it must coexist with Foundry's top-left scene navigation and not cover the canvas centre; collapsible to a thin bar; AND (c) a separate "Engagement board" window (full detail). Positions show in both the board (soldier x Focus Titan grid) and on the canvas (badges/rings around Titan tokens). Three previews, same content, same locked Personnel File style (foundry/design/preview-v2-1-personnel-file.html: tokens, fonts Playfair Display / Special Elite / Noto Serif, paper and leather, file tabs, section rules, stamps, pips and boxes, 10px floor, 4px spacing, anime.js MOTION tokens, reduced motion). They differ in LAYOUT and interaction model, not in visual style. Flat HTML/CSS; no three.js.

## Rules (read the data, do not invent)
data/engagement/round.yaml (steps wings -> deal -> swap -> play -> end; end steps Gas Rolls -> Regeneration +1 -> Background Titan clocks -> Retreat clock; gm_tracker checklist), positions.yaml, anchor-ratings.yaml, attention.yaml, background-titans.yaml, engagement-flow.yaml, engagement-setup.yaml, grab.yaml, squad-tactics.yaml, titan-harm.yaml, size-classes.yaml, data/titans/*.yaml, data/skirmish/skirmish.yaml, data/core/dice-pool.yaml, CONTEXT.md glossary. Initiative: 20 face-up cards numbered 1-20, lowest first, never tied; one card per PC and per un-Winged Squadmate; each Focus Titan gets cards equal to its Tempo; Down/Grabbed soldiers still get cards; a Wing Squadmate acts right after its PC. Turn = one move + one action.

## Content (identical across previews; plainly example data)
Squad of 4 PCs (Petra Hollis Slayer, and three others with different Specialties) + 2 Squadmates (one Winged to Petra, one not). Two Focus Titans: a Medium (Tempo 1) and a Sprinting Abnormal (read its Tempo from data), one Background Titan with its clock. Anchor Rating: Wooded. Round 2, play step in progress: cards dealt, one swap already made (show it), current card highlighted, Titan cards interleaved, Petra's Wing acting after her. Position grid: each soldier's Position toward each Focus Titan (Distant / In Reach / On Body / Blind Spot), with one soldier Grabbed and one Down. Per Titan: Attention ladder and holder, Body Part States summary, Openings, Regeneration clock (round segmented), Next Behavior sealed. Round-end panel: Gas Rolls due (who), Regeneration +1, Background Titan clock, Retreat clock, as a checklist the GM steps through. Retreat clock visible. A toggle or second view showing the same panel in a Skirmish (Foe groups get cards, no Wings, no swap, Engaged/Apart pairs instead of Positions).
Show three surfaces on the page: (1) the top HUD strip over a mock Foundry screen (canvas area with the sidebar open on the Chat tab on the right), at 1600px wide, plus its collapsed state, (2) the Engagement board window (~1000x700), (3) a small mock canvas area with two Titan tokens and soldier tokens showing the Position badges/rings.
Interactions that must work: advance to the next card; perform a swap in the swap step; change a soldier's Position in the grid (canvas badge updates); step through the round-end checklist (clock segments fill); toggle Skirmish view.

## Page contract (Artifact)
No doctype/html/head/body; <title> then <style> first. Scripts only from cdnjs.cloudflare.com or cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/ pinned (anime.js v4). Google Fonts only. Images as data: URIs (icons from foundry/static/assets/icons, portraits/plates from foundry/static/assets, downscaled). Under 3 MB. No fetch, no downloads, no sound. No horizontal page scroll at 400px (surfaces may stack). Paint body background explicitly.
