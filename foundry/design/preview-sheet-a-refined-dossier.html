    What changed from the current sheet

    • Health, Stress, Resolve, gas and Blade Sets share one 74px band; the injury and response chips sit under the track they affect, and every part of it responds to a click.
    • The tab row and the footer are gone: index tabs stick out of the file's right edge, and the footer is tooled into the binder.
    • Each attribute heads its own quick rolls; click the head to roll the attribute alone. Descriptions moved to hover.
    • All 27 quick rolls and the Talent list fit above the fold; Bonus Dice and the Stress Dice every roll adds live on the one label line.
    • Talents are one line each: level pips, Dice or Rule, the use box; the rules text opens in place.
    • Kit rows are 30px with every control inline; Wounds & Mind drops the repeated tracks for a short readout beside the figure.
