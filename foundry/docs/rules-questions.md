# Rules questions from the Foundry system

Questions the Foundry build raised that the rules (data/, docs/rules/) do not settle. Each entry says what the system does until a rules session answers it.

| # | Question | What the system does now | Raised |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Resolve floor.** | The Foundry system clamps Resolve at 0; the rules set no floor (formula ceil((Instinct+Empathy)/2) + Scars - Grief can go negative; data/mind/grief.yaml says `resolve_floor: none`). Rules session to confirm. The sheet notes the formula's own value when it is below 0. | Owner decision, step 2b (2026-09-16) |
| 2 | **Wound Tight and Cover.** Wound Tight (hair-trigger) adds 1 Stress "each time you reach for more". Does a Covered Push still cost the pusher that extra Stress? | The pusher gains no Stress on a Covered Push (data/core/stress-changes.yaml, cover: "The Pushing soldier gains no Stress"), Wound Tight included; the Covering soldier gains 1. | Step 2d (2026-09-16) |
| 3 | **Which roll uses a next-roll penalty.** Cold Sweat gives "a next-roll penalty" that "lasts until used". Is it used by the next roll of any kind, or only by the next attribute roll? | Only an attribute roll counts it (it is a dice penalty) and clears it; a Fear, Stress Response, or Gas Roll leaves it in place. | Step 2d (2026-09-16) |
| 4 | **When a lasting Stress Response ends, as the sheet records it.** A response gained in a Titan Engagement ends with it; outside, "when the rule that called for the roll states", else a day. | The card records the end as the Titan Engagement's end while a Foundry combat is running, and as a day otherwise; the sheet lets the table change it. | Step 2d (2026-09-16) |
| 5 | **The Death Roll's Bonus Dice.** data/core/dice-pool.yaml (roll_exceptions, death-roll) excludes only Help among Bonus Dice sources, while data/harm/death-rolls.yaml says no listed source applies. Can a Death Roll take any Bonus Dice? | The roll dialog offers no Bonus Dice and no Circumstances on a Death Roll (death-rolls.yaml). The two files could say the same thing. | Step 2d (2026-09-16) |
| 6 | **The Nape strike ODM check near a grounded Titan.** The ODM requirement is lifted when "the Titan is grounded". | The quick-roll row blocks a Nape strike while ODM Gear is missing, Jammed, or dry, unless a Titan token on the viewed scene is grounded; it cannot tell which Titan is struck until the tracker (milestone 4). | Step 2d (2026-09-16) |
| 7 | **A named comrade with nobody to name.** A Drive that needs a named comrade names one at join-the-squad (data/character/lifepath.yaml). enlistment.yaml, drive_rules.named_comrade, says a player records another Drive "if no other living soldier is in the Squad when such a Drive would be recorded", but at creation the Drive is recorded at step 2, before the Squad forms. Must a player whose Squad has no other soldier at join-the-squad (a lone player character, no starting Squadmates) swap the Drive then? | The Lifepath wizard lets the player pick any Drive at step 2. At join-the-squad it lists the other soldiers and Squadmates in the world; with none, it says the Drive has no comrade to name and leaves the field empty for the table. | Milestone 3 (2026-09-16) |
| 8 | **Conditional dice Talents in the Graduation Exam.** requirements_ignored (graduation-exam.yaml) lifts entry requirements that need a Titan, a Position, or a horse-free context, but a dice Talent's condition ("against a grounded Titan", "while mounted", "in the care window held when a day passes on an Expedition") is not an entry requirement. Can such a Talent add its dice to a Trial roll? | No: the wizard never counts a conditional dice Talent in a Trial, since the Exam has no Titan, no horse, and no Expedition. Unconditional dice Talents and the rule Talents that name the entry (Hunter's Eye, Sure Hands) apply. | Milestone 3 (2026-09-16) |
| 9 | **Declined Blade Sets and the handles.** standard-issue.yaml lets a soldier decline any Blade Set they would receive, and fits one into empty handles. If a soldier declines some of the Blade Sets, is the one fitted into the handles among those kept? | The wizard counts declines against the carried sets first: the first Blade Set received always goes into empty handles unless every Blade Set is declined. | Milestone 3 (2026-09-16) |
| 10 | **A built soldier's story events.** built_steps (training-years) lets the player name one event of each Training Year for the story, but record_on_sheet has no field for it. Where is it kept? | The wizard writes the named events into the soldier's notes, under a "Training Years" line. | Milestone 3 (2026-09-16) |
| 11 | **More than twenty card holders.** data/engagement/round.yaml deals from twenty cards, one per living player character and un-Winged Squadmate and Tempo cards per Focus Titan. A large Squad with two Tempo 2 Titans can need more than twenty. What happens then? | The tracker refuses to deal more than twenty cards and says why; the table puts more Squadmates on Wings. | Milestone 4 (2026-09-17) |
| 12 | **Which Focus Titan a swap compares against.** The swap needs the two soldiers to hold the same Position or be one step apart, but positions.yaml (comparison) lists the rules it covers and the swap is not among them. Does comparison.otherwise (the living Focus Titan with the earliest label, else the latest corpse) apply? And may two soldiers who hold Positions only relative to a corpse swap? | The tracker compares by comparison.otherwise, and so by the corpse when no Focus Titan is alive. | Milestone 4 (2026-09-17) |
| 13 | **How long the foe rule's "last attacker" lasts.** data/skirmish/foes.yaml (foe_rule, last_attacker) names the candidate "who most recently made a Fight or Shoot roll against this Foe". Is that any time in the Skirmish, or only this round? | Any time in the Skirmish: the tracker keeps each Foe's last attacker until the Skirmish ends or that soldier is no longer a candidate. | Milestone 4 (2026-09-17) |
| 14 | **A dodge cancelling a Titan's later card after a Push.** A dodge's successes cancel against every later card of the same Titan that round. If the soldier Pushes that dodge after a later card has resolved, does the later card's result change? | The tracker copies the dodge's successes onto each later attack card of that Titan when the card is rolled; a later Push updates only the attack card it answered, and the table fixes an earlier-resolved card by hand. | Milestone 4 (2026-09-17) |
| 15 | **The reference Titan of a fall at a Titan's death.** A soldier the dying Titan held Grabbed and lifted is freed and falls first (titan-harm.yaml, titan_death, freed), holding On Body relative to the dying body. falls.yaml (height) finds the reference Titan among Focus Titans; is the dying body still one at that moment, so its Size Class can raise the band? | The tracker reads the dying body as the reference Titan: On Body gives high, raised to extreme for a Large Titan or in the Giant Forest. | Milestone 4, v1 (2026-09-17) |
| 16 | **Which steps the retreat's "shortest chain" counts.** Option 1 moves "one step along a shortest chain of steps their move can make" toward distant, while options 3 and 4 count "the fewest step rows of the Anchor Rating". Does option 1's chain use only the rows the soldier's kinds of move can make (on foot or mounted, and ODM Gear when had), or every row? | Option 1 counts only the rows the soldier's move can make now; options 3 and 4 count every row of the Anchor Rating (with the grounded and corpse rows). | Milestone 4, v1 (2026-09-17) |
| 17 | **A death marked by hand during a Titan Engagement.** A Death Roll's death, or one the GM records on the sheet, is not a Titan's card or Grab, so the event's Titan is the nearest Focus Titan (fear-rolls.yaml, event_titan). Is every such death a comrade-dies trigger for the witnesses, whenever in the round it is marked? | The tracker rolls the witnesses' Fear Rolls as soon as the dead status is set on a soldier taking part, except during the engagement-end steps (limits, after_engagement_end). | Milestone 4, v1 (2026-09-17) |
